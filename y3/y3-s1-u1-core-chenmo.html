1 回huí家jiā路lù上shàng，他tā偶ǒu然rán发fā现xiàn，商shāng店diàn橱chú窗chuāng里lǐ的de模mó特tè儿ér换huàn上shàng了le白bái衣yī素sù裙qún。看kàn着zhe那nà套tào服fú装zhuāng，他tā脑nǎo海hǎi中zhōng又yòu闪shǎn现xiàn出chū那nà个ge沉chén默mò的de女nǚ生shēng，思sī绪xù一yí下xià子zi就jiù回huí到dào了le中zhōng学xué时shí的de那nà段duàn岁suì月yuè。
1On his way home, he happened to notice that the mannequin in the shop window had been dressed in a plain white blouse and skirt. Looking at the outfit, the image of that quiet girl flashed through his mind again — and in an instant his thoughts were back in those schooldays years ago.
2 那nà年nián，他tā念niàn中zhōng三sān。隔gé着zhe狭xiá窄zhǎi的de过guò道dào，同tóng排pái坐zuò着zhe一yí个gè女nǚ生shēng，她tā的de名míng字zì非fēi常cháng特tè别bié，叫jiào冷lěng月yuè。冷lěng月yuè不bù轻qīng易yì同tóng人rén交jiāo往wǎng，总zǒng是shì穿chuān着zhe白bái衣yī素sù裙qún，下xià巴bā抬tái得dé高gāo高gāo的de，有yǒu点diǎn儿ér拒jù人rén千qiān里lǐ。有yǒu一yī次cì他tā背bèi书shū包bāo时shí，不bù小xiǎo心xīn把bǎ冷lěng月yuè漂piào亮liàng的de铅qiān笔bǐ盒hé打dǎ落luò在zài地dì。她tā拧níng起qǐ眉méi毛máo望wàng着zhe不bù知zhī所suǒ措cuò的de他tā，但dàn始shǐ终zhōng抿mǐn着zhe嘴zuǐ没méi说shuō一yī句jù不bù中zhōng听tīng的de话huà。
2That year, he was in Secondary 3. Across the narrow aisle, in the same row, sat a girl with an unusual name — Leng Yue. She didn't mingle easily; she was always in a plain white blouse and skirt, chin held high, keeping everyone at arm's length. One time, when he was slinging his backpack on, he accidentally knocked her pretty pencil case to the floor. She knitted her brows and looked at him standing there flustered — but kept her lips pressed shut and said nothing unkind.
3 他tā对duì她tā的de沉chén默mò心xīn存cún感gǎn激jī。
3He was grateful for her silence.
4 不bù久jiǔ，冷lěng月yuè住zhù院yuàn了le，据jù说shuō患huàn了le肺fèi炎yán。他tā看kàn着zhe过guò道dào那nà边biān空kōng座zuò位wèi上shàng的de纸zhǐ屑xiè，便biàn悄qiāo悄qiāo地dì捡jiǎn去qù扔rēng了le。
4Soon after, Leng Yue was admitted to hospital — pneumonia, people said. Seeing scraps of paper on her empty seat across the aisle, he quietly picked them up and threw them away.
5 男nán生shēng的de父fù亲qīn是shì肿zhǒng瘤liú医yī院yuàn的de主zhǔ治zhì医yī生shēng。有yǒu一yì天tiān，他tā无wú意yì中zhōng听tīng到dào父fù亲qīn在zài与yǔ同tóng事shì打dǎ电diàn话huà时shí提tí到dào冷lěng月yuè这zhè个ge名míng字zì。父fù亲qīn说shuō这zhè个ge女nǚ孩hái得dé了le癌ái症zhèng，即jí使shǐ做zuò手shǒu术shù，也yě徒tú劳láo无wú功gōng，只zhǐ能néng等děng待dài那nà最zuì可kě怕pà的de结jié局jú。
5The boy's father was a senior doctor at the cancer hospital. One day, he overheard his father mention the name Leng Yue while on a call with a colleague. His father said the girl had cancer — that even surgery would be futile, and they could only wait for the worst.
6 之zhī后hòu，他tā每měi天tiān都dōu把bǎ冷lěng月yuè的de空kōng座zuò位wèi擦cā拭shì一yī遍biàn，但dàn对duì冷lěng月yuè的de病bìng情qíng，他tā始shǐ终zhōng保bǎo持chí沉chén默mò，没méi对duì任rèn何hé人rén吐tǔ露lù过guò。
6After that, he wiped down Leng Yue's empty seat every day. But about her illness, he stayed silent — he never breathed a word to anyone.
7 三sān个gè月yuè后hòu的de某mǒu一yì天tiān，冷lěng月yuè来lái上shàng学xué了le，还hái是shì那nà身shēn白bái衣yī素sù裙qún，只zhǐ是shì脸liǎn色sè苍cāng白bái了le许xǔ多duō。班bān里lǐ没méi有yǒu人rén知zhī道dào真zhēn相xiàng，大dà家jiā只zhǐ知zhī道dào她tā经jīng常cháng请qǐng病bìng假jià，有yǒu些xiē调tiáo皮pí的de男nán生shēng因yīn此cǐ在zài背bèi地dì里lǐ都dōu叫jiào她tā“林lín妹mèi妹mèi”。其qí实shí，连lián冷lěng月yuè本běn人rén也yě不bù知zhī道dào真zhēn相xiàng，因yīn为wèi诊zhěn断duàn书shū上shàng仅jǐn仅jǐn写xiě着zhe肺fèi炎yán。她tā患huàn的de是shì不bù治zhì之zhī症zhèng，而ér她tā又yòu是shì个gè忧yōu郁yù脆cuì弱ruò的de女nǚ孩hái，她tā的de父fù母mǔ把bǎ她tā送sòng回huí学xué校xiào，是shì为wèi了le让ràng她tā安ān然rán度dù过guò最zuì后hòu的de日rì子zi。
7One day, three months later, Leng Yue came back to school — still in the same plain white blouse and skirt, only her face much paler. No one in the class knew the truth; they only knew she was often away on sick leave. Some of the more mischievous boys started calling her "Lin Daiyu" behind her back. In fact, even Leng Yue herself didn't know the truth — her diagnosis slip only said "pneumonia." What she actually had was incurable, and she was a melancholic, fragile girl. Her parents had sent her back to school so she could live out her last days in peace.
8 男nán生shēng变biàn了le。他tā常cháng常cháng主zhǔ动dòng与yǔ冷lěng月yuè说shuō话huà，时shí不bù时shí为wèi她tā倒dào来lái热rè水shuǐ；在zài她tā偶ǒu尔ěr哼hēng一yī支zhī歌gē时shí为wèi她tā热rè烈liè鼓gǔ掌zhǎng；还hái有yǒu一yī次cì，听tīng说shuō她tā生shēng日rì，他tā买mǎi来lái贺hè卡kǎ动dòng员yuán全quán班bān同tóng学xué在zài卡kǎ上shàng签qiān名míng。
8The boy changed. He often went out of his way to talk to Leng Yue, pouring her hot water now and then; applauding enthusiastically whenever she hummed a tune; and once, when he heard it was her birthday, he bought a card and got the whole class to sign it.
9 大dà家jiā议yì论lùn纷fēn纷fēn，相xiāng互hù挤jǐ眉méi弄nòng眼yǎn。有yǒu人rén说shuō他tā是shì冷lěng月yuè忠zhōng实shí的de骑qí士shì，冷lěng月yuè听tīng说shuō后hòu有yǒu意yì躲duǒ着zhe他tā。可kě是shì他tā一yì如rú既jì往wǎng地dì照zhào顾gù冷lěng月yuè，并bìng且qiě始shǐ终zhōng保bǎo持chí沉chén默mò。因yīn为wèi他tā知zhī道dào，如rú果guǒ那nà消xiāo息xī传chuán到dào冷lěng月yuè耳ěr里lǐ，准zhǔn是shì杀shā伤shāng力lì很hěn大dà的de一yī把bǎ利lì刃rèn。
9The class gossiped, exchanged knowing looks. Some said he was Leng Yue's devoted knight. When she heard that, she began to avoid him. But he went on looking out for her just as before — and he stayed silent through it all. Because he knew that if the truth ever reached her ears, it would cut like a dagger.
10 这zhè期qī间jiān，冷lěng月yuè高gāo烧shāo过guò几jǐ次cì，忽hū而ér住zhù院yuàn，忽hū而ér来lái学xué校xiào，但dàn她tā的de座zuò位wèi始shǐ终zhōng被bèi擦cā拭shì得dé一yī尘chén不bù染rǎn。大dà家jiā也yě渐jiàn渐jiàn习xí惯guàn了le他tā对duì冷lěng月yuè异yì乎hū寻xún常cháng的de关guān切qiè。
10During that time, Leng Yue ran high fevers more than once — going in and out of hospital and school. But her seat was always kept spotless. The class gradually grew used to his unusual attentiveness towards her.
11 直zhí到dào有yǒu一yì天tiān，奇qí迹jì发fā生shēng了le。冷lěng月yuè痊quán愈yù了le，体tǐ内nèi再zài也yě找zhǎo不bú到dào癌ái细xì胞bāo。医yī生shēng说shuō这zhè种zhǒng情qíng况kuàng发fā生shēng的de概gài率lǜ也yě许xǔ是shì十shí万wàn分fēn之zhī一yī，简jiǎn直zhí就jiù是shì奇qí迹jì。这zhè时shí，冷lěng月yuè才cái知zhī道dào发fā生shēng的de一yī切qiè，才cái知zhī道dào邻lín桌zhuō的de他tā竟jìng是shì她tā主zhǔ治zhì医yī生shēng的de儿ér子zi。
11Then one day, a miracle happened. Leng Yue made a full recovery — no cancer cells could be found in her body anymore. The doctors said the odds of this were perhaps one in a hundred thousand; it was nothing short of a miracle. Only then did Leng Yue learn everything — including that the boy who sat next to her was, of all people, her doctor's son.
12 冷lěng月yuè给gěi他tā写xiě了le一yī张zhāng纸zhǐ条tiáo，上shàng面miàn只zhǐ有yǒu六liù个gè字zì：谢xiè谢xiè你nǐ的de沉chén默mò。男nán生shēng没méi有yǒu说shuō什shén么me，他tā看kàn着zhe纸zhǐ条tiáo上shàng的de字zì，想xiǎng起qǐ了le以yǐ前qián她tā对duì那nà件jiàn小xiǎo事shì的de沉chén默mò……
12Leng Yue wrote him a short note — just six characters: "Thank you for your silence." The boy said nothing. Looking at the words on the paper, he thought back to her silence over that small incident all those years ago…
13 多duō年nián过guò去qù，男nán生shēng对duì那nà段duàn往wǎng事shì的de记jì忆yì依yī然rán十shí分fēn清qīng晰xī。他tā望wàng着zhe橱chú窗chuāng里lǐ的de白bái衣yī素sù裙qún，默mò默mò在zài心xīn里lǐ祝zhù福fú：希xī望wàng你nǐ一yī切qiè安ān好hǎo，一yī生shēng健jiàn康kāng。
13Years have passed, but the boy remembers it all with perfect clarity. Looking at the plain white blouse and skirt in the shop window, he offers a silent wish: I hope you are well — I hope you stay healthy, always.
