1 人rén与yǔ人rén之zhī间jiān存cún在zài着zhe各gè种zhǒng各gè样yàng的de社shè会huì关guān系xì。其qí中zhōng，每měi个gè人rén都dū会huì面miàn对duì的de就jiù是shì邻lín里lǐ关guān系xì，建jiàn立lì良liáng好hǎo的de邻lín里lǐ关guān系xì非fēi常cháng重zhòng要yào。
1There are all sorts of social relationships between people. Among them, one everybody has to navigate is the relationship with neighbours — and building good neighbourly ties matters a great deal.
2 邻lín里lǐ之zhī间jiān，唇chún齿chǐ相xiāng依yī，不bú是shì亲qīn人rén，胜shèng似sì亲qīn人rén。俗sú话huà说shuō：“远yuǎn亲qīn不bù如rú近jìn邻lín。”“远yuǎn亲qīn”虽suī是shì亲qīn人rén，但dàn因yīn彼bǐ此cǐ距jù离lí遥yáo远yuǎn，不bù可kě能néng经jīng常cháng见jiàn面miàn。相xiāng反fǎn的de，我wǒ们men与yǔ邻lín居jū却què每měi天tiān抬tái头tóu不bú见jiàn低dī头tóu见jiàn，关guān系xì往wǎng往wǎng比bǐ“远yuǎn亲qīn”更gèng亲qīn近jìn。在zài日rì常cháng生shēng活huó中zhōng，当dāng你nǐ遇yù到dào意yì想xiǎng不bù到dào的de紧jǐn急jí情qíng况kuàng需xū要yào帮bāng助zhù的de时shí候hòu，远yuǎn方fāng的de亲qīn戚qī无wú法fǎ及jí时shí赶gǎn到dào。这zhè时shí，可kě以yǐ马mǎ上shàng伸shēn出chū援yuán手shǒu的de常cháng常cháng是shì住zhù家jiā附fù近jìn的de邻lín居jū。
2Neighbours are as closely linked as lips and teeth — not family, and yet more than family. As the saying goes, "A nearby neighbour beats a distant relative." A "distant relative" may be kin by blood, but separated by geography, you can't see each other often. A neighbour, on the other hand, you run into every single day — and the bond is often closer than with far-off relatives. In everyday life, when an unexpected emergency hits and you need help, distant family can't rush to your side in time. The person who can reach out with a helping hand immediately is usually the one living just next door.
3 几jǐ年nián前qián，裕yù廊láng东dōng一yí个gè组zǔ屋wū单dān位wèi发fā生shēng火huǒ患huàn，失shī火huǒ的de单dān位wèi住zhù着zhe林lín女nǚ士shì一yì家jiā母mǔ女nǚ三sān人rén。虽suī然rán她tā们men侥jiǎo幸xìng逃táo生shēng，但dàn无wú情qíng的de大dà火huǒ把bǎ家jiā中zhōng的de一yī切qiè全quán部bù烧shāo光guāng了le。在zài她tā们men最zuì艰jiān难nán的de时shí候hòu，邻lín居jū们men不bù约yuē而ér同tóng地dì伸shēn出chū援yuán手shǒu。有yǒu的de腾téng出chū房fáng间jiān让ràng她tā们men暂zàn住zhù，有yǒu的de为wèi她tā们men提tí供gōng一yī日rì三sān餐cān，有yǒu的de则zé帮bāng忙máng处chǔ理lǐ搬bān家jiā的de事shì情qíng。不bù仅jǐn如rú此cǐ，邻lín居jū们men还hái纷fēn纷fēn慷kāng慨kǎi解jiě囊náng，捐juān款kuǎn捐juān物wù，帮bāng她tā们men渡dù过guò难nán关guān。这zhè些xiē善shàn良liáng无wú私sī的de行xíng为wéi汇huì成chéng了le一yī股gǔ股gǔ暖nuǎn流liú，温wēn暖nuǎn了le林lín家jiā母mǔ女nǚ的de心xīn。所suǒ谓wèi“一yī排pái篱lí笆bā十shí根gēn桩zhuāng，一yì家jiā有yǒu难nán大dà家jiā帮bāng”，在zài危wēi急jí时shí刻kè，更gèng能néng看kàn出chū邻lín里lǐ关guān系xì的de重zhòng要yào。
3A few years back, a fire broke out in an HDB flat in Jurong East. The flat was home to Mrs Lin and her two daughters. Though all three narrowly escaped, the merciless fire burnt everything they had to the ground. In their hardest moment, the neighbours reached out without needing to be asked. Some gave up rooms so the family could stay temporarily; others provided three meals a day; others helped handle the move. More than that, neighbours gave generously — donating money and goods to help the family through the crisis. These quiet, selfless acts flowed together like streams of warmth, melting the hearts of Mrs Lin and her daughters. As the saying goes, "A fence stands on ten posts; when one family is in trouble, the others all help." It's in times of crisis that the importance of good neighbours shines clearest.
4 邻lín里lǐ关guān系xì的de重zhòng要yào不bù只zhǐ体tǐ现xiàn在zài危wēi急jí的de关guān头tóu，在zài日rì常cháng生shēng活huó的de点diǎn点diǎn滴dī滴dī中zhōng也yě能néng看kàn出chū来lái。住zhù在zài淡dàn滨bīn尼ní的de马mǎ来lái同tóng胞bāo阿ā都dōu拉lā与yǔ邻lín居jū的de关guān系xì一yí向xiàng融róng洽qià。农nóng历lì新xīn年nián时shí，他tā主zhǔ动dòng在zài居jū住zhù楼lóu层céng的de走zǒu廊láng布bù置zhì新xīn年nián装zhuāng饰shì，为wèi大dà家jiā营yíng造zào喜xǐ庆qìng的de佳jiā节jié氛fēn围wéi。当dāng被bèi问wèn及jí为wèi何hé这zhè么me做zuò时shí，阿ā都dōu拉lā说shuō，当dāng年nián父fù亲qīn去qù世shì的de时shí候hòu，他tā心xīn力lì交jiāo瘁cuì，没méi有yǒu时shí间jiān顾gù及jí家jiā里lǐ的de大dà小xiǎo事shì。幸xìng好hǎo左zuǒ邻lín右yòu舍shè主zhǔ动dòng慰wèi问wèn，帮bāng他tā照zhào料liào盆pén栽zāi，打dǎ扫sǎo走zǒu廊láng，疏shū解jiě了le他tā身shēn心xīn上shàng所suǒ承chéng受shòu的de巨jù大dà压yā力lì。因yīn此cǐ，他tā想xiǎng借jiè农nóng历lì新xīn年nián的de装zhuāng饰shì表biǎo达dá感gǎn激jī之zhī情qíng，也yě希xī望wàng借jiè此cǐ拉lā近jìn各gè族zú邻lín居jū之zhī间jiān的de关guān系xì。
4The importance of neighbourly ties shows not only in crises but also in the small details of daily life. Abdullah, a Malay neighbour living in Tampines, has always been on good terms with those around him. During Lunar New Year, he takes the initiative to decorate the corridor of his floor, creating a festive atmosphere for everyone. Asked why he does this, Abdullah explains: years ago, when his father passed away, he was mentally and physically drained, with no time to attend to the household. Fortunately, his neighbours on both sides came to offer condolences — they watered his plants, swept the corridor, and eased the enormous strain he was under. That is why he wants to use Lunar New Year decorations to express his gratitude, and at the same time bring the different communities of neighbours closer together.
5 不bù管guǎn是shì遇yù到dào紧jǐn急jí情qíng况kuàng，还hái是shì帮bāng忙máng处chǔ理lǐ日rì常cháng小xiǎo事shì，都dōu能néng看kàn出chū邻lín里lǐ关guān系xì的de重zhòng要yào。亲qīn人rén虽suī亲qīn，也yě可kě能néng有yǒu照zhào顾gù不bú到dào的de时shí候hòu。如rú果guǒ邻lín居jū能néng够gòu雪xuě中zhōng送sòng炭tàn，则zé恰qià好hǎo可kě以yǐ弥mí补bǔ这zhè种zhǒng缺quē憾hàn。正zhèng如rú谚yàn语yǔ所suǒ说shuō：“没méi有yǒu木mù头tou，支zhī不bù起qǐ房fáng子zi；没méi有yǒu邻lín居jū，过guò不bù好hǎo日rì子zi。”与yǔ邻lín居jū和hé睦mù相xiāng处chǔ，可kě以yǐ让ràng我wǒ们men的de生shēng活huó更gèng加jiā愉yú快kuài，更gèng加jiā舒shū心xīn。
5Whether it's meeting an emergency or helping out with the little things, the importance of good neighbours is clear. Family are family, but they can't always be there to help. When neighbours offer timely help in a crisis — like sending charcoal on a snowy day — they fill precisely that gap. As the proverb says: "Without wood, you can't build a house; without neighbours, you can't live well." Getting on harmoniously with neighbours makes our lives happier and more at ease.
6 要yào让ràng邻lín里lǐ关guān系xì更gèng加jiā和hé睦mù，邻lín居jū之zhī间jiān应yīng该gāi多duō一yì些xiē关guān心xīn，少shǎo一yì些xiē冷lěng漠mò；多duō一yì些xiē包bāo容róng，少shǎo一yì些xiē挑tiāo剔tī；多duō一yì些xiē礼lǐ让ràng，少shǎo一yì些xiē计jì较jiào。如rú果guǒ能néng做zuò到dào这zhè几jǐ点diǎn，那nà么me家jiā家jiā户hù户hù都dōu可kě以yǐ拥yōng有yǒu“不bú是shì亲qīn人rén，胜shèng似sì亲qīn人rén”的de好hǎo邻lín居jū。
6To make neighbourly relations more harmonious, there should be more care and less indifference; more tolerance and less nitpicking; more give-and-take and less calculating. If we can manage these few things, then every household can have neighbours who are "not family, and yet more than family."
