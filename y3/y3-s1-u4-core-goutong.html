1 我wǒ们men每měi天tiān都dōu与yǔ很hěn多duō人rén沟gōu通tōng：和hé朋péng友yǒu闲xián聊liáo是shì一yī种zhǒng沟gōu通tōng，问wèn老lǎo师shī问wèn题tí是shì一yī种zhǒng沟gōu通tōng，在zài朋péng友yǒu的de贴tiē文wén上shàng点diǎn赞zàn和hé留liú言yán也yě是shì一yī种zhǒng沟gōu通tōng。英yīng国guó诗shī人rén约yuē翰hàn·多duō恩ēn说shuō过guò：“没méi有yǒu人rén是shì一yī座zuò孤gū岛dǎo。”这zhè句jù话huà告gào诉sù我wǒ们men，每měi个gè人rén都dōu需xū要yào与yǔ别bié人rén进jìn行xíng交jiāo流liú。可kě以yǐ说shuō，我wǒ们men的de生shēng活huó离lí不bù开kāi沟gōu通tōng。
1Every day we communicate with many people: chatting casually with friends is a form of communication, asking a teacher a question is communication, and liking or leaving a comment on a friend's post is communication too. The English poet John Donne once said, "No man is an island." His words remind us that everyone needs to connect with others. You could say that our lives cannot do without communication.
2 从cóng个gè人rén方fāng面miàn来lái说shuō，沟gōu通tōng是shì维wéi持chí良liáng好hǎo关guān系xì的de润rùn滑huá剂jì，它tā可kě以yǐ促cù进jìn朋péng友yǒu之zhī间jiān的de交jiāo流liú，加jiā深shēn彼bǐ此cǐ的de情qíng谊yì。如rú果guǒ缺quē少shǎo沟gōu通tōng，日rì子zi久jiǔ了le，再zài深shēn厚hòu的de感gǎn情qíng也yě会huì逐zhú渐jiàn转zhuǎn淡dàn。很hěn多duō人rén在zài小xiǎo学xué时shí都dōu有yǒu亲qīn密mì的de朋péng友yǒu，可kě是shì升shēng上shàng不bù同tóng的de中zhōng学xué后hòu，沟gōu通tōng的de机jī会huì少shǎo了le，友yǒu情qíng也yě越yuè来lái越yuè难nán以yǐ维wéi持chí，一yì些xiē好hǎo朋péng友yǒu最zuì后hòu甚shèn至zhì完wán全quán失shī去qù联lián络luò。这zhè样yàng的de例lì子zi不bù胜shèng枚méi举jǔ。每měi当dāng看kàn到dào这zhè种zhǒng情qíng况kuàng，难nán免miǎn令lìng人rén扼è腕wàn叹tàn息xī。由yóu此cǐ可kě见jiàn，沟gōu通tōng在zài维wéi系xì友yǒu情qíng方fāng面miàn具jù有yǒu非fēi常cháng重zhòng要yào的de作zuò用yòng。
2On a personal level, communication is the lubricant that keeps good relationships running smoothly — it deepens the bond between friends. Without communication, over time even the deepest connection gradually fades. Many people have close friends in primary school, but once they move on to different secondary schools, opportunities to communicate shrink, friendships become harder to sustain, and sometimes old friends lose contact altogether. Examples of this are too numerous to list. Every time we see it, we can't help but feel regret. This shows just how vital communication is to holding friendships together.
3 对duì家jiā庭tíng而ér言yán，沟gōu通tōng更gèng是shì必bì不bù可kě少shǎo。现xiàn今jīn社shè会huì生shēng活huó节jié奏zòu快kuài，很hěn多duō人rén早zǎo出chū晚wǎn归guī，终zhōng日rì忙máng碌lù，很hěn少shǎo和hé家jiā人rén沟gōu通tōng，以yǐ致zhì与yǔ家jiā人rén的de关guān系xì渐jiàn渐jiàn疏shū远yuǎn。其qí实shí，即jí使shǐ亲qīn如rú家jiā人rén，也yě需xū要yào经jīng常cháng交jiāo流liú。这zhè样yàng一yī来lái，孩hái子zi会huì更gèng体tǐ恤xù父fù母mǔ的de辛xīn劳láo，父fù母mǔ会huì更gèng了liǎo解jiě孩hái子zi的de心xīn思sī，家jiā庭tíng氛fēn围wéi也yě会huì因yīn此cǐ变biàn得dé更gèng加jiā融róng洽qià。
3For families, communication is all the more essential. Life moves fast today — many people leave early and come home late, staying busy all day, and rarely talk with their families. This slowly pulls family members apart. In truth, even the closest family members need to communicate often. When they do, children become more empathetic to their parents' hard work, parents understand their children's thoughts better, and the atmosphere at home grows warmer.
4 沟gōu通tōng不bù只zhǐ对duì维wéi系xì友yǒu情qíng和hé促cù进jìn家jiā人rén的de关guān系xì有yǒu帮bāng助zhù，对duì团tuán体tǐ的de成chéng功gōng也yě起qǐ着zhe关guān键jiàn的de作zuò用yòng。任rèn何hé一yí个gè团tuán队duì，要yào达dá成chéng目mù标biāo，就jiù必bì须xū上shàng下xià一yī心xīn，团tuán结jié互hù信xìn。要yào做zuò到dào这zhè一yì点diǎn，就jiù离lí不bù开kāi适shì当dàng的de沟gōu通tōng。现xiàn代dài管guǎn理lǐ理lǐ论lùn之zhī父fù切qiè斯sī特tè·巴bā纳nà德dé曾céng经jīng这zhè样yàng说shuō：“管guǎn理lǐ者zhě最zuì基jī本běn的de职zhí责zé是shì建jiàn立lì与yǔ维wéi持chí一yí个gè畅chàng通tōng的de沟gōu通tōng管guǎn道dào。”在zài团tuán队duì中zhōng，领lǐng导dǎo者zhě应yīng该gāi懂dǒng得de以yǐ谦qiān虚xū的de态tài度dù倾qīng听tīng他tā人rén的de意yì见jiàn，懂dǒng得de通tōng过guò巧qiǎo妙miào的de提tí问wèn鼓gǔ励lì下xià属shǔ畅chàng所suǒ欲yù言yán。这zhè样yàng的de沟gōu通tōng是shì尊zūn重zhòng，也yě是shì鼓gǔ励lì，可kě以yǐ增zēng强qiáng团tuán体tǐ的de凝níng聚jù力lì。
4Communication doesn't just matter for friendships and family — it plays a critical role in the success of any group. For a team to reach its goals, everyone has to be of one mind, united and trusting. That cannot happen without proper communication. Chester Barnard, the father of modern management theory, once said: "The most basic responsibility of a manager is to establish and maintain a channel of open communication." Within a team, leaders should know how to listen humbly to others' views, and use thoughtful questions to encourage team members to speak their minds. This kind of communication is both a mark of respect and an encouragement — and it strengthens a team's cohesion.
5 从cóng更gèng广guǎng泛fàn的de层céng面miàn来lái看kàn，沟gōu通tōng对duì国guó家jiā的de稳wěn定dìng与yǔ发fā展zhǎn也yě大dà有yǒu裨bì益yì。这zhè一yì点diǎn从cóng我wǒ国guó的de历lì史shǐ发fā展zhǎn中zhōng就jiù可kě以yǐ找zhǎo到dào证zhèng明míng。上shàng个gè世shì纪jì60年nián代dài，各gè个gè族zú群qún聚jù居jū在zài不bù同tóng地dì区qū，彼bǐ此cǐ之zhī间jiān的de交jiāo流liú相xiāng对duì不bù足zú。那nà个ge时shí候hòu，新xīn加jiā坡pō面miàn临lín种zhǒng种zhǒng严yán峻jùn的de挑tiǎo战zhàn，社shè会huì上shàng出chū现xiàn了le大dà量liàng的de流liú言yán蜚fēi语yǔ，各gè种zhǒng扰rǎo乱luàn民mín心xīn的de论lùn调diào也yě甚shèn嚣xiāo尘chén上shàng，最zuì终zhōng导dǎo致zhì激jī烈liè的de种zhǒng族zú冲chōng突tū。这zhè些xiē事shì件jiàn让ràng国guó人rén意yì识shí到dào，种zhǒng族zú之zhī间jiān的de沟gōu通tōng、理lǐ解jiě和hé包bāo容róng是shì构gòu建jiàn和hé谐xié社shè会huì的de基jī础chǔ。在zài此cǐ之zhī后hòu，我wǒ国guó制zhì定dìng了le更gèng多duō促cù进jìn种zhǒng族zú融róng合hé的de政zhèng策cè。这zhè些xiē政zhèng策cè涵hán盖gài了le许xǔ多duō具jù体tǐ的de措cuò施shī，例lì如rú，确què保bǎo同tóng一yī个gè社shè区qū里lǐ居jū住zhù着zhe不bù同tóng种zhǒng族zú的de人rén，以yǐ增zēng进jìn彼bǐ此cǐ的de交jiāo流liú与yǔ了liǎo解jiě。在zài日rì常cháng的de相xiāng处chǔ和hé沟gōu通tōng中zhōng，各gè个gè种zhǒng族zú彼bǐ此cǐ包bāo容róng，国guó家jiā也yě因yīn此cǐ得dé以yǐ稳wěn定dìng发fā展zhǎn。
5On a broader level, communication also benefits a nation's stability and development. We can see this in Singapore's own history. In the 1960s, different ethnic communities lived separately in different areas, with relatively little contact between them. At that time, Singapore faced severe challenges; rumours and gossip were rife, and unsettling voices were loud in the public sphere — ultimately leading to fierce racial conflict. Those events made Singaporeans realise that communication, understanding, and tolerance between races are the foundation of a harmonious society. After that, Singapore introduced more policies to promote racial integration. These covered many concrete measures — for example, ensuring that people of different races live in the same neighbourhoods, so that they can better communicate and understand one another. Through daily contact and communication, the different races have come to tolerate each other, and the country has been able to develop in stability.
6 总zǒng而ér言yán之zhī，小xiǎo到dào个gè人rén和hé家jiā庭tíng，大dà至zhì团tuán体tǐ和hé国guó家jiā，都dōu离lí不bù开kāi沟gōu通tōng。良liáng好hǎo的de沟gōu通tōng会huì为wéi生shēng活huó添tiān加jiā更gèng多duō的de亮liàng色sè，让ràng社shè会huì更gèng加jiā安ān定dìng和hé谐xié。反fǎn之zhī，如rú果guǒ缺quē少shǎo沟gōu通tōng，很hěn多duō矛máo盾dùn和hé问wèn题tí都dū会huì因yīn之zhī而ér起qǐ。让ràng我wǒ们men从cóng今jīn天tiān开kāi始shǐ，从cóng自zì身shēn做zuò起qǐ，为wèi促cù进jìn人rén与yǔ人rén之zhī间jiān的de沟gōu通tōng与yǔ交jiāo流liú，尽jǐn自zì己jǐ的de一yī份fèn力lì。
6In sum, from individuals and families to groups and nations, none of us can do without communication. Good communication adds colour and brightness to life, and makes society more stable and harmonious. Without it, many of our conflicts and problems arise. Let us start today, with ourselves, and do our part to encourage communication and exchange between people.
