1 上shàng中zhōng学xué时shí，学xué业yè繁fán重zhòng，上shàng课kè时shí间jiān又yòu长zhǎng，因yīn此cǐ我wǒ和hé班bān上shàng的de几jǐ个gè女nǚ孩hái总zǒng会huì想xiǎng办bàn法fǎ调tiáo剂jì一yí下xià单dān调diào乏fá味wèi的de生shēng活huó。有yǒu一yī次cì，不bù知zhī是shì谁shuí带dài了le一yī罐guàn饼bǐng干gān，金jīn黄huáng色sè的de小xiǎo饼bǐng干gān上shàng粘zhān着zhe五wǔ颜yán六liù色sè的de糖táng粉fěn，既jì好hǎo看kàn又yòu好hǎo吃chī。
1Back in secondary school, the workload was heavy and the school day was long, so me and a few of the girls in class were always finding ways to break up the monotony. One day — I can't remember who brought them — there was a tin of biscuits. Little golden biscuits coated in colourful sugar sprinkles. They looked as good as they tasted.
2 从cóng那nà以yǐ后hòu，班bān上shàng便biàn流liú行xíng吃chī这zhè种zhǒng小xiǎo饼bǐng干gān。六liù个gè饼bǐng干gān罐guàn摆bǎi在zài教jiào室shì后hòu面miàn，就jiù好hǎo像xiàng我wǒ们men六liù个gè女nǚ孩hái子zi，亲qīn亲qīn热rè热rè地dì排pái成chéng一yī排pái。休xiū息xī铃líng声shēng一yī响xiǎng，我wǒ们men便biàn迫pò不bù及jí待dài地dì抱bào着zhe饼bǐng干gān罐guàn跑pǎo到dào校xiào园yuán内nèi的de一yī棵kē大dà树shù旁páng。大dà家jiā坐zuò在zài树shù荫yīn下xià，美měi滋zī滋zī地dì吃chī着zhe饼bǐng干gān。
2From then on, these little biscuits became the class fad. Six biscuit tins sat at the back of the classroom — like the six of us girls, lined up shoulder-to-shoulder. The moment the bell rang for recess, we'd scoop up our tins and dash off to a big tree in the school grounds. There we'd sit in the shade, happily munching away.
3 唯wéi独dú佳jiā敏mǐn是shì不bù大dà吃chī饼bǐng干gān的de，因yīn此cǐ她tā那nà个ge别bié致zhì的de青qīng瓷cí罐guàn里lǐ的de饼bǐng干gān，总zǒng是shì比bǐ我wǒ们men的de多duō，比bǐ我wǒ们men的de满mǎn。每měi次cì休xiū息xī时shí，我wǒ们men一yì边biān吃chī着zhe饼bǐng干gān，一yì边biān叽jī叽jī喳zhā喳zhā地dì聊liáo天tiān，而ér佳jiā敏mǐn总zǒng是shì在zài一yī旁páng读dú着zhe她tā那nà本běn似sì乎hū永yǒng远yuǎn也yě读dú不bù完wán的de小xiǎo说shuō。问wèn她tā为wèi什shén么me不bù吃chī，她tā说shuō：“吃chī多duō了le牙yá齿chǐ会huì坏huài的de！”
3Only Jia Min didn't eat much. So her elegant celadon jar always had more biscuits in it than ours, always fuller than ours. At recess, while we chattered away nibbling our biscuits, Jia Min would be off to one side, buried in that novel of hers that seemed like it would never end. Asked why she wasn't eating, she'd say, "If you eat too many, your teeth will go bad!"
4 后hòu来lái有yǒu人rén告gào诉sù我wǒ，佳jiā敏mǐn自zì幼yòu父fù母mǔ双shuāng亡wáng，跟gēn着zhe年nián老lǎo的de外wài婆pó一yì起qǐ生shēng活huó。她tā连lián学xué费fèi都dōu交jiāo不bù齐qí，哪nǎ还hái有yǒu闲xián钱qián买mǎi饼bǐng干gān吃chī？她tā那nà一yī罐guàn饼bǐng干gān，吃chī了le就jiù没méi得dé添tiān了le，她tā怕pà被bèi人rén瞧qiáo不bù起qǐ，就jiù借jiè口kǒu说shuō吃chī饼bǐng干gān牙yá齿chǐ会huì坏huài。我wǒ听tīng了le后hòu，心xīn头tóu一yī紧jǐn，有yǒu股gǔ说shuō不bù出chū的de悲bēi凉liáng。
4Later someone told me that Jia Min had lost both her parents when she was little, and was living with her elderly grandmother. She couldn't even afford her school fees — where would she find spare money for biscuits? Her one tin was all she had; once it was gone, there'd be no refilling it. She was afraid of being looked down on, so she made up the excuse about biscuits rotting your teeth. When I heard this, my heart clenched — an ache I couldn't put into words.
5 一yī次cì上shàng体tǐ育yù课kè，我wǒ因yīn为wèi口kǒu渴kě，匆cōng匆cōng忙máng忙máng地dì去qù食shí堂táng喝hē水shuǐ，没méi想xiǎng到dào接jiē下xià来lái却què目mù睹dǔ了le惊jīng人rén的de一yí幕mù。经jīng过guò教jiào室shì前qián的de花huā丛cóng时shí，我wǒ突tū然rán看kàn见jiàn佳jiā敏mǐn一yí个gè人rén站zhàn在zài饼bǐng干gān罐guàn前qián。她tā依yī次cì从cóng我wǒ们men的de饼bǐng干gān罐guàn中zhōng抓zhuā了le些xiē饼bǐng干gān塞sāi进jìn嘴zuǐ里lǐ，狼láng吞tūn虎hǔ咽yàn地dì吃chī着zhe。
5During one PE lesson, I was thirsty and rushed to the canteen for water — and ended up witnessing something that startled me. Passing the flowerbeds in front of our classroom, I suddenly saw Jia Min standing alone in front of the biscuit tins. She was grabbing handfuls from each of our tins in turn, stuffing them into her mouth, eating ravenously.
6 我wǒ看kàn得dé目mù瞪dèng口kǒu呆dāi。可kě不bù知zhī怎zěn么me的de，所suǒ有yǒu的de惊jīng讶yà与yǔ鄙bǐ夷yí渐jiàn渐jiàn散sàn去qù，两liǎng行xíng温wēn热rè的de泪lèi水shuǐ却què无wú声shēng无wú息xī地dì流liú淌tǎng下xià来lái。我wǒ怕pà她tā发fā现xiàn，转zhuǎn身shēn悄qiāo悄qiāo地dì离lí开kāi。
6I stood there dumbfounded. But somehow, all my shock and disdain quietly melted away — and two warm streams of tears began trickling down my face. Afraid she'd notice me, I turned and slipped away.
7 放fàng学xué后hòu，等děng教jiào室shì里lǐ的de人rén都dōu走zǒu了le，我wǒ飞fēi快kuài地dì关guān上shàng门mén，拉lā上shàng窗chuāng帘lián，一yī把bǎ抱bào起qǐ我wǒ的de饼bǐng干gān罐guàn，先xiān给gěi另lìng外wài的de几jǐ个gè逐zhú一yī补bǔ上shàng一yī大dà把bǎ饼bǐng干gān，再zài将jiāng剩shèng下xià的de通tōng通tōng倒dào进jìn那nà个ge青qīng瓷cí罐guàn。然rán后hòu我wǒ从cóng书shū包bāo里lǐ取qǔ出chū一yī袋dài饼bǐng干gān，倒dào入rù自zì己jǐ的de饼bǐng干gān罐guàn。提tí心xīn吊diào胆dǎn地dì忙máng完wán这zhè一yī切qiè，我wǒ的de心xīn才cái慢màn慢màn平píng静jìng下xià来lái。
7After school, once everyone had left the classroom, I quickly shut the door and drew the curtains. I scooped up my own tin, topped up each of the other girls' tins with a big handful, and tipped the rest into the celadon jar. Then I pulled a packet of biscuits out of my schoolbag and filled my own tin back up. Only after I'd finished all this, heart racing the whole time, did I finally calm down.
8 时shí光guāng飞fēi逝shì，中zhōng学xué生shēng活huó很hěn快kuài就jiù结jié束shù了le，同tóng学xué们men各gè奔bèn前qián程chéng。十shí多duō年nián后hòu，我wǒ收shōu到dào了le一yī封fēng信xìn。
8Time flew by. Secondary school soon ended, and everyone went their separate ways. More than ten years later, a letter arrived.
9 ……你nǐ一yí定dìng还hái记jì得de那nà个ge青qīng瓷cí罐guàn吧ba？那nà是shì我wǒ外wài婆pó留liú给gěi我wǒ的de，据jù说shuō还hái是shì个gè古gǔ董dǒng。现xiàn在zài，居jū然rán有yǒu人rén愿yuàn意yì出chū几jǐ万wàn块kuài要yào买mǎi它tā，可kě是shì我wǒ怎zěn么me可kě能néng舍shě得de卖mài呢ne？你nǐ倒dào进jìn去qù的de饼bǐng干gān，它tā的de价jià值zhí不bú是shì金jīn钱qián可kě以yǐ衡héng量liáng的de啊a！
9…You must still remember that celadon jar, don't you? My grandmother left it to me — apparently it's an antique. These days, people are offering tens of thousands for it — but how could I ever bear to sell it? The biscuits you poured into it — their value is beyond anything money can measure!
10 其qí实shí，当dāng年nián那nà个ge偷tōu饼bǐng干gān吃chī的de女nǚ孩hái，那nà时shí已yǐ经jīng觉jué察chá到dào了le花huā丛cóng中zhōng的de那nà双shuāng眼yǎn睛jīng——那nà双shuāng世shì界jiè上shàng最zuì纯chún洁jié、最zuì美měi丽lì的de眼yǎn睛jīng。因yīn为wèi那nà双shuāng眼yǎn睛jīng的de注zhù视shì，更gèng因yīn为wèi那nà之zhī后hòu的de宽kuān容róng与yǔ体tǐ谅liàng，那nà个ge差chà点diǎn儿ér走zǒu上shàng歧qí途tú的de女nǚ孩hái，在zài后hòu来lái充chōng满mǎn苦kǔ难nàn的de岁suì月yuè里lǐ，再zài也yě不bù曾céng走zǒu错cuò一yī步bù……
10The truth is, that girl stealing biscuits all those years ago had already sensed the pair of eyes watching from the flowerbeds — the purest, most beautiful pair of eyes in the world. Because of that gaze, and even more because of the kindness and understanding that followed, that girl — who had been one step from going astray — never took another wrong step through all the hard years that came after…
11 这zhè封fēng信xìn是shì我wǒ当dāng年nián的de同tóng窗chuāng佳jiā敏mǐn寄jì来lái的de，她tā现xiàn在zài是shì一yī位wèi成chéng功gōng的de企qǐ业yè家jiā。
11That letter was from Jia Min, my old schoolmate. Today, she is a successful businesswoman.
