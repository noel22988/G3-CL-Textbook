1 随suí着zhe移yí动dòng互hù联lián网wǎng的de普pǔ及jí，越yuè来lái越yuè多duō的de居jū民mín在zài社shè交jiāo网wǎng络luò平píng台tái上shàng创chuàng建jiàn社shè区qū网wǎng络luò群qún组zǔ，交jiāo流liú信xìn息xī，分fēn享xiǎng经jīng验yàn。这zhè种zhǒng沟gōu通tōng方fāng式shì不bù但dàn促cù进jìn了le居jū民mín之zhī间jiān的de交jiāo流liú，也yě让ràng邻lín里lǐ之zhī间jiān的de感gǎn情qíng更gèng加jiā深shēn厚hòu。我wǒ们men访fǎng问wèn了le三sān位wèi加jiā入rù社shè区qū网wǎng络luò群qún组zǔ的de居jū民mín，了liǎo解jiě他tā们men的de体tǐ验yàn和hé看kàn法fǎ。
1With mobile internet now everywhere, more and more residents are creating neighbourhood chat groups on social media to exchange information and share experiences. This way of communicating not only improves how residents connect, but also strengthens the bonds between neighbours. We spoke to three residents who have joined such groups, to hear about their experiences and views.
一yì起qǐ团tuán购gòu
Group Buying Together
2 居jū民mín加jiā入rù社shè区qū网wǎng络luò群qún组zǔ，可kě以yǐ和hé邻lín居jū一yì起qǐ团tuán购gòu，节jié省shěng生shēng活huó开kāi销xiāo。我wǒ们men是shì三sān口kǒu之zhī家jiā，在zài订dìng购gòu食shí物wù时shí，订dìng得dé少shǎo不bù划huá算suàn，订dìng得dé多duō又yòu吃chī不bù完wán。加jiā入rù群qún组zǔ后hòu，我wǒ开kāi始shǐ跟gēn邻lín居jū一yì起qǐ上shàng网wǎng团tuán购gòu，不bù但dàn价jià格gé便pián宜yi，还hái能néng节jié省shěng运yùn费fèi。时shí间jiān久jiǔ了le，我wǒ们men都dōu对duì彼bǐ此cǐ的de饮yǐn食shí习xí惯guàn和hé喜xǐ好hào有yǒu了le更gèng多duō的de了liǎo解jiě。所suǒ谓wèi“邻lín里lǐ一yì家jiā人rén，互hù助zhù大dà家jiā亲qīn”，网wǎng络luò群qún组zǔ增zēng进jìn了le邻lín里lǐ之zhī间jiān的de感gǎn情qíng。 ——马mǎ女nǚ士shì （37岁suì，家jiā庭tíng主zhǔ妇fù）
2Joining the neighbourhood chat group lets you do group buys with your neighbours and cut down on household spending. We're a family of three — ordering too little isn't economical, but ordering too much means the food goes to waste. Once I joined the group, I started doing online group buys with the neighbours — not only are prices lower, we also save on delivery fees. Over time, we've all come to know each other's food habits and preferences better. As the saying goes, "Neighbours are one family, helping each other brings everyone close." The chat group has deepened our relationships. — Mrs Ma (37, homemaker)
共gòng享xiǎng资zī源yuán
Sharing Resources
3 我wǒ曾céng经jīng通tōng过guò群qún组zǔ向xiàng邻lín居jū借jiè锯jù子zi，邻lín居jū也yě曾céng向xiàng我wǒ借jiè梯tī子zi。有yǒu的de邻lín居jū孩hái子zi长zhǎng大dà了le，就jiù通tōng过guò群qún组zǔ把bǎ一yì些xiē玩wán具jù送sòng给gěi有yǒu需xū要yào的de人rén家jiā。加jiā入rù社shè区qū网wǎng络luò群qún组zǔ，邻lín居jū之zhī间jiān可kě以yǐ互hù通tōng有yǒu无wú，关guān系xì也yě变biàn得dé更gèng好hǎo了le。 ——王wáng先xiān生shēng（51岁suì，工gōng程chéng师shī）
3I've borrowed a saw from a neighbour through the group, and another neighbour has borrowed a ladder from me. Some neighbours whose children have grown up pass on old toys through the group to families who need them. Joining the neighbourhood chat group means neighbours can share what they have — and our relationships have grown better because of it. — Mr Wang (51, engineer)
通tōng报bào及jí分fēn享xiǎng社shè区qū信xìn息xī
Passing On Community News
4 居jū民mín可kě以yǐ通tōng过guò社shè区qū网wǎng络luò群qún组zǔ迅xùn速sù通tōng报bào信xìn息xī，及jí时shí解jiě决jué问wèn题tí。最zuì近jìn，我wǒ在zài遛liú狗gǒu时shí，拴shuān狗gǒu的de绳shéng子zi没méi有yǒu系xì牢láo，一yī不bù留liú神shén，它tā就jiù跑pǎo丢diū了le。我wǒ找zhǎo了le好hǎo久jiǔ，仍réng不bú见jiàn小xiǎo狗gǒu的de踪zōng影yǐng。我wǒ赶gǎn紧jǐn通tōng过guò群qún组zǔ发fā出chū求qiú助zhù信xìn息xī，请qǐng大dà家jiā帮bāng忙máng寻xún找zhǎo。结jié果guǒ不bú到dào一yí个gè小xiǎo时shí，就jiù有yǒu邻lín居jū帮bāng我wǒ找zhǎo到dào了le小xiǎo狗gǒu。以yǐ前qián，社shè区qū活huó动dòng的de信xìn息xī都dōu贴tiē在zài组zǔ屋wū底dǐ层céng的de布bù告gào栏lán上shàng，我wǒ们men经jīng过guò时shí，未wèi必bì会huì留liú意yì。现xiàn在zài，这zhè些xiē信xìn息xī都dōu发fā在zài群qún组zǔ里lǐ，我wǒ们men可kě以yǐ更gèng容róng易yì了liǎo解jiě活huó动dòng内nèi容róng。这zhè样yàng一yī来lái，也yě有yǒu助zhù于yú鼓gǔ励lì更gèng多duō居jū民mín参cān与yù社shè区qū活huó动dòng，促cù进jìn邻lín里lǐ关guān系xì。 ——何hé小xiǎo姐jiě（20岁suì，大dà学xué生shēng）
4Through the chat group, residents can share news quickly and solve problems in time. Recently, I was walking my dog when the leash wasn't fastened properly — I looked away for just a moment and he ran off. I searched and searched, but there was no sign of him. I quickly put out a call for help on the group. In less than an hour, a neighbour had found him for me. In the past, community event notices were posted on the notice board in the void deck of our HDB block — we might walk past without noticing. Now, those notices go straight into the group chat, so we know what's happening much more easily. That also helps encourage more residents to take part in community events, which strengthens neighbourhood ties. — Miss He (20, university student)
5 过guò去qù，有yǒu些xiē人rén虽suī然rán同tóng住zhù一yí个gè社shè区qū，但dàn彼bǐ此cǐ之zhī间jiān很hěn少shǎo往wǎng来lái。久jiǔ而ér久jiǔ之zhī，身shēn边biān的de邻lín居jū都dōu成chéng了le“熟shú悉xī的de陌mò生shēng人rén”。现xiàn在zài，社shè区qū网wǎng络luò群qún组zǔ为wèi邻lín里lǐ之zhī间jiān的de沟gōu通tōng与yǔ互hù动dòng提tí供gōng了le新xīn的de渠qú道dào。虽suī然rán这zhè种zhǒng新xīn的de沟gōu通tōng方fāng式shì可kě能néng存cún在zài一yì些xiē弊bì端duān，但dàn整zhěng体tǐ而ér言yán，仍réng是shì利lì大dà于yú弊bì。总zǒng之zhī，社shè区qū网wǎng络luò群qún组zǔ促cù进jìn了le邻lín居jū之zhī间jiān的de互hù动dòng与yǔ互hù助zhù，加jiā深shēn了le他tā们men对duì彼bǐ此cǐ的de了liǎo解jiě，也yě增zēng强qiáng了le他tā们men对duì社shè区qū的de归guī属shǔ感gǎn。
5In the past, some people lived in the same community for years but rarely interacted. Over time, the neighbours around them became "familiar strangers." Today, neighbourhood chat groups offer a new channel for communication and interaction between residents. Though this new way of communicating has some drawbacks, on the whole the benefits far outweigh the costs. In short, neighbourhood chat groups promote interaction and mutual support between neighbours, deepen their understanding of each other, and strengthen their sense of belonging to the community.
