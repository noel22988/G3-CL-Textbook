《唯一的听众》 The Only Listener

语速 Speed 0.9× (set before play)

生词 Vocabulary · 7 words

洋yáng溢yì

慈cí祥xiáng

1 用yòng父fù亲qīn和hé妹mèi妹mèi的de话huà来lái说shuō，我wǒ在zài音yīn乐yuè方fāng面miàn简jiǎn直zhí是shì个gè“白bái痴chī”。这zhè是shì他tā们men受shòu了le我wǒ数shù次cì“折zhé磨mó”之zhī后hòu所suǒ下xià的de结jié论lùn。因yīn此cǐ我wǒ不bù敢gǎn在zài家jiā里lǐ练liàn习xí，每měi次cì都dōu跑pǎo到dào家jiā后hòu面miàn的de一yī片piàn树shù林lín里lǐ练liàn琴qín。
1In my father's and little sister's words, I was a complete "idiot" when it came to music. That was the verdict they'd reached after suffering through my practice several times. So I didn't dare practise at home — every day I ran off to a small patch of woods behind the house to play my violin.
2 一yì天tiān早zǎo晨chén，我wǒ悄qiāo悄qiāo走zǒu出chū家jiā门mén，心xīn里lǐ充chōng满mǎn了le神shén圣shèng感gǎn，仿fǎng佛fú要yào去qù干gàn一yī件jiàn非fēi常cháng伟wěi大dà的de事shì。树shù林lín里lǐ静jìng极jí了le。我wǒ在zài一yī棵kē树shù下xià站zhàn好hǎo，架jià起qǐ小xiǎo提tí琴qín，像xiàng举jǔ行xíng一yí个gè隆lóng重zhòng的de仪yí式shì，拉lā响xiǎng了le第dì一yī支zhī曲qǔ子zi。但dàn难nán听tīng的de琴qín声shēng让ràng我wǒ很hěn快kuài又yòu沮jǔ丧sàng起qǐ来lái，我wǒ不bù由yóu得de诅zǔ咒zhòu自zì己jǐ：“我wǒ真zhēn是shì个gè白bái痴chī！”
2One morning, I slipped out quietly, a sense of reverence in my heart, as if I were about to do something tremendously important. The woods were utterly still. I stood under a tree, raised my violin like someone beginning a grand ceremony, and played the first notes. But the ugly sound of my playing soon plunged me back into despair. I couldn't help cursing myself: "I really am an idiot!"
3 突tū然rán，我wǒ感gǎn觉jué到dào背bèi后hòu有yǒu人rén，转zhuǎn过guò身shēn时shí，吓xià了le一yī跳tiào：一yī位wèi极jí瘦shòu极jí瘦shòu的de老lǎo妇fù人rén静jìng静jìng地dì坐zuò在zài木mù椅yǐ上shàng，平píng静jìng地dì望wàng着zhe我wǒ。我wǒ的de脸liǎn顿dùn时shí烧shāo起qǐ来lái，心xīn想xiǎng：这zhè么me难nán听tīng的de声shēng音yīn一yí定dìng破pò坏huài了le这zhè位wèi老lǎo人rén独dú享xiǎng的de幽yōu静jìng。
3Suddenly, I sensed someone behind me. I spun round — and got a shock: a very, very thin old woman was sitting quietly on a wooden bench, calmly watching me. My face flushed hot in an instant. I must have ruined this old lady's private peace with such an awful sound, I thought.
4 我wǒ抱bào歉qiàn地dì冲chōng老lǎo人rén笑xiào了le笑xiào，准zhǔn备bèi溜liū走zǒu。老lǎo人rén叫jiào住zhù了le我wǒ，说shuō：“是shì我wǒ打dǎ扰rǎo了le你nǐ吗ma，小xiǎo伙huǒ子zi？不bù过guò，我wǒ每měi天tiān早zǎo晨chén都dōu在zài这zhè儿ér坐zuò一yī会huì儿er。我wǒ猜cāi你nǐ一yí定dìng拉lā得dé非fēi常cháng好hǎo，只zhǐ可kě惜xī我wǒ的de耳ěr朵duǒ聋lóng了le。如rú果guǒ不bù介jiè意yì我wǒ在zài场chǎng的de话huà，请qǐng继jì续xù吧ba。”
4I gave her an apologetic smile and was about to slip away. The old lady called me back. "Am I disturbing you, young man? I sit here for a little while every morning. I'm sure you play very well — it's just that my ears are deaf. If you don't mind me being here, do please carry on."
5 我wǒ指zhǐ了le指zhǐ琴qín，摇yáo了le摇yáo头tóu，意yì思si是shì说shuō我wǒ拉lā不bù好hǎo。
5I pointed at the violin and shook my head — meaning that I wasn't any good.
6 “也yě许xǔ我wǒ会huì用yòng心xīn去qù感gǎn受shòu这zhè音yīn乐yuè。我wǒ能néng做zuò你nǐ的de听tīng众zhòng吗ma？每měi天tiān早zǎo晨chén？”
6"Perhaps I can feel the music with my heart. Could I be your audience? Every morning?"
7 老lǎo人rén的de话huà让ràng我wǒ有yǒu些xiē羞xiū愧kuì，同tóng时shí也yě给gěi了le我wǒ几jǐ分fēn信xìn心xīn。毕bì竟jìng有yǒu人rén夸kuā我wǒ了le，尽jǐn管guǎn她tā听tīng不bú见jiàn。于yú是shì，我wǒ又yòu开kāi始shǐ拉lā了le起qǐ来lái。
7The old woman's words left me feeling a little ashamed, but they also gave me a small measure of confidence. After all, someone had praised me — even if she couldn't hear. So I began to play again.
8 在zài这zhè以yǐ后hòu，每měi天tiān清qīng晨chén，我wǒ都dōu到dào小xiǎo树shù林lín里lǐ去qù练liàn琴qín，面miàn对duì唯wéi一yī的de听tīng众zhòng——一yī位wèi耳ěr聋lóng的de老lǎo人rén。她tā总zǒng是shì很hěn平píng静jìng地dì望wàng着zhe我wǒ，每měi当dāng我wǒ停tíng下xià来lái时shí，她tā都dōu不bù忘wàng说shuō一yī句jù：“真zhēn不bù错cuò。我wǒ的de心xīn已yǐ经jīng感gǎn受shòu到dào了le。谢xiè谢xiè你nǐ，小xiǎo伙huǒ子zi。”我wǒ心xīn里lǐ洋yáng溢yì着zhe一yī种zhǒng前qián所suǒ未wèi有yǒu的de感gǎn觉jué。
8From then on, every morning, I went to the little woods to practise — playing for my one and only listener, a deaf old lady. She always watched me calmly, and whenever I paused, she never forgot to say: "That was lovely. My heart has felt it. Thank you, young man." A feeling I had never known before brimmed inside me.
9 很hěn快kuài我wǒ就jiù发fā觉jué自zì己jǐ变biàn了le，我wǒ又yòu在zài家jiā里lǐ练liàn琴qín了le。一yī种zhǒng力lì量liàng在zài我wǒ身shēn上shàng滋zī长zhǎng。以yǐ前qián我wǒ坐zuò在zài椅yǐ子zi上shàng拉lā琴qín，现xiàn在zài则zé站zhàn着zhe练liàn习xí。我wǒ站zhàn得dé很hěn直zhí，两liǎng臂bì累lèi得dé又yòu酸suān又yòu痛tòng，同tóng时shí，汗hàn水shuǐ湿shī透tòu了le衬chèn衣yī。
9Soon, I noticed I had changed. I began practising at home again. A new kind of strength was growing in me. Where I used to sit on a chair while playing, now I stood — standing straight, both arms aching with fatigue, sweat soaking through my shirt.
10 每měi天tiān清qīng晨chén，我wǒ都dōu要yào为wèi这zhè位wèi耳ěr聋lóng的de听tīng众zhòng尽jìn心xīn尽jìn力lì地dì演yǎn奏zòu，而ér她tā总zǒng是shì早zǎo早zǎo地dì坐zuò在zài木mù椅yǐ上shàng等děng我wǒ。有yǒu一yī次cì，她tā说shuō我wǒ的de琴qín声shēng给gěi她tā带dài来lái快kuài乐lè和hé幸xìng福fú。我wǒ也yě常cháng常cháng忘wàng记jì了le她tā听tīng不bú见jiàn。只zhǐ看kàn见jiàn老lǎo人rén微wēi笑xiào着zhe靠kào在zài木mù椅yǐ上shàng，手shǒu指zhǐ好hǎo像xiàng在zài打dǎ着zhe节jié奏zòu。她tā平píng静jìng地dì望wàng着zhe我wǒ，慈cí祥xiáng的de眼yǎn神shén像xiàng深shēn深shēn的de潭tán水shuǐ。
10Each morning, I poured my heart into playing for that deaf listener, who was always already waiting for me on the wooden bench. One day, she said my playing brought her joy and happiness. I often forgot, too, that she couldn't hear. All I saw was the old lady leaning against the bench, smiling, her fingers moving as if keeping time. She watched me calmly, her kindly eyes like a deep, still pool.
11 我wǒ一yì直zhí珍zhēn藏cáng着zhe这zhè个ge秘mì密mì，直zhí到dào有yǒu一yì天tiān，我wǒ拉lā的de乐yuè曲qǔ让ràng主zhǔ修xiū音yīn乐yuè的de妹mèi妹mèi大dà吃chī一yī惊jīng。她tā追zhuī问wèn我wǒ得dé到dào了le哪nǎ位wèi名míng师shī的de指zhǐ点diǎn，我wǒ告gào诉sù她tā：“是shì一yī位wèi老lǎo太tài太tài，就jiù住zhù在zài12号hào楼lóu，非fēi常cháng瘦shòu，满mǎn头tóu白bái发fà，不bù过guò她tā是shì一yí个gè聋lóng子zi。”
11I kept this secret treasured in my heart until one day, when a piece I played left my sister — who was majoring in music — utterly stunned. She pressed me to tell her which great teacher I'd been studying with. I told her: "An old lady who lives in Block 12. Very thin, with a full head of white hair — but she's deaf."
12 “聋lóng子zi？”妹mèi妹mèi惊jīng叫jiào起qǐ来lái，仿fǎng佛fú我wǒ在zài开kāi玩wán笑xiào，“她tā是shì音yīn乐yuè学xué院yuàn最zuì有yǒu声shēng望wàng的de教jiào授shòu，曾céng经jīng是shì乐yuè团tuán的de首shǒu席xí小xiǎo提tí琴qín手shǒu。你nǐ竟jìng说shuō她tā是shì聋lóng子zi！”
12"Deaf?" my sister cried out, as if I were joking. "She's the most renowned professor at the music conservatory — and used to be the principal violinist of the orchestra. And you're telling me she's deaf!"
13 后hòu来lái，拉lā小xiǎo提tí琴qín成chéng了le我wǒ无wú法fǎ割gē舍shě的de爱ài好hào。我wǒ也yě参cān加jiā了le许xǔ多duō演yǎn出chū，为wèi成chéng百bǎi上shàng千qiān的de观guān众zhòng演yǎn奏zòu。
13In the years that followed, playing the violin became a love I could never let go of. I've taken part in many performances, playing for audiences of hundreds, even thousands.
14 每měi当dāng拿ná起qǐ小xiǎo提tí琴qín时shí，我wǒ眼yǎn前qián就jiù浮fú现xiàn出chū那nà位wèi耳ěr“聋lóng”的de老lǎo人rén，那nà位wèi我wǒ每měi天tiān清qīng晨chén面miàn对duì的de唯wéi一yī的de听tīng众zhòng。
14Every time I pick up the violin, the image of that "deaf" old lady comes back to me — the one and only listener I played for every morning.
