About the Exhibition
Rodin's Words. "Beauty is not lacking in life — what's lacking are eyes that can see it." — Auguste Rodin
Curator's Note. As long as we feel things with our heart, we'll discover that beautiful things are everywhere. Our students have used their lenses to capture fleeting moments of beauty, and photographs to interpret their own understanding of the true, the good, and the beautiful.
吴wú敬jìng明míng（中zhōng二èr）
作zuò品pǐn说shuō明míng上shàng学xué途tú中zhōng，我wǒ看kàn见jiàn一yī辆liàng汽qì车chē因yīn抛pāo锚máo而ér停tíng在zài路lù中zhōng央yāng，司sī机jī显xiǎn得de惊jīng慌huāng失shī措cuò。这zhè时shí，一yī辆liàng载zài着zhe客kè工gōng的de罗luó厘lí停tíng了le下xià来lái。客kè工gōng们men都dōu跳tiào下xià罗luó厘lí，帮bāng忙máng把bǎ车chē推tuī到dào路lù边biān。他tā们men的de行xíng为wéi令lìng我wǒ既jì感gǎn动dòng又yòu敬jìng佩pèi。
Wu Jingming (Secondary 2)
Photo Caption. On my way to school, I saw a car that had broken down right in the middle of the road, with its driver looking panicked. Just then, a lorry carrying migrant workers pulled up. The workers jumped down and helped push the car to the side of the road. What they did moved and impressed me deeply.

Li Zhenrong (Secondary 1)
Photo Caption. Mao Mao is a stray dog I adopted two years ago. Every time I come home, he runs up and leaps at me, tail wagging with excitement. Whenever he sees I'm feeling low, he'll bring a ball in his mouth, coaxing me to play fetch with him and cheer me up. His total trust and reliance on me have turned into a warm strength — they give me the courage to face life's challenges.

Wen Qing'an (Secondary 3)
Photo Caption. Last year, a friend and I went hiking overseas. After climbing all through the night, we finally reached the summit just before sunrise. As the sun rose through a sea of clouds, we all leapt with joy. Gazing at the mountain range in the distance, we understood what it means that "Heaven and Earth hold a great beauty, and speak no word of it." Looking out at the world and the vast universe beyond, we knew there were still countless beautiful things waiting for us to discover and experience.
