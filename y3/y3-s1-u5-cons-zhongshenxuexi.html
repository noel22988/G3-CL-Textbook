1 终zhōng身shēn学xué习xí指zhǐ的de是shì持chí续xù的de、贯guàn穿chuān一yī生shēng的de学xué习xí过guò程chéng。每měi个gè人rén都dōu应yīng该gāi具jù备bèi终zhōng身shēn学xué习xí的de精jīng神shén。
1Lifelong learning means continuous learning that runs through the whole of a person's life. Everyone should embrace the spirit of lifelong learning.
2 为wèi什shén么me我wǒ们men应yīng该gāi抱bào有yǒu终zhōng身shēn学xué习xí的de精jīng神shén？因yīn为wèi只zhǐ有yǒu如rú此cǐ，我wǒ们men才cái能néng在zài人rén生shēng中zhōng不bù断duàn取qǔ得dé进jìn步bù。人rén们men常cháng说shuō“活huó到dào老lǎo，学xué到dào老lǎo”，因yīn为wèi在zài人rén生shēng的de各gè个gè阶jiē段duàn都dōu有yǒu需xū要yào学xué习xí的de事shì物wù。不bù断duàn学xué习xí，我wǒ们men才cái能néng持chí续xù进jìn步bù，才cái不bú会huì因yīn为wèi与yǔ时shí代dài脱tuō节jié而ér落luò伍wǔ。建jiàn国guó总zǒng理lǐ李lǐ光guāng耀yào先xiān生shēng就jiù是shì这zhè方fāng面miàn的de楷kǎi模mó。他tā一yī生shēng都dōu在zài孜zī孜zī不bù倦juàn地dì努nǔ力lì学xué习xí。年nián轻qīng时shí，他tā刻kè苦kǔ攻gōng读dú，成chéng为wéi一yī名míng出chū色sè的de律lǜ师shī。成chéng为wéi总zǒng理lǐ后hòu，虽suī然rán公gōng务wù繁fán忙máng，但dàn他tā仍réng然rán不bù断duàn学xué习xí各gè种zhǒng知zhī识shí和hé技jì能néng。到dào了le晚wǎn年nián，他tā也yě从cóng未wèi停tíng止zhǐ学xué习xí，而ér且qiě从cóng不bù敷fū衍yǎn了liǎo事shì。他tā88岁suì时shí，有yǒu人rén问wèn他tā为wèi何hé如rú此cǐ有yǒu活huó力lì，他tā回huí应yìng道dào：每měi天tiān学xué习xí新xīn的de事shì物wù，对duì生shēng命mìng充chōng满mǎn热rè忱chén，对duì周zhōu遭zāo事shì物wù和hé世shì界jiè大dà小xiǎo事shì都dōu要yào有yǒu好hào奇qí心xīn。可kě以yǐ说shuō，终zhōng身shēn学xué习xí的de信xìn念niàn在zài他tā身shēn上shàng得dé到dào充chōng分fèn的de体tǐ现xiàn，这zhè也yě是shì他tā不bù断duàn取qǔ得dé成chéng就jiù的de重zhòng要yào因yīn素sù之zhī一yī。
2Why should we hold on to the spirit of lifelong learning? Because only in this way can we keep progressing through life. People often say, "Live long, learn long" — because every stage of life has things we need to learn. Only by continuing to learn can we keep moving forward, without falling out of step with the times. Singapore's founding Prime Minister Mr Lee Kuan Yew is an exemplary model. All his life, he laboured at learning tirelessly. In his youth, he studied hard and became an outstanding lawyer. After becoming Prime Minister, despite an enormous workload, he continued to learn new knowledge and skills. Even in his later years, he never stopped learning — and never did so half-heartedly. When he was 88, someone asked him how he kept so energetic. He replied: learn something new every day, stay passionate about life, and keep curious about everything around you, big and small. You could say that the belief in lifelong learning was fully embodied in him, and it was one of the key reasons he kept on achieving throughout his life.
3 反fǎn之zhī，如rú果guǒ没méi有yǒu终zhōng身shēn学xué习xí的de精jīng神shén，则zé很hěn可kě能néng在zài学xué习xí上shàng取qǔ得dé一yì点diǎn成chéng果guǒ后hòu就jiù固gù步bù自zì封fēng，无wú法fǎ继jì续xù进jìn步bù。俗sú话huà说shuō得dé好hǎo，学xué如rú逆nì水shuǐ行xíng舟zhōu，不bù进jìn则zé退tuì。如rú果guǒ一yí个gè人rén毕bì业yè后hòu就jiù以yǐ为wéi到dào达dá了le学xué习xí的de终zhōng点diǎn，那nà么me接jiē下xià来lái他tā很hěn可kě能néng会huì失shī去qù学xué习xí的de动dòng力lì，无wú法fǎ再zài体tǐ会huì学xué习xí和hé探tàn索suǒ的de乐lè趣qù。这zhè样yàng的de人rén很hěn容róng易yì被bèi时shí代dài淘táo汰tài。宋sòng朝cháo文wén学xué家jiā王wáng安ān石shí笔bǐ下xià方fāng仲zhòng永yǒng的de故gù事shì，就jiù是shì一yí个gè很hěn好hǎo的de佐zuǒ证zhèng。方fāng仲zhòng永yǒng从cóng小xiǎo天tiān资zī聪cōng颖yǐng，小xiǎo小xiǎo年nián纪jì，写xiě的de诗shī就jiù令lìng人rén赞zàn叹tàn不bù已yǐ。出chū名míng后hòu，他tā父fù亲qīn带dài着zhe他tā四sì处chù拜bài访fǎng，夸kuā耀yào孩hái子zi的de才cái能néng。由yóu于yú没méi有yǒu继jì续xù学xué习xí，这zhè个ge孩hái子zi的de潜qián能néng得dé不bú到dào进jìn一yí步bù发fā展zhǎn，长zhǎng大dà后hòu成chéng了le一yí个gè平píng庸yōng的de人rén。由yóu此cǐ可kě见jiàn，即jí使shǐ天tiān赋fù很hěn高gāo，要yào是shì停tíng止zhǐ学xué习xí，也yě无wú法fǎ持chí续xù取qǔ得dé进jìn步bù。
3By contrast, without the spirit of lifelong learning, a person may well achieve a little and then close themselves off, unable to keep progressing. As the saying goes, learning is like rowing a boat against the current — if you don't advance, you drift back. If someone graduates and thinks they've reached the end of learning, they're likely to lose their motivation, and will no longer taste the joy of discovery and inquiry. Such a person is easily left behind by the times. The story of Fang Zhongyong, told by the Song-dynasty writer Wang Anshi, is a good example. Fang Zhongyong was blessed with a sharp mind from a young age — his childhood poems were extraordinary. Once he became famous, his father took him on visits to show off the boy's talent. Because he stopped learning, the boy's potential went undeveloped, and as he grew up he turned into an ordinary man. This shows that however great one's natural gifts, without continued learning, one cannot keep progressing.
4 有yǒu人rén觉jué得de，学xué习xí是shì一yī件jiàn辛xīn苦kǔ的de事shì，终zhōng身shēn学xué习xí岂qǐ不bù是shì一yī辈bèi子zi都dōu要yào很hěn辛xīn苦kǔ？其qí实shí，我wǒ们men应yīng该gāi扩kuò大dà对duì学xué习xí的de理lǐ解jiě，将jiāng学xué习xí看kàn作zuò是shì对duì精jīng彩cǎi人rén生shēng的de长cháng期qī探tàn索suǒ。每měi个gè人rén天tiān生shēng就jiù有yǒu学xué习xí的de能néng力lì，也yě有yǒu学xué习xí的de欲yù望wàng，在zài学xué有yǒu所suǒ得de时shí都dū会huì获huò得dé满mǎn足zú感gǎn。从cóng这zhè个ge意yì义yì上shàng说shuō，终zhōng身shēn学xué习xí不bù但dàn不bú会huì让ràng人rén生shēng变biàn得dé更gèng辛xīn苦kǔ，反fǎn而ér可kě以yǐ使shǐ人rén生shēng过guò得dé更gèng充chōng实shí，更gèng有yǒu意yì义yì。
4Some people think learning is hard work — does that mean lifelong learning is a lifetime of hardship? In truth, we should broaden our understanding of what learning means, and see it as a long exploration of a rich life. Everyone is born with the capacity and the desire to learn, and we all feel satisfaction when we've learned something. From this perspective, lifelong learning doesn't make life harder — it makes life fuller, and more meaningful.
5 要yào具jù备bèi终zhōng身shēn学xué习xí的de精jīng神shén就jiù不bù能néng纸zhǐ上shàng谈tán兵bīng，而ér是shì必bì须xū落luò实shí到dào行xíng动dòng上shàng。我wǒ们men要yào先xiān确què定dìng学xué习xí目mù标biāo，这zhè样yàng学xué习xí才cái会huì更gèng有yǒu动dòng力lì。在zài学xué习xí的de过guò程chéng中zhōng，不bù但dàn要yào通tōng过guò阅yuè读dú增zēng广guǎng见jiàn闻wén，也yě要yào与yǔ别bié人rén多duō多duō交jiāo流liú，向xiàng有yǒu经jīng验yàn的de人rén虚xū心xīn请qǐng教jiào。更gèng重zhòng要yào的de是shì，学xué习xí不bù应yīng该gāi只zhǐ着zhuó眼yǎn于yú眼yǎn前qián的de利lì益yì，而ér应yīng该gāi从cóng学xué习xí的de本běn质zhì出chū发fā，享xiǎng受shòu学xué习xí带dài来lái的de乐lè趣qù。
5To really embody lifelong learning, we can't stop at words on paper — we have to put it into action. First, we should set clear learning goals, so we have real motivation. In the process of learning, we should not only broaden our horizons through reading, but also talk and exchange ideas with others, and humbly seek advice from those with experience. Most importantly, learning shouldn't be pursued only for immediate benefit; it should start from the essence of learning itself — enjoying the joy that comes from it.
6 在zài瞬shùn息xī万wàn变biàn的de信xìn息xī时shí代dài，新xīn的de事shì物wù层céng出chū不bù穷qióng。只zhǐ要yào具jù备bèi终zhōng身shēn学xué习xí的de精jīng神shén，不bù管guǎn面miàn对duì怎zěn样yàng的de挑tiǎo战zhàn，我wǒ们men都dū会huì更gèng有yǒu信xìn心xīn去qù面miàn对duì。
6In today's fast-changing information age, new things keep emerging. So long as we have the spirit of lifelong learning, whatever challenges we face, we'll meet them with greater confidence.
