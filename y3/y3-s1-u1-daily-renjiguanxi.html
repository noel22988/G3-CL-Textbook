Treat Others with Sincerity
Xiao Tiao. We should treat others with sincerity. If you have concerns about a friend, you should say them openly — there's no need to hide them.
Tian Tian. Being frank is good, but you should also be mindful of how you say things. Don't be too blunt, and definitely not rude — otherwise you'll hurt people's feelings.
懂dǒng得de退tuì让ràng
义yì阳yáng当dāng我wǒ们men和hé别bié人rén的de意yì见jiàn出chū现xiàn分fēn歧qí时shí，要yào好hǎo好hǎo沟gōu通tōng。很hěn多duō时shí候hòu，只zhǐ要yào大dà家jiā互hù相xiāng包bāo容róng，各gè让ràng一yī步bù，就jiù可kě以yǐ化huà解jiě矛máo盾dùn和hé冲chōng突tū。
世shì亮liàng没méi错cuò！大dà至zhì国guó家jiā，小xiǎo至zhì个gè人rén，都dōu应yīng该gāi通tōng过guò磋cuō商shāng或huò沟gōu通tōng来lái达dá成chéng共gòng识shí，解jiě决jué纷fēn争zhēng。
Know When to Give Way
Yi Yang. When we disagree with someone, we should communicate properly. Often, if both sides are willing to be tolerant and each takes a step back, conflicts can be resolved.
Shi Liang. Exactly! Whether between nations or between individuals, consensus should be reached through negotiation or dialogue, and disputes resolved that way.

Understand Each Other
Xiao Shuang. Everyone has a different personality and upbringing. We need to learn to see things from the other person's perspective and put ourselves in their shoes — that's what makes our relationships harmonious.
Yun Fei. The key to mutual understanding is knowing how to listen. For example, Guoqiang is my best friend, and our personalities are completely different. But we're both willing to listen to each other's thoughts and understand each other — that's what has made our friendship so strong.
尊zūn重zhòng对duì方fāng
国guó强qiáng我wǒ们men要yào尊zūn重zhòng朋péng友yǒu的de想xiǎng法fǎ和hé感gǎn受shòu，不bù能néng强qiǎng迫pò他tā们men接jiē受shòu我wǒ们men的de意yì见jiàn。我wǒ们men也yě要yào尊zūn重zhòng朋péng友yǒu的de隐yǐn私sī，不bù能néng把bǎ他tā们men的de隐yǐn私sī透tòu露lù给gěi别bié人rén。
丽lì华huá我wǒ同tóng意yì！我wǒ有yǒu个gè同tóng学xué经jīng常cháng谈tán论lùn别bié人rén的de隐yǐn私sī，最zuì后hòu大dà家jiā都dōu不bù愿yuàn跟gēn他tā交jiāo往wǎng。
Respect the Other Person
Guoqiang. We should respect our friends' thoughts and feelings, and not force them to accept our opinions. We should also respect their privacy — don't leak what they've shared in confidence.
Lihua. I agree! I have a classmate who loves to talk about other people's private matters — in the end, no one wanted to be friends with him.
