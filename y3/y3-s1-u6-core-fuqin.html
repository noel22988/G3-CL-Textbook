1 阿ā丽lì到dào机jī场chǎng去qù迎yíng接jiē自zì彭péng亨hēng到dào斗dòu湖hú来lái的de父fù亲qīn。他tā长cháng途tú跋bá涉shè的de原yuán因yīn是shì想xiǎng念niàn他tā那nà个ge独dú生shēng外wài孙sūn志zhì明míng。
1Ah Li went to the airport to meet her father, who had come all the way from Pahang to Tawau. The long journey had only one reason: he missed his only grandson, Zhiming.
2 看kàn见jiàn父fù亲qīn从cóng机jī场chǎng走zǒu出chū来lái，阿ā丽lì非fēi常cháng高gāo兴xìng。她tā和hé丈zhàng夫fū结jié婚hūn后hòu，从cóng西xī马mǎ的de中zhōng部bù搬bān迁qiān到dào东dōng马mǎ，和hé父fù亲qīn相xiāng聚jù的de时shí间jiān因yīn此cǐ变biàn少shǎo了le。
2When Ah Li saw her father walking out of the arrivals gate, she was overjoyed. After marrying her husband, she had moved from central West Malaysia to East Malaysia, so her time with her father had grown scarce.
3 阿ā丽lì从cóng小xiǎo和hé父fù亲qīn不bù太tài亲qīn密mì，见jiàn到dào父fù亲qīn时shí，也yě不bù习xí惯guàn用yòng言yán语yǔ表biǎo达dá那nà深shēn埋mái在zài心xīn中zhōng的de思sī念niàn和hé牵qiān挂guà，每měi次cì接jiē机jī时shí，就jiù只zhǐ是shì淡dàn淡dàn地dì唤huàn一yī句jù：“爸bà爸bà。”
3Ah Li had never been close to her father growing up. Even seeing him now, she couldn't quite bring herself to put into words the longing and concern buried deep in her heart — at every airport pickup, all she could manage was a quiet "Dad."
4 父fù亲qīn看kàn到dào女nǚ儿ér阿ā丽lì，心xīn里lǐ很hěn高gāo兴xìng，但dàn脸liǎn上shàng的de表biǎo情qíng却què十shí分fēn严yán肃sù，只zhǐ在zài喉hóu咙lóng里lǐ深shēn深shēn地dì“嗯ń”了le一yī声shēng。
4Seeing his daughter Ah Li, her father was secretly delighted — but his face stayed stern. All that came out of his throat was a deep, noncommittal grunt.
5 “阿ā公gōng，行xíng李lǐ让ràng我wǒ来lái提tí。”志zhì明míng上shàng前qián把bǎ行xíng李lǐ接jiē过guò去qù。
5"Grandpa, let me take the luggage." Zhiming stepped forward and took the bags from him.
6 父fù亲qīn欣xīn慰wèi地dì看kàn着zhe孝xiào顺shùn的de外wài孙sūn，脸liǎn上shàng的de笑xiào容róng犹yóu如rú绽zhàn放fàng的de鲜xiān花huā。他tā心xīn里lǐ对duì阿ā丽lì当dāng年nián不bù听tīng话huà，不bù多duō生shēng几jǐ个gè孩hái子zi的de事shì仍réng旧jiù不bù能néng释shì怀huái。
6Her father looked gratefully at his filial grandson, a smile blooming on his face like an opening flower. Yet deep down, he still couldn't let go of how Ah Li had defied him years ago by not having more children.
7 一yī到dào家jiā，阿ā丽lì就jiù交jiāo代dài佣yōng人rén开kāi饭fàn。本běn来lái和hé外wài孙sūn有yǒu说shuō有yǒu笑xiào的de父fù亲qīn，一yī看kàn到dào桌zhuō子zi上shàng的de菜cài，便biàn绷bēng着zhe脸liǎn不bù讲jiǎng话huà。
7The moment they got home, Ah Li told the helper to serve lunch. Her father, who had been laughing and joking with his grandson, fell silent the instant he saw the dishes on the table, his face tightening.
8 阿ā丽lì看kàn到dào父fù亲qīn的de脸liǎn色sè，急jí忙máng解jiě释shì：“伟wěi成chéng说shuō不bù必bì等děng他tā。公gōng司sī的de事shì很hěn忙máng，他tā中zhōng午wǔ一yì般bān都dōu不bù回huí来lái吃chī饭fàn。”父fù亲qīn的de脸liǎn色sè依yī然rán阴yīn沉chén，对duì于yú老lǎo人rén家jiā的de不bù谅liàng解jiě。阿ā丽lì觉jué得de很hěn无wú奈nài。
8Seeing his expression, Ah Li hurried to explain: "Weicheng said not to wait for him. Things are busy at the office — he generally doesn't come home for lunch." Her father's face remained dark. Faced with the old man's refusal to understand, Ah Li could only feel helpless.
9 一yī顿dùn饭fàn唯wéi有yǒu志zhì明míng轻qīng松sōng愉yú快kuài地dì说shuō话huà：“阿ā公gōng，等děng一yí下xià我wǒ载zài您nín去qù看kàn新xīn建jiàn的de一yī座zuò寺sì庙miào，那nà里lǐ幽yōu静jìng又yòu干gān净jìng，您nín一yí定dìng喜xǐ欢huān。”
9Only Zhiming filled the meal with easy, cheerful chatter: "Grandpa, in a bit I'll take you to see a newly-built temple — it's peaceful and clean, you'll love it."
10 “好hǎo的de。”父fù亲qīn只zhǐ夹jiā摆bǎi在zài他tā前qián面miàn的de菜cài，阿ā丽lì赶gǎn紧jǐn把bǎ桌zhuō上shàng那nà盘pán煎jiān得dé香xiāng喷pēn喷pēn的de鱼yú推tuī过guò去qù。“爸bà爸bà，您nín最zuì爱ài吃chī的de。”“我wǒ最zuì爱ài吃chī的de？”父fù亲qīn诧chà异yì地dì看kàn着zhe她tā，“谁shuí告gào诉sù你nǐ的de？”
10"Alright." Her father picked only from the dishes in front of him. Ah Li quickly pushed the plate of fragrantly pan-fried fish closer to him. "Dad, your favourite." "My favourite?" Her father looked at her in surprise. "Who told you that?"
11 是shì啊a，谁shuí说shuō的de呢ne？
11Yes — who had said so?
12 从cóng前qián几jǐ角jiǎo钱qián一yī公gōng斤jīn的de小xiǎo鱼yú，如rú今jīn已yǐ经jīng涨zhǎng到dào一yī公gōng斤jīn三sān令lìng吉jí，虽suī然rán很hěn可kě口kǒu，但dàn是shì华huá人rén一yì般bān都dōu很hěn少shǎo购gòu买mǎi，认rèn为wéi那nà是shì贱jiàn价jià的de海hǎi鲜xiān。
12These small fish, which used to cost just a few cents a kilo in the old days, had now risen to three ringgit a kilo. They were tasty, but the Chinese community generally shunned them as cheap seafood.
13 阿ā丽lì记jì得de非fēi常cháng清qīng楚chǔ，二èr三sān十shí年nián前qián，在zài家jiā里lǐ，最zuì贵guì、最zuì好hǎo，令lìng全quán家jiā小xiǎo孩hái抢qiǎng着zhe吃chī的de，就jiù是shì这zhè种zhǒng小xiǎo鱼yú。她tā时shí常cháng因yīn为wèi争zhēng不bú到dào鱼yú而ér哭kū。每měi次cì看kàn着zhe兄xiōng弟dì姐jiě妹mèi碗wǎn里lǐ的de鱼yú，而ér她tā的de碗wǎn里lǐ却què一yī条tiáo都dōu没méi有yǒu时shí，她tā总zǒng是shì哽gěng咽yè着zhe说shuō不bù出chū话huà。父fù亲qīn嘴zuǐ上shàng骂mà她tā“你nǐ怎zěn么me那nà么me爱ài哭kū”，却què还hái是shì把bǎ自zì己jǐ碗wǎn里lǐ的de鱼yú夹jiā给gěi她tā。
13Ah Li remembered it clearly. Twenty or thirty years ago, back at home, the most expensive and prized dish — the one all the children scrambled to eat — was this same small fish. She often cried because she couldn't get any. Each time she saw her brothers' and sisters' bowls filled with fish while hers was empty, she'd choke up, unable to say a word. Her father would scold her — "Why are you always crying?" — but then pluck the fish from his own bowl and put it in hers.
14 长zhǎng大dà后hòu，每měi次cì父fù亲qīn一yī说shuō要yào来lái，阿ā丽lì就jiù一yí定dìng会huì买mǎi这zhè种zhǒng小xiǎo鱼yú。她tā要yào让ràng父fù亲qīn吃chī到dào她tā心xīn目mù中zhōng最zuì美měi味wèi的de鱼yú。
14When she grew up, every time her father said he was coming to visit, Ah Li would always buy these same little fish. She wanted her father to eat what was, in her heart, the most delicious fish in the world.
15 “我wǒ以yǐ为wéi……爸bà爸bà爱ài吃chī。”阿ā丽lì吞tūn吞tūn吐tǔ吐tǔ地dì说shuō：“我wǒ们men家jiā以yǐ前qián常cháng吃chī的de，爸bà爸bà忘wàng记jì了le吗ma？我wǒ……我wǒ从cóng小xiǎo就jiù很hěn爱ài吃chī。”
15"I thought… Dad liked them," Ah Li said hesitantly. "We used to have them all the time at home — have you forgotten, Dad? I… I've loved them since I was little."
16 “原yuán来lái……”父fù亲qīn的de眼yǎn眶kuàng湿shī润rùn了le。
16"So that's what it was…" Her father's eyes grew wet.
17 他tā每měi回huí看kàn到dào阿ā丽lì饭fàn桌zhuō上shàng的de小xiǎo鱼yú，就jiù有yǒu些xiē生shēng气qì，觉jué得de女nǚ儿ér对duì远yuǎn道dào而ér来lái的de父fù亲qīn太tài小xiǎo气qì。每měi当dāng他tā要yào去qù探tàn望wàng女nǚ儿ér，朋péng友yǒu们men都dū会huì羡xiàn慕mù地dì说shuō：“斗dòu湖hú的de海hǎi鲜xiān是shì全quán马mǎ最zuì便pián宜yi、最zuì好hǎo吃chī的de，你nǐ的de女nǚ儿ér一yí定dìng大dà鱼yú大dà虾xiā招zhāo待dài你nǐ。”他tā当dāng时shí听tīng了le，心xīn里lǐ很hěn不bú是shì滋zī味wèi。
17Every time he saw these little fish on Ah Li's table, he had felt a small sting of anger, thinking his daughter was being stingy with a father who had travelled all this way. Whenever he told friends he was going to visit her, they would say enviously: "Tawau seafood is the cheapest and best in all of Malaysia — your daughter must be treating you to big fish and big prawns." Hearing that, he had always felt a pang of bitterness inside.
18 父fù亲qīn小xiǎo口kǒu小xiǎo口kǒu地dì咀jǔ嚼jué着zhe小xiǎo鱼yú，一yī再zài重chóng复fù“是shì的de，很hěn好hǎo吃chī，很hěn香xiāng，很hěn好hǎo吃chī。”然rán后hòu他tā各gè夹jiā一yī条tiáo放fàng在zài他tā们men碗wǎn里lǐ，“志zhì明míng，快kuài吃chī呀ya……阿ā丽lì，你nǐ也yě吃chī呀ya。”
18Her father chewed the little fish in small, careful mouthfuls, repeating softly: "Yes, very tasty. So fragrant. Very tasty." Then he put one in each of their bowls. "Zhiming, eat up… Ah Li, you eat some too."
19 似sì乎hū连lián阿ā丽lì不bù多duō生shēng几jǐ个gè孩hái子zi的de事shì，他tā也yě谅liàng解jiě了le。
19And it seemed that, at last, even Ah Li's decision long ago not to have more children — he could forgive.
