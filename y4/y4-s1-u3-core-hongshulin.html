1 在zài热rè带dài和hé亚yà热rè带dài地dì区qū的de海hǎi岸àn和hé河hé口kǒu，经jīng常cháng可kě以yǐ见jiàn到dào茂mào密mì的de红hóng树shù林lín。这zhè些xiē树shù木mù看kàn上shàng去qù貌mào不bù惊jīng人rén，但dàn其qí实shí它tā们men在zài恶è劣liè的de环huán境jìng中zhōng练liàn就jiù了le一yī身shēn本běn事shì。它tā们men的de根gēn经jīng常cháng浸jìn泡pào在zài高gāo盐yán度dù的de海hǎi水shuǐ中zhōng，面miàn对duì海hǎi浪làng和hé潮cháo水shuǐ的de冲chōng击jī，毫háo不bù惧jù怕pà。
1Along the coasts and river mouths of the tropics and subtropics, you'll often see dense mangrove forests. These trees aren't much to look at, but in fact they've honed an extraordinary set of skills under brutal conditions. Their roots are constantly submerged in high-salinity seawater, and they meet the pounding of waves and tides without fear.
2 过guò去qù，人rén们men对duì红hóng树shù林lín的de重zhòng要yào性xìng缺quē乏fá了liǎo解jiě。在zài开kāi发fā土tǔ地dì、发fā展zhǎn经jīng济jì的de过guò程chéng中zhōng，不bù少shǎo红hóng树shù林lín成chéng为wéi牺xī牲shēng品pǐn。1819年nián，新xīn加jiā坡pō有yǒu大dà约yuē75平píng方fāng公gōng里lǐ的de红hóng树shù林lín。到dào了le1993年nián，红hóng树shù林lín的de面miàn积jī锐ruì减jiǎn到dào不bù足zú5平píng方fāng公gōng里lǐ。近jìn年nián来lái，随suí着zhe环huán保bǎo意yì识shí的de增zēng强qiáng，人rén们men逐zhú渐jiàn认rèn识shí到dào红hóng树shù林lín的de重zhòng要yào。有yǒu关guān部bù门mén积jī极jí开kāi展zhǎn保bǎo育yù工gōng作zuò，红hóng树shù林lín的de面miàn积jī也yě因yīn此cǐ稳wěn步bù增zēng加jiā。
2In the past, people didn't fully grasp how important mangroves were. In the process of developing land and growing the economy, many mangroves were sacrificed. In 1819, Singapore had roughly 75 square kilometres of mangroves. By 1993, that area had plummeted to less than 5 square kilometres. In recent years, as environmental awareness has grown, people have gradually come to recognise the value of mangroves. The relevant authorities have been actively pursuing conservation efforts, and the area of mangroves has been steadily growing again.
3 或huò许xǔ有yǒu人rén会huì说shuō，红hóng树shù林lín无wú法fǎ为wéi人rén类lèi提tí供gōng木mù材cái，没méi什shén么me用yòng处chǔ。如rú果guǒ你nǐ也yě这zhè么me认rèn为wéi，那nà就jiù大dà错cuò特tè错cuò了le。其qí实shí，红hóng树shù林lín有yǒu着zhe极jí为wéi重zhòng大dà的de作zuò用yòng。它tā被bèi称chēng为wéi对duì抗kàng气qì候hòu变biàn化huà的de“超chāo级jí英yīng雄xióng”，为wèi减jiǎn少shǎo大dà气qì中zhōng的de二èr氧yǎng化huà碳tàn和hé保bǎo护hù海hǎi岸àn线xiàn提tí供gōng了le一yí个gè很hěn好hǎo的de解jiě决jué方fāng案àn。
3Some might say that mangroves don't provide timber for human use, so they're not particularly useful. If that's what you think, you couldn't be more wrong. Mangroves play a hugely significant role. They've been called "superheroes" in the fight against climate change — offering an excellent solution for both reducing atmospheric carbon dioxide and protecting coastlines.
4 现xiàn今jīn世shì界jiè正zhèng面miàn对duì气qì候hòu暖nuǎn化huà问wèn题tí。这zhè主zhǔ要yào是shì由yóu于yú人rén类lèi大dà量liàng使shǐ用yòng煤méi炭tàn、石shí油yóu等děng化huà石shí燃rán料liào，并bìng因yīn此cǐ排pái放fàng出chū大dà量liàng的de二èr氧yǎng化huà碳tàn所suǒ导dǎo致zhì的de。红hóng树shù林lín能néng够gòu吸xī收shōu大dà气qì中zhōng的de二èr氧yǎng化huà碳tàn，并bìng把bǎ它tā储chǔ存cún起qǐ来lái。这zhè些xiē树shù木mù的de根gēn生shēng长zhǎng在zài海hǎi边biān的de泥ní地dì中zhōng，能néng够gòu把bǎ吸xī收shōu的de碳tàn锁suǒ定dìng在zài泥ní地dì土tǔ壤rǎng里lǐ长zhǎng达dá数shù千qiān年nián之zhī久jiǔ。红hóng树shù林lín储chǔ存cún碳tàn的de能néng力lì十shí分fēn强qiáng大dà，它tā每měi公gōng顷qǐng的de碳tàn储chǔ存cún能néng力lì比bǐ热rè带dài雨yǔ林lín高gāo出chū三sān至zhì五wǔ倍bèi。目mù前qián，我wǒ国guó的de红hóng树shù林lín约yuē占zhàn国guó土tǔ总zǒng面miàn积jī的de百bǎi分fēn之zhī一yī。虽suī然rán面miàn积jī不bù大dà，但dàn这zhè些xiē红hóng树shù林lín竟jìng然rán储chǔ存cún了le约yuē45万wàn吨dūn的de碳tàn，相xiāng当dāng于yú62万wàn名míng国guó人rén一yì年nián的de碳tàn排pái放fàng量liàng。从cóng这zhè个ge意yì义yì上shàng说shuō，红hóng树shù林lín在zài对duì抗kàng气qì候hòu变biàn化huà方fāng面miàn做zuò出chū了le巨jù大dà的de贡gòng献xiàn。
4The world today is facing a problem of climate warming. This is mainly caused by humanity's heavy use of fossil fuels like coal and oil, which release vast amounts of carbon dioxide. Mangroves can absorb atmospheric carbon dioxide and lock it away. The roots of these trees grow in coastal mud, and can keep the carbon they absorb locked in that mud for thousands of years. The carbon-storage capacity of mangroves is enormous — three to five times that of a tropical rainforest per hectare. Currently, mangroves cover only about 1% of Singapore's total land area. Small as that area is, these mangroves store roughly 450,000 tonnes of carbon — equivalent to a year's carbon emissions from 620,000 Singaporeans. In that sense, mangroves are making a huge contribution to fighting climate change.
5 除chú了le能néng吸xī收shōu大dà量liàng的de碳tàn，看kàn上shàng去qù身shēn躯qū矮ǎi小xiǎo的de红hóng树shù林lín还hái有yǒu助zhù于yú抵dǐ挡dǎng海hǎi潮cháo和hé风fēng暴bào对duì海hǎi岸àn线xiàn的de侵qīn蚀shí。当dāng海hǎi浪làng冲chōng击jī红hóng树shù林lín的de根gēn部bù时shí，那nà些xiē缠chán结jié在zài一yì起qǐ的de树shù根gēn会huì把bǎ波bō浪làng的de能néng量liàng消xiāo耗hào掉diào。波bō浪làng从cóng这zhè些xiē树shù根gēn的de空kòng隙xì中zhōng穿chuān过guò时shí，力lì道dào会huì减jiǎn弱ruò很hěn多duō，因yīn此cǐ可kě以yǐ减jiǎn轻qīng对duì海hǎi岸àn的de侵qīn蚀shí。如rú果guǒ海hǎi岸àn线xiàn上shàng有yǒu大dà片piàn的de红hóng树shù林lín，就jiù会huì形xíng成chéng一yí道dào天tiān然rán的de屏píng障zhàng，有yǒu效xiào抵dǐ抗kàng潮cháo水shuǐ和hé海hǎi浪làng的de冲chōng击jī。如rú果guǒ没méi有yǒu红hóng树shù林lín，就jiù需xū要yào费fèi时shí费fèi力lì建jiàn造zào防fáng波bō堤dī，其qí成chéng本běn相xiàng当dāng于yú培péi育yù红hóng树shù林lín的de1000倍bèi。因yīn此cǐ，在zài保bǎo护hù海hǎi岸àn方fāng面miàn，种zhòng植zhí红hóng树shù林lín堪kān称chēng是shì一yí个gè既jì有yǒu效xiào又yòu经jīng济jì的de选xuǎn择zé。今jīn天tiān，全quán世shì界jiè正zhèng面miàn对duì气qì候hòu暖nuǎn化huà、海hǎi平píng面miàn上shàng升shēng等děng严yán峻jùn的de挑tiǎo战zhàn。在zài这zhè种zhǒng情qíng况kuàng下xià，像xiàng新xīn加jiā坡pō这zhè样yàng的de岛dǎo国guó，尤yóu其qí需xū要yào大dà片piàn的de红hóng树shù林lín来lái保bǎo护hù自zì己jǐ的de海hǎi岸àn线xiàn。
5Beyond absorbing huge amounts of carbon, these short-statured mangroves also help defend coastlines against the erosion caused by tides and storms. When waves hit the roots, those tangled roots dissipate the wave's energy. By the time water has passed through the gaps between the roots, much of its force has been lost — and so the erosion of the coast is greatly reduced. With a wide stretch of mangroves along a coastline, you have a natural barrier that effectively withstands the pounding of waves and tides. Without mangroves, expensive and time-consuming seawalls have to be built — at roughly 1,000 times the cost of cultivating mangroves. So in coastal protection, planting mangroves can truly be called both effective and economical. The world today faces grave challenges including climate warming and rising sea levels. In a situation like this, an island nation like Singapore especially needs large stretches of mangroves to protect its coastlines.
6 为wèi了le更gèng好hǎo地dì对duì抗kàng气qì候hòu变biàn化huà，我wǒ们men需xū要yào好hǎo好hǎo呵hē护hù红hóng树shù林lín，让ràng这zhè个ge“超chāo级jí英yīng雄xióng”做zuò出chū更gèng大dà的de贡gòng献xiàn。
6To better fight climate change, we need to take real care of our mangroves — and let this "superhero" make an even greater contribution.
