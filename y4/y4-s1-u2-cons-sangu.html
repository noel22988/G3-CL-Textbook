《三顾茅庐》 Three Visits to the Thatched Cottage

1 三sān顾gù茅máo庐lú的de故gù事shì发fā生shēng在zài东dōng汉hàn末mò年nián。当dāng时shí，群qún雄xióng争zhēng霸bà，天tiān下xià大dà乱luàn。北běi方fāng的de曹cáo操cāo和hé江jiāng东dōng的de孙sūn权quán势shì力lì都dōu很hěn强qiáng大dà，相xiāng比bǐ之zhī下xià，刘liú备bèi在zài许xǔ多duō方fāng面miàn都dōu处chǔ于yú劣liè势shì。刘liú备bèi听tīng说shuō诸zhū葛gě亮liàng足zú智zhì多duō谋móu，决jué定dìng请qǐng他tā出chū来lái帮bāng助zhù自zì己jǐ。
1The story of "Three Visits to the Thatched Cottage" took place at the end of the Eastern Han dynasty. At that time, warlords contended for supremacy and the realm was in chaos. To the north, Cao Cao was powerful; in the southeast, Sun Quan held strong. By comparison, Liu Bei was at a disadvantage in many respects. Hearing that Zhuge Liang was a man of great wisdom and resourcefulness, Liu Bei resolved to invite him out of seclusion to lend him his aid.
2 刘liú备bèi带dài着zhe礼lǐ物wù，与yǔ关guān羽yǔ、张zhāng飞fēi一yì起qǐ前qián往wǎng诸zhū葛gě亮liàng居jū住zhù的de地dì方fāng——隆lóng中zhōng。一yī路lù上shàng，群qún山shān起qǐ伏fú，林lín木mù青qīng翠cuì，风fēng景jǐng十shí分fēn秀xiù丽lì。他tā们men来lái到dào一yī间jiān茅máo庐lú前qián，下xià了le马mǎ，刘liú备bèi亲qīn自zì敲qiāo门mén。门mén开kāi了le，出chū来lái一yí个gè童tóng子zi。刘liú备bèi表biǎo明míng来lái意yì，童tóng子zi答dá道dào：“先xiān生shēng一yī早zǎo就jiù出chū门mén了le。”刘liú备bèi问wèn诸zhū葛gě亮liàng去qù了le哪nǎ里lǐ，什shén么me时shí候hòu回huí来lái，童tóng子zi一yī概gài不bù知zhī道dào。刘liú备bèi深shēn感gǎn惆chóu怅chàng，心xīn直zhí口kǒu快kuài的de张zhāng飞fēi却què说shuō：”既jì然rán不bù在zài家jiā，干gān脆cuì回huí去qù算suàn了le。”刘liú备bèi无wú奈nài，只zhǐ好hǎo上shàng马mǎ返fǎn回huí。
2Bearing gifts, Liu Bei set out with Guan Yu and Zhang Fei for Longzhong, where Zhuge Liang lived. Along the way, mountains rose and fell, the forests were a deep green — the scenery was lovely indeed. They came to a thatched cottage, dismounted, and Liu Bei personally knocked on the door. A boy servant opened it. Liu Bei explained his purpose, and the boy answered: "The master left early in the morning." Liu Bei asked where he had gone and when he would return, but the boy did not know. Liu Bei was disappointed. Plain-spoken Zhang Fei said: "Since he's not home, we may as well just turn back." With no other choice, Liu Bei mounted his horse and headed home.
3 刘liú备bèi天tiān天tiān派pài人rén打dǎ听tīng诸zhū葛gě亮liàng的de消xiāo息xī。过guò了le一yī段duàn日rì子zi，他tā听tīng说shuō诸zhū葛gě亮liàng回huí来lái了le，便biàn立lì即jí叫jiào人rén备bèi马mǎ。张zhāng飞fēi很hěn不bù情qíng愿yuàn地dì说shuō：“这zhè个ge人rén不bù过guò是shì个gè村cūn夫fū，何hé必bì您nín亲qīn自zì去qù请qǐng呢ne？派pài人rén叫jiào他tā过guò来lái就jiù好hǎo了le。”刘liú备bèi斥chì责zé道dào：“诸zhū葛gě亮liàng是shì有yǒu才cái能néng的de人rén，怎zěn么me可kě以yǐ随suí便biàn派pài人rén去qù叫jiào他tā来lái呢ne？”说shuō完wán，他tā再zài次cì前qián往wǎng拜bài访fǎng诸zhū葛gě亮liàng，关guān羽yǔ和hé张zhāng飞fēi也yě只zhǐ好hǎo跟gēn着zhe一yì起qǐ去qù。时shí值zhí隆lóng冬dōng，天tiān气qì严yán寒hán。张zhāng飞fēi抱bào怨yuàn道dào：“天tiān寒hán地dì冻dòng的de，为wèi什shén么me跑pǎo这zhè么me远yuǎn去qù见jiàn这zhè个ge村cūn夫fū？我wǒ们men还hái是shì回huí去qù吧ba。”刘liú备bèi说shuō：“我wǒ冒mào着zhe严yán寒hán前qián去qù拜bài访fǎng，正zhèng是shì要yào他tā知zhī道dào我wǒ的de诚chéng意yì。你nǐ这zhè么me怕pà冷lěng，那nà你nǐ就jiù回huí去qù吧ba。”张zhāng飞fēi嚷rǎng道dào：“我wǒ死sǐ都dōu不bù怕pà，还hái会huì怕pà冷lěng吗ma？只zhǐ是shì担dān心xīn大dà哥gē这zhè次cì又yòu白bái跑pǎo一yī趟tàng。”刘liú备bèi回huí应yìng道dào：“不bù必bì多duō说shuō，跟gēn着zhe我wǒ去qù就jiù是shì。”
3Day after day, Liu Bei sent men to bring news of Zhuge Liang. After some time, he heard that Zhuge Liang had returned and immediately called for his horse to be readied. Zhang Fei said grudgingly: "He's just a country man — why must you go in person? Send someone to fetch him." Liu Bei reprimanded him sharply: "Zhuge Liang is a man of great talent. How could we send a messenger to summon him?" With that, he set off again to call on Zhuge Liang, and Guan Yu and Zhang Fei could only go with him. It was the depth of winter, and the weather was bitterly cold. Zhang Fei complained: "In this freezing weather, why go all that way to see some country man? Let's just turn back." Liu Bei said: "I'm braving the cold to call on him precisely so he knows my sincerity. If you find the cold so unbearable, then go back." Zhang Fei protested: "I don't fear death — would I fear cold? I'm only worried our brother will make the journey for nothing again." Liu Bei replied: "No more talk — just follow."
4 三sān人rén来lái到dào茅máo庐lú，见jiàn一yí个gè青qīng年nián正zhèng在zài读dú书shū。刘liú备bèi大dà喜xǐ，忙máng上shàng前qián施shī礼lǐ道dào：“今jīn天tiān总zǒng算suàn见jiàn到dào先xiān生shēng了le！”那nà青qīng年nián急jí忙máng还huán礼lǐ道dào：“我wǒ是shì诸zhū葛gě亮liàng的de弟dì弟di。真zhēn不bù巧qiǎo，我wǒ哥gē哥gē和hé朋péng友yǒu一yì起qǐ出chū外wài游yóu玩wán去qù了le。”刘liú备bèi失shī望wàng地dì摇yáo了le摇yáo头tóu，留liú了le一yī封fēng信xìn给gěi诸zhū葛gě亮liàng，便biàn起qǐ身shēn告gào辞cí了le。
4The three reached the cottage, and saw a young man reading. Liu Bei was overjoyed and quickly stepped forward with a bow: "At last, today I shall meet the master!" The young man hurriedly returned the bow: "I am Zhuge Liang's younger brother. Most unfortunate — my brother has gone out with a friend." Liu Bei shook his head in disappointment, left a letter for Zhuge Liang, and took his leave.
5 冬dōng去qù春chūn来lái，刘liú备bèi决jué定dìng再zài去qù拜bài访fǎng诸zhū葛gě亮liàng。关guān羽yǔ和hé张zhāng飞fēi听tīng说shuō后hòu，一yì起qǐ赶gǎn来lái劝quàn阻zǔ。张zhāng飞fēi大dà声shēng说shuō道dào：“这zhè个ge村cūn夫fū，有yǒu什shén么me了liǎo不bù起qǐ！您nín不bù用yòng再zài去qù了le，这zhè次cì我wǒ去qù。如rú果guǒ他tā不bù愿yuàn来lái，我wǒ就jiù用yòng麻má绳shéng把bǎ他tā绑bǎng来lái！”刘liú备bèi很hěn生shēng气qì，训xùn斥chì道dào：“你nǐ怎zěn么me可kě以yǐ如rú此cǐ无wú礼lǐ！这zhè次cì你nǐ不bù用yòng去qù了le。”张zhāng飞fēi说shuō：“两liǎng位wèi哥gē哥gē都dōu去qù，我wǒ当dāng然rán要yào跟gēn着zhe了le。”刘liú备bèi说shuō：“如rú果guǒ你nǐ要yào去qù，千qiān万wàn不bù可kě失shī礼lǐ！”张zhāng飞fēi低dī着zhe头tóu，连lián声shēng答dā应yìng。
5Winter passed and spring came, and Liu Bei resolved to call on Zhuge Liang again. When Guan Yu and Zhang Fei heard, they hurried to dissuade him. Zhang Fei said loudly: "This country man — what's so special about him? You needn't go again. This time I'll go. If he won't come, I'll tie him with hemp rope and drag him here!" Liu Bei was furious and rebuked him: "How can you speak so rudely? You needn't come this time." Zhang Fei said: "If both my brothers are going, of course I have to follow." Liu Bei said: "If you must come, you absolutely must not be discourteous!" Zhang Fei lowered his head and agreed, again and again.
6 三sān人rén到dào了le后hòu，刘liú备bèi请qǐng童tóng子zi通tōng报bào，童tóng子zi却què说shuō诸zhū葛gě亮liàng正zhèng在zài草cǎo堂táng午wǔ睡shuì。刘liú备bèi赶gǎn紧jǐn说shuō：“既jì然rán如rú此cǐ，别bié叫jiào醒xǐng先xiān生shēng。我wǒ们men可kě以yǐ等děng他tā醒xǐng来lái。”他tā让ràng关guān羽yǔ和hé张zhāng飞fēi在zài大dà门mén外wài等děng着zhe，自zì己jǐ则zé恭gōng敬jìng地dì站zhàn在zài草cǎo堂táng外wài面miàn的de台tái阶jiē下xià等děng候hòu。等děng了le许xǔ久jiǔ，诸zhū葛gě亮liàng还hái没méi有yǒu醒xǐng。张zhāng飞fēi大dà怒nù，高gāo声shēng对duì关guān羽yǔ说shuō：“这zhè个ge村cūn夫fū，太tài傲ào慢màn了le！竟jìng然rán让ràng我wǒ们men站zhàn在zài这zhè里lǐ等děng了le这zhè么me久jiǔ！等děng我wǒ去qù屋wū后hòu放fàng一yī把bǎ火huǒ，看kàn他tā会huì不bú会huì起qǐ来lái！”关guān羽yǔ连lián忙máng阻zǔ止zhǐ他tā。这zhè时shí只zhǐ见jiàn诸zhū葛gě亮liàng翻fān了le一yí个gè身shēn，面miàn朝cháo墙qiáng壁bì，又yòu睡shuì着zháo了le。童tóng子zi要yào进jìn去qù通tōng报bào，刘liú备bèi却què说shuō：“不bú要yào惊jīng动dòng先xiān生shēng。”又yòu过guò了le好hǎo一yí阵zhèn子zi，诸zhū葛gě亮liàng醒xǐng了le。听tīng说shuō刘liú备bèi已yǐ经jīng等děng候hòu多duō时shí，诸zhū葛gě亮liàng连lián忙máng把bǎ他tā请qǐng进jìn草cǎo堂táng，坐zuò下xià长cháng谈tán。
6When the three of them arrived, Liu Bei asked the boy to announce them, but the boy said that Zhuge Liang was taking his afternoon nap in the cottage. Liu Bei said quickly: "In that case, do not wake the master. We can wait until he is up." He had Guan Yu and Zhang Fei wait outside the gate, while he himself stood respectfully at the foot of the steps outside the cottage. A long time passed, and still Zhuge Liang did not wake. Zhang Fei was furious, and said loudly to Guan Yu: "This country fellow — how arrogant! Making us stand here this long! Let me go round the back of the house and start a fire — and we'll see if he doesn't get up then!" Guan Yu hurriedly restrained him. Just then, Zhuge Liang turned over to face the wall, and went back to sleep. The boy made to go in and announce them, but Liu Bei said: "Do not disturb the master." Another good while passed before Zhuge Liang woke. On hearing that Liu Bei had been waiting for so long, he hastily invited him into the cottage, where they sat down for a long talk.
7 畅chàng谈tán之zhī后hòu，刘liú备bèi发fā现xiàn诸zhū葛gě亮liàng果guǒ然rán博bó学xué多duō才cái，便biàn再zài三sān恳kěn请qǐng他tā出chū山shān相xiāng助zhù。诸zhū葛gě亮liàng为wèi刘liú备bèi的de诚chéng心xīn所suǒ打dǎ动dòng，决jué定dìng全quán力lì辅fǔ助zhù他tā打dǎ天tiān下xià。后hòu来lái，他tā成chéng为wéi刘liú备bèi的de军jūn师shī，为wèi蜀shǔ汉hàn王wáng朝cháo贡gòng献xiàn了le毕bì生shēng的de智zhì慧huì和hé才cái能néng。
7After their conversation, Liu Bei found that Zhuge Liang was truly a man of vast learning and great gifts. He earnestly entreated him, again and again, to come out of seclusion and aid him. Moved by Liu Bei's sincerity, Zhuge Liang resolved to help him win the realm with all his might. He later became Liu Bei's chief strategist, and devoted the wisdom and talents of his entire life to the cause of the Shu-Han dynasty.
