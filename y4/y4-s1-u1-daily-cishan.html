Foreword
About This Directory. In our society, there are always those who run into difficulties of one kind or another and need a helping hand. When it comes to supporting vulnerable groups, none of us can step aside — we all bear that responsibility. We welcome you to join the volunteer teams of the charities below, and work alongside us to share the burden of those who need help.
共gòng济jì社shè区qū
使shǐ命mìng关guān注zhù残cán障zhàng人rén士shì的de需xū要yào，为wèi他tā们men打dǎ造zào一yí个gè温wēn馨xīn的de家jiā园yuán。
提tí供gōng的de服fú务wù设shè立lì方fāng便biàn轮lún椅yǐ出chū入rù的de诊zhěn所suǒ和hé超chāo市shì。提tí供gōng职zhí业yè指zhǐ导dǎo、培péi训xùn、就jiù业yè配pèi对duì等děng服fú务wù。聘pìn请qǐng残cán障zhàng人rén士shì，让ràng他tā们men也yě享xiǎng有yǒu同tóng工gōng同tóng酬chóu的de权quán利lì。
Inclusive Community
Mission. To attend to the needs of persons with disabilities, and build them a warm and welcoming home.
What We Provide. We set up wheelchair-accessible clinics and supermarkets. We offer career guidance, training, and job-matching services. We hire persons with disabilities ourselves — so they too enjoy the right to equal pay for equal work.
客kè工gōng关guān怀huái中zhōng心xīn
使shǐ命mìng援yuán助zhù和hé关guān怀huái客kè工gōng，让ràng他tā们men获huò得dé应yīng有yǒu的de福fú利lì和hé待dài遇yù。
提tí供gōng的de服fú务wù为wèi遇yù到dào困kùn难nán的de客kè工gōng提tí供gōng他tā们men负fù担dān得dé起qǐ的de医yī疗liáo服fú务wù。关guān注zhù客kè工gōng的de心xīn理lǐ健jiàn康kāng，为wèi他tā们men提tí供gōng心xīn理lǐ辅fǔ导dǎo。当dāng客kè工gōng与yǔ雇gù主zhǔ发fā生shēng纠jiū纷fēn时shí，为wèi他tā们men提tí供gōng法fǎ律lǜ援yuán助zhù。
Migrant Worker Care Centre
Mission. To support and care for migrant workers, ensuring they receive the welfare and treatment they are entitled to.
What We Provide. We offer affordable medical services to migrant workers facing hardship. We pay attention to their mental health and offer counselling. When disputes arise between workers and their employers, we provide legal assistance.
善shàn心xīn小xiǎo铺pù
使shǐ命mìng为wèi低dī收shōu入rù群qún体tǐ提tí供gōng生shēng活huó所suǒ需xū，并bìng让ràng他tā们men有yǒu自zì由yóu选xuǎn择zé食shí物wù的de机jī会huì。
提tí供gōng的de服fú务wù为wèi受shòu助zhù者zhě提tí供gōng购gòu物wù卡kǎ。受shòu助zhù者zhě每měi个gè月yuè可kě以yǐ凭píng卡kǎ在zài善shàn心xīn小xiǎo铺pù的de各gè个gè分fēn店diàn领lǐng取qǔ日rì常cháng用yòng品pǐn。让ràng受shòu助zhù者zhě在zài指zhǐ定dìng的de小xiǎo贩fàn摊tān位wèi用yòng这zhè张zhāng卡kǎ购gòu买mǎi食shí物wù。
Goodheart Pantry
Mission. To provide daily necessities to low-income households, and to give them the freedom to choose their own food.
What We Provide. We give beneficiaries a shopping card. Each month, they can use it to collect daily essentials at any branch of Goodheart Pantry, and to buy food from designated hawker stalls.
慈cí安ān护hù理lǐ院yuàn
使shǐ命mìng为wèi临lín终zhōng病bìng患huàn提tí供gōng慈cí怀huái护hù理lǐ服fú务wù，让ràng他tā们men得dé到dào更gèng好hǎo的de呵hē护hù与yǔ关guān怀huái。
提tí供gōng的de服fú务wù为wèi病bìng患huàn提tí供gōng医yī疗liáo方fāng面miàn的de照zhào顾gù和hé情qíng感gǎn上shàng的de支zhī持chí。为wèi家jiā属shǔ提tí供gōng心xīn理lǐ辅fǔ导dǎo，缓huǎn解jiě他tā们men的de紧jǐn张zhāng与yǔ焦jiāo虑lǜ。
Comfort Hospice Care
Mission. To provide hospice care for patients in their final stage of life, so they receive the tenderest care and attention.
What We Provide. We offer medical care and emotional support to patients. We provide counselling to family members, easing the strain and anxiety they are under.
