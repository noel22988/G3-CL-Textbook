1 大dà自zì然rán有yǒu自zì己jǐ的de平píng衡héng之zhī道dào，但dàn这zhè种zhǒng平píng衡héng却què常cháng常cháng遭zāo到dào破pò坏huài。
1Nature has its own way of finding balance — but again and again, that balance is upset.
无wú心xīn之zhī举jǔ酿niàng成chéng大dà祸huò
An Innocent Act, a Massive Disaster
2 19世shì纪jì，随suí着zhe外wài来lái人rén口kǒu的de涌yǒng入rù，很hěn多duō动dòng植zhí物wù被bèi引yǐn入rù澳ào大dà利lì亚yà，兔tù子zi就jiù是shì其qí中zhōng之zhī一yī。由yóu于yú兔tù子zi繁fán殖zhí速sù度dù非fēi常cháng快kuài，加jiā上shàng环huán境jìng适shì宜yí，没méi有yǒu天tiān敌dí，到dào了le20世shì纪jì初chū，兔tù子zi的de数shù量liàng已yǐ超chāo过guò100亿yì。急jí速sù增zēng加jiā的de兔tù子zi给gěi草cǎo原yuán带dài来lái危wēi害hài，影yǐng响xiǎng了le牛niú羊yáng的de饲sì料liào来lái源yuán，也yě破pò坏huài了liǎo当dàng地dì的de生shēng态tài系xì统tǒng。为wèi了le控kòng制zhì兔tù子zi的de数shù量liàng，人rén们men采cǎi用yòng了le各gè种zhǒng办bàn法fǎ，甚shèn至zhì动dòng用yòng军jūn队duì进jìn行xíng捕bǔ杀shā。一yì直zhí到dào20世shì纪jì中zhōng期qī，情qíng况kuàng才cái有yǒu所suǒ好hǎo转zhuǎn。
2In the 19th century, as immigrants flooded into Australia, many animals and plants were brought in along with them — including rabbits. Rabbits breed extremely quickly, and with a favourable environment and no natural predators, by the early 20th century their numbers had passed 10 billion. The exploding rabbit population devastated the grasslands, eating into the feed supply for cattle and sheep, and disrupting the local ecosystem. People tried every method to control them, even calling in the military to cull them. It wasn't until the mid-20th century that the situation finally improved.
从cóng刻kè意yì引yǐn进jìn到dào加jiā以yǐ防fáng治zhì
From Deliberate Introduction to Active Control
3 原yuán产chǎn中zhōng美měi洲zhōu的de福fú寿shòu螺luó，在zài20世shì纪jì80年nián代dài作zuò为wéi食shí物wù引yǐn入rù中zhōng国guó，但dàn最zuì终zhōng因yīn不bù受shòu欢huān迎yíng而ér没méi有yǒu成chéng为wéi餐cān桌zhuō上shàng的de佳jiā肴yáo。令lìng人rén意yì想xiǎng不bù到dào的de是shì，由yóu于yú气qì候hòu条tiáo件jiàn适shì宜yí，繁fán殖zhí力lì惊jīng人rén的de福fú寿shòu螺luó急jí剧jù增zēng加jiā。它tā们men啃kěn食shí稻dào苗miáo，给gěi农nóng业yè生shēng产chǎn造zào成chéng严yán重zhòng的de危wēi害hài。谁shuí能néng想xiǎng到dào，看kàn似shì不bù起qǐ眼yǎn的de福fú寿shòu螺luó，最zuì后hòu竟jìng变biàn成chéng需xū要yào大dà力lì防fáng治zhì的de外wài来lái入rù侵qīn物wù种zhǒng。
3Apple snails, native to Central America, were introduced to China as a food source in the 1980s. They never caught on with diners and never became the prized delicacy people had hoped for. Unexpectedly, however, the climate suited them, and these prolific breeders multiplied at a startling rate. They began gnawing through rice seedlings, causing serious damage to farming. Who would have thought that the unassuming apple snail would end up an invasive species that has to be aggressively controlled?
受shòu到dào伤shāng害hài的de“地dì球qiú之zhī肺fèi”
The Wounded "Lungs of the Earth"
4 亚yà马mǎ逊xùn雨yǔ林lín，被bèi称chēng为wéi“地dì球qiú之zhī肺fèi”。这zhè片piàn生shēng机jī盎àng然rán的de雨yǔ林lín，每měi年nián可kě以yǐ吸xī收shōu10亿yì吨dūn二èr氧yǎng化huà碳tàn。然rán而ér，由yóu于yú过guò度dù开kāi发fā、大dà肆sì砍kǎn伐fá等děng原yuán因yīn，这zhè片piàn雨yǔ林lín遭zāo受shòu了le严yán重zhòng的de破pò坏huài。大dà片piàn林lín地dì变biàn成chéng农nóng田tián，大dà量liàng珍zhēn稀xī的de树shù木mù消xiāo失shī不bú见jiàn。如rú果guǒ亚yà马mǎ逊xùn雨yǔ林lín继jì续xù遭zāo到dào破pò坏huài，很hěn多duō动dòng植zhí物wù会huì失shī去qù栖qī息xī地dì，人rén类lèi也yě会huì自zì食shí其qí果guǒ。
4The Amazon rainforest is known as "the lungs of the earth." Brimming with life, this rainforest can absorb a billion tonnes of carbon dioxide every year. But over-exploitation, large-scale logging, and other forces have inflicted serious damage. Huge tracts of forest have been turned into farmland; vast numbers of rare trees have vanished. If the destruction of the Amazon continues, countless animals and plants will lose their habitats — and humanity, too, will reap what it has sown.
反fǎn思sī
Reflection
5 不bù管guǎn是shì有yǒu心xīn的de还hái是shì无wú意yì的de，人rén类lèi的de行xíng为wéi往wǎng往wǎng会huì对duì自zì然rán生shēng态tài造zào成chéng难nán以yǐ预yù料liào的de后hòu果guǒ。我wǒ们men一yí定dìng要yào牢láo记jì教jiào训xùn，切qiè勿wù破pò坏huài生shēng态tài的de平píng衡héng，以yǐ免miǎn受shòu到dào大dà自zì然rán的de惩chéng罚fá。
5Whether deliberate or accidental, human actions often produce ecological consequences we can't foresee. We must keep these lessons firmly in mind. Do not break nature's balance — lest nature itself punish us in return.
