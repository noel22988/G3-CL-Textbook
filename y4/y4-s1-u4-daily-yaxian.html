1 上shàng世shì纪jì60年nián代dài，东dōng南nán亚yà的de一yì些xiē国guó家jiā为wèi了le促cù进jìn区qū域yù的de合hé作zuò与yǔ稳wěn定dìng，成chéng立lì了le东dōng南nán亚yà国guó家jiā联lián盟méng，即jí“亚yà细xì安ān”，又yòu称chēng为wéi“东dōng盟méng”。
1In the 1960s, several Southeast Asian nations came together to strengthen regional cooperation and stability, forming the Association of Southeast Asian Nations — known as ASEAN.
概gài况kuàng
Overview
2 迄qì今jīn为wéi止zhǐ，亚yà细xì安ān共gòng有yǒu10个gè国guó家jiā，总zǒng人rén口kǒu超chāo过guò6亿yì。这zhè10个gè国guó家jiā是shì：文wén莱lái、柬jiǎn埔pǔ寨zhài、印yìn度dù尼ní西xī亚yà、老lǎo挝wō、马mǎ来lái西xī亚yà、缅miǎn甸diān、菲fēi律lǜ宾bīn、新xīn加jiā坡pō、泰tài国guó、越yuè南nán。
2To date, ASEAN has 10 member countries with a combined population of over 600 million. These 10 nations are: Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, and Vietnam.
丰fēng富fù而ér多duō样yàng的de物wù种zhǒng
Rich and Diverse Wildlife
3 亚yà细xì安ān各gè国guó土tǔ地dì面miàn积jī的de总zǒng和hé只zhǐ占zhàn全quán球qiú的de3%，却què拥yōng有yǒu全quán球qiú约yuē20%的de动dòng植zhí物wù品pǐn种zhǒng。全quán球qiú物wù种zhǒng极jí为wéi丰fēng富fù多duō样yàng的de国guó家jiā一yí共gòng有yǒu17个gè，其qí中zhōng3个gè就jiù在zài东dōng南nán亚yà，分fēn别bié是shì印yìn度dù尼ní西xī亚yà、马mǎ来lái西xī亚yà和hé菲fēi律lǜ宾bīn。亚yà细xì安ān各gè国guó大dà多duō有yǒu着zhe蜿wān蜒yán曲qū折zhé的de海hǎi岸àn线xiàn，周zhōu边biān有yǒu辽liáo阔kuò的de海hǎi洋yáng。海hǎi中zhōng珊shān瑚hú礁jiāo的de面miàn积jī超chāo过guò全quán球qiú的de四sì分fēn之zhī一yī。
3ASEAN nations together cover just 3% of the world's land area — yet they are home to about 20% of the planet's animal and plant species. Of the 17 "megadiverse" countries on Earth, three are in Southeast Asia: Indonesia, Malaysia, and the Philippines. Most ASEAN nations have winding coastlines bordered by vast seas. Their coral reefs make up more than a quarter of the world's total.
多duō元yuán化huà的de宗zōng教jiào信xìn仰yǎng
Diversity of Religions
4 印yìn度dù尼ní西xī亚yà是shì全quán球qiú回huí教jiào徒tú最zuì多duō的de国guó家jiā；菲fēi律lǜ宾bīn有yǒu超chāo过guò八bā成chéng的de人rén是shì天tiān主zhǔ教jiào徒tú；泰tài国guó、柬jiǎn埔pǔ寨zhài等děng国guó的de国guó民mín大dà部bù分fèn是shì佛fó教jiào徒tú。亚yà细xì安ān国guó家jiā虽suī然rán在zài宗zōng教jiào信xìn仰yǎng方fāng面miàn有yǒu诸zhū多duō不bù同tóng，但dàn各gè国guó互hù相xiāng包bāo容róng，共gòng同tóng维wéi护hù区qū域yù的de和hé平píng与yǔ稳wěn定dìng。
4Indonesia has the largest Muslim population in the world. Over 80% of Filipinos are Catholic. Most citizens of Thailand and Cambodia are Buddhist. ASEAN countries differ greatly in matters of faith, yet the member states extend tolerance to one another, working together to maintain peace and stability across the region.

1. 吴wú哥gē窟kū
1. Angkor Wat
6 吴wú哥gē窟kū是shì世shì界jiè闻wén名míng的de古gǔ迹jì。它tā那nà壮zhuàng观guān的de建jiàn筑zhù和hé精jīng致zhì的de雕diāo刻kè令lìng人rén啧zé啧zé称chēng奇qí，赞zàn叹tàn不bù已yǐ。吴wú哥gē窟kū拥yōng有yǒu深shēn厚hòu的de文wén化huà底dǐ蕴yùn，也yě让ràng我wǒ们men看kàn到dào了le柬jiǎn埔pǔ寨zhài古gǔ代dài文wén明míng灿càn烂làn辉huī煌huáng的de一yí面miàn。
6Angkor Wat is one of the world's most famous historic sites. Its grand architecture and exquisite carvings draw gasps of wonder and admiration. With its profound cultural depth, Angkor Wat shows us the resplendent side of ancient Cambodian civilisation.
2. 峇bā厘lí岛dǎo
2. Bali
7 印yìn度dù尼ní西xī亚yà有yǒu“千qiān岛dǎo之zhī国guó”的de美měi誉yù，峇bā厘lí岛dǎo就jiù是shì其qí中zhōng一yī个gè岛dǎo屿yǔ。岛dǎo上shàng有yǒu高gāo耸sǒng的de火huǒ山shān和hé迷mí人rén的de沙shā滩tān，还hái有yǒu极jí富fù特tè色sè的de地dì方fāng文wén化huà。每měi年nián都dōu有yǒu大dà量liàng的de国guó际jì游yóu客kè来lái峇bā厘lí岛dǎo观guān光guāng度dù假jià。
7Indonesia has earned the lovely epithet "land of a thousand islands," and Bali is one of them. The island has towering volcanoes, captivating beaches, and a richly distinctive local culture. Every year, vast numbers of international visitors come to Bali to sightsee and relax.
3. 努nǔ鲁lǔ伊yī曼màn皇huáng宫gōng
3. Istana Nurul Iman
8 文wén莱lái的de努nǔ鲁lǔ伊yī曼màn皇huáng宫gōng，规guī模mó比bǐ英yīng国guó的de白bái金jīn汉hàn宫gōng大dà三sān倍bèi，里lǐ面miàn有yǒu一yī千qiān多duō个gè房fáng间jiān。整zhěng座zuò皇huáng宫gōng金jīn碧bì辉huī煌huáng，绚xuàn丽lì夺duó目mù。它tā既jì是shì文wén莱lái苏sū丹dān的de住zhù处chù，也yě是shì文wén莱lái政zhèng府fǔ机jī构gòu的de所suǒ在zài地dì。
8Brunei's Istana Nurul Iman is three times the size of Buckingham Palace in the United Kingdom, with over a thousand rooms inside. The whole palace is splendid in gold and jade, breathtakingly brilliant to look upon. It is both the Sultan of Brunei's residence and the seat of the Bruneian government.
