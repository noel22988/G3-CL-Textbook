1 新xīn加jiā坡pō人rén的de友yǒu善shàn，让ràng我wǒ感gǎn动dòng。爱ài上shàng这zhè个ge城chéng市shì，是shì因yīn为wèi她tā的de质zhì朴piáo。虽suī然rán是shì大dà都dū市shì，很hěn多duō人rén却què非fēi常cháng清qīng纯chún、善shàn良liáng、热rè情qíng。
1The friendliness of Singaporeans moves me. I came to love this city because of its plain honesty. It may be a big city, but so many people here are genuinely innocent, kind, and warm-hearted.
2 我wǒ刚gāng到dào新xīn加jiā坡pō时shí人rén生shēng地dì不bù熟shú，经jīng常cháng问wèn路lù，每měi次cì被bèi问wèn的de人rén都dōu恨hèn不bù得dé亲qīn自zì带dài我wǒ去qù，也yě真zhēn有yǒu送sòng我wǒ到dào目mù的dì地dì的de。孤gū身shēn在zài外wài，一yī有yǒu困kùn难nán，不bù用yòng张zhāng口kǒu就jiù会huì有yǒu人rén主zhǔ动dòng上shàng前qián来lái帮bāng忙máng。下xià雨yǔ的de时shí候hòu，我wǒ没méi带dài伞sǎn，几jǐ次cì有yǒu人rén邀yāo请qǐng我wǒ共gòng打dǎ一yī把bǎ，伞sǎn不bù大dà，分fēn享xiǎng的de却què是shì温wēn暖nuǎn。
2When I first arrived in Singapore, I knew no one and nothing about the place, and was always asking for directions. Every time, the person I asked seemed almost desperate to take me there themselves — and some really did walk me all the way. Alone in a foreign place, the moment I had any difficulty, before I'd even said anything, someone would come over to help. When it rained and I had no umbrella, more than once someone invited me to share theirs. The umbrella wasn't big, but what was being shared was warmth.
3 有yǒu一yì天tiān下xià午wǔ去qù逛guàng街jiē，大dà概gài的de方fāng位wèi我wǒ是shì知zhī道dào的de，只zhǐ是shì不bù晓xiǎo得de到dào底dǐ在zài哪nǎ一yī站zhàn下xià车chē比bǐ较jiào近jìn。到dào了le那nà一yí带dài，我wǒ感gǎn觉jué自zì己jǐ好hǎo像xiàng坐zuò过guò了le站zhàn。于yú是shì，我wǒ就jiù在zài巴bā士shì停tíng下xià来lái时shí跑pǎo上shàng前qián去qù问wèn司sī机jī。司sī机jī很hěn热rè心xīn地dì给gěi我wǒ指zhǐ路lù，说shuō如rú果guǒ下xià一yí个gè车chē站zhàn下xià车chē，就jiù得dé走zǒu更gèng远yuǎn的de路lù。他tā还hái特tè地dì打dǎ开kāi车chē门mén让ràng我wǒ下xià车chē，并bìng反fǎn复fù指zhǐ给gěi我wǒ看kàn。我wǒ下xià了le车chē，看kàn见jiàn他tā仍réng然rán隔gé着zhe玻bō璃lí为wèi我wǒ指zhǐ方fāng向xiàng。蓦mò然rán间jiān，一yī股gǔ暖nuǎn流liú在zài我wǒ心xīn中zhōng流liú淌tǎng。
3One afternoon, I was going shopping. I roughly knew the area, but wasn't sure which stop to get off at to be closest. When I got near, I felt I might have already gone past. So when the bus stopped, I ran up and asked the driver. He was warm and helpful, telling me that if I got off at the next stop, I'd have a much longer walk. He even specially opened the door to let me off there, and showed me the direction over and over. After I'd alighted, I looked back and saw him, still pointing through the glass to show me which way to go. All of a sudden, a warm current flowed through my heart.
4 我wǒ常cháng去qù看kàn的de中zhōng医yī师shī，刚gāng跟gēn她tā认rèn识shí的de时shí候hòu，曾céng经jīng为wèi收shōu费fèi的de事shì发fā生shēng过guò误wù会huì：我wǒ发fā现xiàn她tā每měi次cì都dōu按àn定dìng价jià收shōu我wǒ22块kuài诊zhěn费fèi，有yǒu些xiē人rén她tā却què只zhǐ收shōu8块kuài、10块kuài。我wǒ问wèn她tā为wèi什shén么me会huì有yǒu这zhè样yàng的de不bù同tóng。她tā就jiù像xiàng老lǎo朋péng友yǒu一yí样yàng，推tuī心xīn置zhì腹fù地dì告gào诉sù我wǒ：“我wǒ看kàn病bìng时shí会huì根gēn据jù病bìng人rén的de经jīng济jì状zhuàng况kuàng收shōu钱qián。附fù近jìn的de居jū民mín有yǒu些xiē我wǒ认rèn得de，对duì经jīng济jì状zhuàng况kuàng不bù好hǎo的de，我wǒ就jiù少shǎo收shōu一yì些xiē。这zhè样yàng，他tā们men有yǒu了le病bìng才cái会huì来lái看kàn，否fǒu则zé他tā们men岂qǐ不bù是shì只zhǐ能néng有yǒu苦kǔ挨āi？医yī者zhě父fù母mǔ心xīn，我wǒ父fù亲qīn一yì直zhí是shì这zhè样yàng教jiào我wǒ的de，我wǒ也yě一yì直zhí秉bǐng持chí这zhè样yàng的de信xìn念niàn。不bú要yào计jì较jiào钱qián的de多duō少shǎo，关guān键jiàn是shì要yào人rén人rén都dōu看kàn得de起qǐ病bìng。其qí实shí很hěn多duō时shí候hòu我wǒ连lián草cǎo药yào的de本běn钱qián都dōu收shōu不bù回huí来lái。”她tā还hái告gào诉sù我wǒ，她tā给gěi那nà些xiē高gāo收shōu入rù的de人rén看kàn病bìng时shí，常cháng常cháng劝quàn他tā们men捐juān钱qián，即jí使shǐ每měi次cì捐juān的de不bù多duō，但dàn聚jù沙shā成chéng塔tǎ，多duō少shǎo可kě以yǐ对duì比bǐ较jiào贫pín穷qióng的de人rén有yǒu些xiē帮bāng助zhù。没méi有yǒu想xiǎng到dào，一yí个gè普pǔ通tōng的de中zhōng医yī师shī可kě以yǐ如rú此cǐ为wèi那nà些xiē境jìng况kuàng不bù佳jiā的de病bìng人rén着zhuó想xiǎng。她tā的de行xíng为wéi，又yòu一yī次cì让ràng我wǒ为wèi新xīn加jiā坡pō人rén的de善shàn良liáng而ér感gǎn动dòng。
4I often saw a particular TCM practitioner, and when we first met, we had a small misunderstanding over fees. I noticed she always charged me the standard S$22 consultation fee, while charging some others only S$8 or S$10. I asked her why this difference. Like an old friend, she opened her heart and told me: "When I treat patients, I charge based on their financial situation. Some of the people in this neighbourhood I know personally, and for those whose finances are tight, I charge less. That way, when they're unwell, they'll actually come and see me — otherwise wouldn't they just have to suffer in silence? A doctor should have a parent's heart for the patient. My father taught me this, and I've held to it ever since. Don't worry about how much money is involved — what matters is that everyone can afford to see a doctor. Honestly, half the time I don't even cover the cost of the herbs." She also told me that when she treated higher-income patients, she'd often encourage them to donate. Each donation might be modest, but grain by grain a heap is built — between them, they could do something to help poorer patients. I'd never expected an ordinary TCM doctor to be so thoughtful towards patients in difficult circumstances. Her actions touched me deeply — once again — by the goodness of Singaporeans.
5 善shàn良liáng的de举jǔ动dòng有yǒu一yī种zhǒng魔mó力lì，能néng让ràng人rén在zài受shòu到dào感gǎn染rǎn的de同tóng时shí也yě去qù感gǎn染rǎn他tā人rén。我wǒ带dài着zhe感gǎn恩ēn的de心xīn，开kāi始shǐ留liú意yì身shēn边biān的de人rén。
5Acts of kindness have a kind of magic — when they touch you, they also make you want to touch others. I began, with a grateful heart, to pay attention to those around me.
6 以yǐ前qián我wǒ总zǒng是shì行xíng色sè匆cōng匆cōng，根gēn本běn不bú会huì多duō看kàn一yī眼yǎn周zhōu围wéi的de人rén；现xiàn在zài我wǒ学xué会huì了le放fàng慢màn脚jiǎo步bù，学xué会huì了le体tǐ贴tiē关guān怀huái。比bǐ方fāng说shuō：进jìn出chū电diàn梯tī时shí，为wèi别bié人rén按àn着zhe按àn钮niǔ并bìng耐nài心xīn等děng待dài；出chū入rù商shāng场chǎng时shí，主zhǔ动dòng快kuài走zǒu一yī步bù为wèi后hòu面miàn的de人rén拉lā开kāi门mén；下xià台tái阶jiē时shí，帮bāng助zhù带dài小xiǎo孩hái的de母mǔ亲qīn抬tái婴yīng儿ér车chē……
6I used to be always in a hurry, and never gave the people around me a second look. Now I've learned to slow down, learned to pay attention and care. For instance: holding the lift button patiently as people get on or off; in shopping malls, walking a few quick steps ahead to hold the door for the person behind me; on staircases, helping a mother with a young child lift her stroller down the steps…
7 我wǒ所suǒ做zuò的de这zhè一yī切qiè虽suī然rán微wēi不bù足zú道dào，却què是shì在zài尽jǐn自zì己jǐ的de能néng力lì，回huí报bào别bié人rén曾céng经jīng给gěi我wǒ的de帮bāng助zhù。我wǒ相xiāng信xìn，这zhè样yàng的de举jǔ动dòng能néng够gòu给gěi善shàn良liáng插chā上shàng翅chì膀bǎng，让ràng它tā传chuán播bō开kāi来lái，经jīng久jiǔ不bù衰shuāi。
7What I do may be small, but it's me doing what I can to return the kindness others have shown me. I believe these little gestures can give kindness wings — letting it spread, far and wide, and last.
