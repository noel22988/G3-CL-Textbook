1 在zài国guó际jì关guān系xì中zhōng，小xiǎo国guó往wǎng往wǎng显xiǎn得de不bù受shòu重zhòng视shì。那nà么me，小xiǎo国guó是shì不bú是shì就jiù不bù必bì在zài国guó际jì交jiāo往wǎng中zhōng费fèi心xīn费fèi力lì了le呢ne？事shì实shí并bìng非fēi如rú此cǐ。小xiǎo国guó更gèng应yīng该gāi积jī极jí参cān与yù国guó际jì事shì务wù，通tōng过guò国guó家jiā之zhī间jiān的de合hé作zuò，应yìng对duì复fù杂zá而ér多duō变biàn的de国guó际jì环huán境jìng。
1In international relations, small states can seem to be sidelined. Does that mean small states needn't bother to engage on the international stage? Hardly. Small states need to engage all the more actively in international affairs — and meet a complex, ever-shifting global environment through cooperation between countries.
2 风fēng云yún变biàn幻huàn的de国guó际jì环huán境jìng给gěi各gè国guó都dū带dài来lái巨jù大dà的de挑tiǎo战zhàn，要yào应yìng对duì这zhè些xiē挑tiǎo战zhàn，小xiǎo国guó更gèng需xū要yào依yī靠kào国guó际jì间jiān的de合hé作zuò。小xiǎo国guó实shí力lì弱ruò小xiǎo，单dān靠kào自zì己jǐ的de力lì量liàng，很hěn难nán与yǔ大dà国guó竞jìng争zhēng。当dāng大dà国guó之zhī间jiān发fā生shēng纠jiū纷fēn时shí，小xiǎo国guó往wǎng往wǎng面miàn对duì选xuǎn边biān站zhàn的de压yā力lì。弱ruò肉ròu强qiáng食shí是shì国guó际jì政zhèng治zhì的de残cán酷kù事shì实shí，小xiǎo国guó如rú果guǒ不bù选xuǎn边biān站zhàn，就jiù可kě能néng被bèi迫pò靠kào边biān站zhàn。反fǎn之zhī，如rú果guǒ小xiǎo国guó之zhī间jiān能néng够gòu加jiā强qiáng合hé作zuò，就jiù可kě以yǐ扩kuò大dà自zì己jǐ的de影yǐng响xiǎng力lì，更gèng好hǎo地dì维wéi护hù自zì身shēn的de主zhǔ权quán。正zhèng如rú我wǒ国guó领lǐng导dǎo人rén指zhǐ出chū的de，小xiǎo国guó也yě可kě以yǐ在zài国guó际jì交jiāo往wǎng中zhōng起qǐ到dào推tuī动dòng的de作zuò用yòng。我wǒ们men可kě以yǐ通tōng过guò国guó家jiā之zhī间jiān的de协xié作zuò来lái弥mí补bǔ自zì身shēn规guī模mó的de不bù足zú，这zhè样yàng就jiù可kě以yǐ在zài联lián合hé国guó更gèng有yǒu效xiào地dì发fā挥huī作zuò用yòng。俗sú语yǔ说shuō：“一yī根gēn筷kuài子zi容róng易yì断duàn，十shí根gēn筷kuài子zi坚jiān如rú铁tiě。”要yào更gèng好hǎo地dì应yìng对duì国guó际jì环huán境jìng变biàn化huà带dài来lái的de挑tiǎo战zhàn，小xiǎo国guó就jiù必bì须xū加jiā强qiáng团tuán结jié，紧jǐn密mì合hé作zuò，以yǐ集jí体tǐ的de方fāng式shì为wèi切qiè身shēn利lì益yì发fā声shēng。
2A turbulent international environment poses enormous challenges to every country, and small states must rely all the more on international cooperation to face them. Small states are weak in their own right; relying on their own strength alone, they can hardly compete with the great powers. When the great powers are in dispute, small states often come under pressure to pick a side. "The strong devour the weak" is a harsh truth of international politics. If a small state refuses to pick a side, it can find itself shoved to the side instead. By contrast, if small states can deepen cooperation among themselves, they can extend their influence and better defend their sovereignty. As Singapore's leaders have pointed out, small states too can play a driving role in international relations. We can offset our limited scale through cooperation between nations, and thereby play a more effective role at the United Nations. As the saying goes, "One chopstick is easy to break; ten chopsticks together are as firm as iron." To better meet the challenges of a changing international environment, small states must strengthen unity, work closely together, and speak collectively in defence of their core interests.
3 除chú了le国guó际jì环huán境jìng变biàn化huà带dài来lái的de挑tiǎo战zhàn，当dāng今jīn世shì界jiè还hái面miàn临lín许xǔ多duō急jí需xū解jiě决jué的de重zhòng大dà问wèn题tí。由yóu于yú这zhè些xiē问wèn题tí对duì小xiǎo国guó影yǐng响xiǎng往wǎng往wǎng更gèng大dà，因yīn此cǐ小xiǎo国guó更gèng需xū要yào大dà力lì推tuī动dòng国guó际jì合hé作zuò来lái应yìng对duì这zhè些xiē问wèn题tí。例lì如rú，当dāng全quán球qiú性xìng传chuán染rǎn病bìng在zài各gè国guó肆sì虐nüè时shí，为wèi了le防fáng止zhǐ疫yì情qíng扩kuò散sàn，世shì界jiè各gè国guó纷fēn纷fēn加jiā强qiáng防fáng疫yì措cuò施shī，甚shèn至zhì断duàn绝jué交jiāo通tōng往wǎng来lái，经jīng济jì发fā展zhǎn也yě因yīn此cǐ停tíng滞zhì不bù前qián。虽suī然rán每měi个gè国guó家jiā都dū会huì受shòu到dào冲chōng击jī，但dàn受shòu影yǐng响xiǎng最zuì大dà的de还hái是shì那nà些xiē缺quē乏fá资zī源yuán的de小xiǎo国guó。在zài这zhè种zhǒng情qíng况kuàng下xià，一yì些xiē小xiǎo国guó更gèng积jī极jí地dì推tuī动dòng国guó家jiā之zhī间jiān的de合hé作zuò，谋móu求qiú双shuāng赢yíng的de结jié果guǒ。最zuì终zhōng，他tā们men获huò得dé了le急jí需xū的de物wù资zī，也yě成chéng功gōng渡dù过guò难nán关guān。
3Beyond a shifting global environment, the world today also faces many pressing problems that demand urgent solutions. Because such problems often hit small states harder, small states need to push international cooperation all the more vigorously. When a global pandemic raged through nations, for instance, countries everywhere stepped up containment measures and even cut off transport links to prevent the spread of disease — bringing economic activity to a standstill. Every country took a hit, but those affected most were small states with limited resources. In that situation, some small states pushed even harder for cooperation between nations, seeking win-win outcomes. In the end, they got hold of the supplies they urgently needed and made it through the crisis.
4 虽suī然rán小xiǎo国guó实shí力lì有yǒu限xiàn，但dàn仍réng然rán可kě以yǐ在zài推tuī动dòng国guó际jì合hé作zuò方fāng面miàn做zuò出chū贡gòng献xiàn。新xīn加jiā坡pō就jiù是shì一yí个gè很hěn好hǎo的de例lì子zi。我wǒ国guó国guó土tǔ面miàn积jī小xiǎo，人rén口kǒu也yě不bù算suàn多duō，但dàn我wǒ们men在zài国guó际jì事shì务wù上shàng并bìng未wèi因yīn此cǐ而ér畏wèi缩suō不bù前qián。相xiāng反fǎn的de，我wǒ们men一yì直zhí致zhì力lì于yú推tuī动dòng国guó际jì间jiān的de合hé作zuò与yǔ交jiāo流liú。1992年nián，我wǒ国guó在zài联lián合hé国guó倡chàng议yì成chéng立lì非fēi正zhèng式shì的de“小xiǎo国guó论lùn坛tán”。这zhè是shì一yí个gè帮bāng助zhù各gè国guó凝níng聚jù共gòng识shí的de平píng台tái，让ràng大dà家jiā探tàn讨tǎo小xiǎo国guó共gòng同tóng关guān注zhù的de课kè题tí。当dāng时shí，这zhè个ge平píng台tái只zhǐ有yǒu16个gè成chéng员yuán国guó；30年nián后hòu，成chéng员yuán国guó已yǐ经jīng增zēng至zhì108个gè。由yóu此cǐ可kě见jiàn，在zài促cù进jìn国guó际jì交jiāo流liú与yǔ合hé作zuò方fāng面miàn，小xiǎo国guó也yě可kě以yǐ发fā挥huī很hěn大dà的de作zuò用yòng。
4Limited though their power may be, small states can still contribute to advancing international cooperation. Singapore is a good example. Our country is small in territory, and our population is modest — but we have not, on that account, shrunk back from international affairs. On the contrary, we have devoted ourselves to advancing cooperation and exchange between nations. In 1992, Singapore proposed at the United Nations the establishment of an informal "Forum of Small States." It was a platform to help member nations build consensus, and to allow small states to discuss issues of common concern. At the time, the forum had only 16 member states; thirty years later, it has grown to 108. This shows clearly that small states, too, can play a major role in advancing international exchange and cooperation.
5 毋wú庸yōng讳huì言yán，在zài当dāng今jīn世shì界jiè，我wǒ们men仍réng然rán需xū要yào面miàn对duì战zhàn争zhēng、疫yì情qíng、能néng源yuán危wēi机jī等děng棘jí手shǒu的de问wèn题tí。虽suī然rán这zhè些xiē问wèn题tí给gěi小xiǎo国guó带dài来lái挑tiǎo战zhàn与yǔ威wēi胁xié，不bù过guò，只zhǐ要yào大dà家jiā通tōng力lì合hé作zuò，就jiù可kě以yǐ更gèng好hǎo地dì维wéi护hù自zì身shēn的de利lì益yì，同tóng时shí也yě可kě以yǐ为wèi国guó际jì社shè会huì做zuò出chū贡gòng献xiàn。
5There is no need to mince words: in today's world, we still face thorny problems — war, pandemics, energy crises, and more. These bring real challenges and threats to small states. But as long as we work together with all our strength, we can better safeguard our own interests, and contribute to the international community at the same time.
