1 老lǎo子zi说shuō：“知zhī人rén者zhě智zhì，自zì知zhī者zhě明míng。”了liǎo解jiě他tā人rén，需xū要yào有yǒu一yí定dìng的de才cái智zhì，不bù过guò，能néng了liǎo解jiě自zì己jǐ，才cái称chēng得de上shàng高gāo明míng。所suǒ以yǐ说shuō，自zì知zhī是shì非fēi常cháng重zhòng要yào的de。
1Laozi said: "To know others is intelligence; to know yourself is wisdom." Understanding others takes a certain cleverness — but only the one who truly understands himself can be called wise. Self-knowledge, in other words, is profoundly important.
2 一yí个gè人rén只zhǐ有yǒu充chōng分fèn了liǎo解jiě自zì己jǐ，才cái能néng更gèng好hǎo地dì规guī划huà人rén生shēng，进jìn而ér取qǔ得dé成chéng就jiù。德dé国guó诗shī人rén歌gē德dé就jiù颇pǒ有yǒu自zì知zhī之zhī明míng。他tā年nián轻qīng时shí立lì志zhì成chéng为wéi画huà家jiā。后hòu来lái，当dāng他tā看kàn到dào绘huì画huà大dà师shī的de杰jié作zuò后hòu，意yì识shí到dào自zì己jǐ很hěn难nán在zài绘huì画huà方fāng面miàn有yǒu所suǒ成chéng就jiù。经jīng过guò慎shèn重zhòng思sī考kǎo，他tā转zhuǎn而ér致zhì力lì于yú文wén学xué创chuàng作zuò，最zuì终zhōng成chéng为wéi一yī名míng享xiǎng誉yù世shì界jiè的de伟wěi大dà诗shī人rén。由yóu此cǐ可kě见jiàn，一yí个gè人rén如rú果guǒ有yǒu自zì知zhī之zhī明míng，对duì自zì己jǐ的de才cái能néng、个gè性xìng特tè点diǎn等děng有yǒu清qīng楚chǔ的de认rèn识shí，就jiù可kě以yǐ做zuò出chū更gèng恰qià当dàng的de人rén生shēng选xuǎn择zé。
2Only when a person knows themselves fully can they better plan their life and ultimately achieve something. The German poet Goethe was a man of considerable self-knowledge. In his youth, he had set his heart on becoming a painter. Later, after seeing the masterworks of the great painters, he realised he was unlikely to achieve much in painting. After careful reflection, he turned instead to literature — and ultimately became one of the world's great poets. This shows that someone with genuine self-knowledge — a clear understanding of their own talents and temperament — can make far better choices in life.
3 人rén应yīng该gāi有yǒu自zì知zhī之zhī明míng，国guó家jiā也yě应yīng如rú此cǐ。不bù论lùn哪nǎ一yí个gè国guó家jiā，都dōu需xū要yào对duì本běn国guó国guó情qíng有yǒu清qīng楚chǔ的de了liǎo解jiě，这zhè样yàng才cái能néng制zhì定dìng出chū合hé适shì的de政zhèng策cè，让ràng国guó家jiā发fā展zhǎn得dé更gèng好hǎo。挪nuó威wēi就jiù是shì一yí个gè很hěn好hǎo的de例lì子zi。20世shì纪jì60年nián代dài，挪nuó威wēi在zài附fù近jìn海hǎi域yù发fā现xiàn大dà量liàng的de石shí油yóu和hé天tiān然rán气qì，之zhī后hòu通tōng过guò开kāi采cǎi资zī源yuán获huò得dé大dà量liàng财cái富fù。挪nuó威wēi政zhèng府fǔ意yì识shí到dào财cái富fù迅xùn速sù增zēng加jiā会huì带dài来lái许xǔ多duō弊bì端duān，也yě注zhù意yì到dào国guó内nèi人rén口kǒu老lǎo龄líng化huà的de问wèn题tí。于yú是shì，他tā们men决jué定dìng将jiāng石shí油yóu利lì润rùn转zhuǎn化huà为wèi用yòng于yú社shè会huì保bǎo障zhàng的de基jī金jīn，以yǐ让ràng世shì世shì代dài代dài都dōu能néng从cóng中zhōng获huò益yì。这zhè种zhǒng独dú树shù一yī帜zhì的de做zuò法fǎ为wèi国guó家jiā的de长cháng远yuǎn发fā展zhǎn打dǎ下xià坚jiān实shí的de基jī础chǔ，而ér这zhè一yī决jué策cè的de产chǎn生shēng则zé离lí不bù开kāi挪nuó威wēi人rén对duì本běn国guó国guó情qíng的de清qīng醒xǐng认rèn识shí与yǔ准zhǔn确què把bǎ握wò。
3If individuals need self-knowledge, so do nations. Every country needs a clear understanding of its own situation in order to draft policies that will enable it to develop well. Norway is a good example. In the 1960s, Norway discovered large reserves of oil and natural gas in nearby waters, and through extracting these resources accumulated great wealth. The Norwegian government recognised that rapid increases in wealth could bring many problems, and was also conscious of the country's ageing population. They therefore decided to channel oil profits into a sovereign social-welfare fund, so that generation after generation could benefit from it. This distinctive approach has laid a solid foundation for Norway's long-term development — and that decision could only have come from a clear-eyed and accurate grasp of the country's own situation.
4 反fǎn之zhī，如rú果guǒ一yí个gè国guó家jiā对duì自zì己jǐ的de国guó情qíng没méi有yǒu准zhǔn确què的de把bǎ握wò，就jiù可kě能néng会huì做zuò出chū错cuò误wù的de判pàn断duàn，影yǐng响xiǎng国guó家jiā的de发fā展zhǎn。公gōng元yuán前qián三sān世shì纪jì，秦qín始shǐ皇huáng一yī统tǒng天tiān下xià，建jiàn立lì起qǐ一yí个gè空kōng前qián强qiáng大dà的de帝dì国guó。新xīn王wáng朝cháo建jiàn立lì后hòu，需xū要yào修xiū复fù战zhàn争zhēng带dài来lái的de创chuāng伤shāng，百bǎi姓xìng也yě需xū要yào时shí间jiān休xiū养yǎng生shēng息xī。然rán而ér，秦qín王wáng朝cháo统tǒng治zhì者zhě没méi有yǒu认rèn识shí到dào这zhè一yì点diǎn。他tā们men实shí行xíng严yán刑xíng峻jùn法fǎ，征zhēng收shōu沉chén重zhòng的de赋fù税shuì，无wú休xiū止zhǐ地dì征zhēng发fā民mín众zhòng去qù守shǒu边biān防fáng、做zuò苦kǔ工gōng。在zài这zhè种zhǒng情qíng况kuàng下xià，人rén们men忍rěn无wú可kě忍rěn，纷fēn纷fēn揭jiē竿gān而ér起qǐ，反fǎn抗kàng暴bào虐nüè的de统tǒng治zhì。结jié果guǒ，这zhè个ge强qiáng大dà的de王wáng朝cháo只zhǐ存cún在zài了le十shí几jǐ年nián就jiù灰huī飞fēi烟yān灭miè了le。古gǔ语yǔ有yǒu云yún：“败bài莫mò大dà于yú不bù自zì知zhī。”秦qín朝cháo灭miè亡wáng的de一yí个gè重zhòng要yào原yuán因yīn就jiù是shì统tǒng治zhì者zhě缺quē乏fá对duì国guó家jiā的de“自zì知zhī”，在zài错cuò误wù的de道dào路lù上shàng越yuè走zǒu越yuè远yuǎn，最zuì终zhōng葬zàng送sòng了le整zhěng个gè王wáng朝cháo。
4Conversely, if a country fails to grasp its own situation accurately, it is liable to make poor judgments, with consequences for its development. In the 3rd century BCE, Qin Shi Huang unified the realm and built an unprecedented empire. With a new dynasty established, what was needed was healing — repairing the wounds of war, giving the people time to rest and recover. But the rulers of Qin did not see this. They imposed harsh laws and severe punishments, levied crushing taxes, and ceaselessly conscripted the populace to guard the frontiers and do hard labour. Under such conditions, the people could no longer endure, and rose up in revolt against the tyrannical regime. The result: this powerful dynasty was reduced to ashes after just a little over a decade. The ancient saying goes, "There is no greater failure than the failure to know oneself." One key reason for the Qin's collapse was the rulers' lack of self-knowledge as a state — they ventured ever further down the wrong road, and ultimately buried the entire dynasty.
5 要yào做zuò到dào自zì知zhī，就jiù需xū要yào正zhèng确què看kàn待dài自zì身shēn的de优yōu势shì与yǔ不bù足zú。有yǒu些xiē决jué策cè者zhě在zài自zì我wǒ评píng估gū时shí过guò于yú看kàn重zhòng自zì身shēn的de优yōu势shì。他tā们men总zǒng是shì把bǎ标biāo准zhǔn放fàng宽kuān，不bù自zì觉jué地dì放fàng大dà自zì身shēn的de能néng力lì，结jié果guǒ往wǎng往wǎng因yīn盲máng目mù乐lè观guān而ér招zhāo致zhì失shī败bài。如rú果guǒ他tā们men失shī败bài后hòu不bù能néng自zì我wǒ检jiǎn讨tǎo，反fǎn而ér推tuī卸xiè责zé任rèn，那nà就jiù永yǒng远yuǎn无wú法fǎ做zuò到dào自zì知zhī。还hái有yǒu一yì些xiē人rén，在zài审shěn视shì自zì己jǐ的de时shí候hòu，常cháng常cháng把bǎ镜jìng子zi放fàng得dé太tài近jìn，看kàn到dào的de尽jìn是shì自zì身shēn的de缺quē陷xiàn。这zhè样yàng做zuò，容róng易yì变biàn得dé自zì卑bēi，也yě会huì导dǎo致zhì自zì身shēn的de发fā展zhǎn停tíng滞zhì不bù前qián。要yào做zuò到dào自zì知zhī，关guān键jiàn在zài于yú客kè观guān地dì认rèn识shí自zì我wǒ。我wǒ们men既jì不bù应yīng自zì视shì过guò高gāo，变biàn得dé趾zhǐ高gāo气qì扬yáng，也yě无wú须xū过guò度dù谦qiān逊xùn，总zǒng是shì觉jué得de自zì己jǐ一yī无wú是shì处chù。
5To know oneself, one must view one's strengths and weaknesses honestly. Some decision-makers, when assessing themselves, place too much weight on their strengths. They keep loosening their own standards, unconsciously inflating their abilities, and so often invite failure through blind optimism. If, after failing, they refuse to look inward and instead deflect responsibility, they will never achieve self-knowledge. Others, when examining themselves, hold the mirror too close — and see only their flaws. That kind of self-scrutiny breeds inferiority and stalls personal growth. The key to self-knowledge is to see ourselves objectively. We should neither think too highly of ourselves and swagger about, nor be excessively humble and feel we are worthless.
6 无wú论lùn是shì个gè人rén还hái是shì国guó家jiā，都dōu需xū要yào具jù备bèi自zì知zhī之zhī明míng。只zhǐ要yào准zhǔn确què了liǎo解jiě自zì身shēn的de优yōu势shì与yǔ不bù足zú，就jiù能néng够gòu把bǎ自zì己jǐ的de角jué色sè扮bàn演yǎn得dé更gèng好hǎo。这zhè样yàng的de人rén更gèng容róng易yì实shí现xiàn梦mèng寐mèi以yǐ求qiú的de人rén生shēng目mù标biāo，这zhè样yàng的de国guó家jiā会huì走zǒu向xiàng更gèng加jiā繁fán荣róng昌chāng盛shèng的de未wèi来lái。
6Whether individuals or nations, all need self-knowledge. With an accurate sense of one's strengths and weaknesses, one can play one's role all the better. Such a person more readily achieves the life goals they have long dreamed of; such a nation moves towards an ever more prosperous and flourishing future.
