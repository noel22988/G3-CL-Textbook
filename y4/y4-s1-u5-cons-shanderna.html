I
1 小xiǎo时shí候hòu，我wǒ常cháng伏fú在zài窗chuāng口kǒu痴chī想xiǎng ——山shān那nà边biān是shì什shén么me呢ne？ 妈mā妈mā给gěi我wǒ说shuō过guò：海hǎi 哦ó，山shān那nà边biān是shì海hǎi吗ma？
1When I was small, I would lean by the window, lost in thought — — What lies on the other side of the mountains? My mother told me: the sea. Ah — is it the sea, beyond the mountains?
2 于yú是shì，怀huái着zhe一yī种zhǒng隐yǐn秘mì的de想xiǎng望wàng 有yǒu一yì天tiān我wǒ终zhōng于yú爬pá上shàng了le那nà个ge山shān顶dǐng 可kě是shì，我wǒ却què几jī乎hū是shì哭kū着zhe回huí来lái了le ——在zài山shān的de那nà边biān，依yī然rán是shì山shān 山shān那nà边biān的de山shān啊a，铁tiě青qīng着zhe脸liǎn 给gěi我wǒ的de幻huàn想xiǎng打dǎ了le一yí个gè零líng分fēn！ 妈mā妈mā，那nà个ge海hǎi呢ne？
2And so, carrying a secret longing, one day I finally climbed to that mountain peak. But I came back almost in tears — — On the other side of the mountains, were still more mountains. The mountains beyond — their faces were iron-grey, and they gave my wild dream a score of zero! Mother — where is that sea?

II
3 在zài山shān的de那nà边biān，是shì海hǎi！ 是shì用yòng信xìn念niàn凝níng成chéng的de海hǎi 今jīn天tiān啊a，我wǒ竟jìng没méi想xiǎng到dào 一yī颗kē从cóng小xiǎo飘piāo来lái的de种zhǒng子zi 却què在zài我wǒ的de心xīn中zhōng扎zhā下xià了le深shēn根gēn
3On the other side of the mountains — there is the sea! A sea formed of conviction itself. Today — to my own surprise — a seed that drifted in from childhood has put down deep roots in my heart.
4 是shì的de，我wǒ曾céng一yī次cì又yòu一yī次cì地dì失shī望wàng过guò 当dāng我wǒ爬pá上shàng那nà一yī座zuò座zuò诱yòu惑huò着zhe我wǒ的de山shān顶dǐng 但dàn我wǒ又yòu一yī次cì次cì鼓gǔ起qǐ信xìn心xīn向xiàng前qián走zǒu去qù 因yīn为wèi我wǒ听tīng到dào海hǎi依yī然rán在zài远yuǎn方fāng为wèi我wǒ喧xuān腾téng ——那nà雪xuě白bái的de海hǎi潮cháo啊a，夜yè夜yè奔bēn来lái 一yī次cì次cì浸jìn湿shī了le我wǒ枯kū干gān的de心xīn灵líng……
4Yes — again and again I have been disappointed, climbing each mountain peak that beckoned me on, but again and again I gathered my faith and walked forward, because I could hear the sea still roaring in the distance for me — — that snow-white tide, surging in night after night, soaking my parched soul again and again…
5 在zài山shān的de那nà边biān，是shì海hǎi吗ma？ 是shì的de！人rén们men啊a，请qǐng相xiāng信xìn—— 在zài不bù停tíng地dì翻fān过guò无wú数shù座zuò山shān后hòu 在zài一yī次cì次cì地dì战zhàn胜shèng失shī望wàng之zhī后hòu 你nǐ终zhōng会huì攀pān上shàng这zhè样yàng一yī座zuò山shān顶dǐng 而ér在zài这zhè座zuò山shān的de那nà边biān，就jiù是shì海hǎi呀ya 是shì一yí个gè全quán新xīn的de世shì界jiè 在zài一yī瞬shùn间jiān照zhào亮liàng你nǐ的de眼yǎn睛jīng……
5Beyond the mountains — is it the sea? Yes! People, believe me — after you have climbed mountain after mountain without stop, after you have overcome disappointment again and again, you will at last scale one such peak, and on the other side of this mountain, there is the sea — a whole new world, and in an instant, it will light up your eyes…

About the Poem
6 《在zài山shān的de那nà边biān》是shì中zhōng国guó当dāng代dài著zhù名míng诗shī人rén王wáng家jiā新xīn在zài大dà学xué时shí期qī创chuàng作zuò的de作zuò品pǐn。这zhè首shǒu诗shī源yuán自zì他tā儿ér时shí的de生shēng活huó经jīng历lì，表biǎo现xiàn了le他tā童tóng年nián时shí对duì未wèi来lái的de向xiàng往wǎng和hé困kùn惑huò，以yǐ及jí成chéng年nián后hòu的de感gǎn悟wù和hé信xìn念niàn。
6"On the Other Side of the Mountains" is a poem by the celebrated contemporary Chinese poet Wang Jiaxin, written during his university years. The poem grew from his childhood experiences, expressing both the longing and confusion he felt as a child looking toward the future, and the understanding and belief he reached as an adult.
7 这zhè首shǒu诗shī体tǐ现xiàn了le一yī种zhǒng积jī极jí进jìn取qǔ的de精jīng神shén。诗shī人rén所suǒ处chù的de20世shì纪jì80年nián代dài是shì一yí个gè崭zhǎn新xīn的de时shí代dài，社shè会huì呈chéng现xiàn出chū与yǔ以yǐ往wǎng迥jiǒng然rán不bù同tóng的de面miàn貌mào。诗shī人rén以yǐ“海hǎi”象xiàng征zhēng理lǐ想xiǎng，以yǐ“群qún山shān”象xiàng征zhēng种zhǒng种zhǒng困kùn难nán，以yǐ“爬pá山shān”比bǐ喻yù艰jiān苦kǔ奋fèn斗dòu。他tā用yòng诗shī的de语yǔ言yán告gào诉sù人rén们men：奔bēn向xiàng理lǐ想xiǎng的de征zhēng途tú是shì漫màn长cháng而ér崎qí岖qū的de，但dàn只zhǐ要yào我wǒ们men百bǎi折zhé不bù挠náo、坚jiān持chí奋fèn斗dòu，最zuì终zhōng一yí定dìng可kě以yǐ抵dǐ达dá理lǐ想xiǎng的de彼bǐ岸àn。
7The poem embodies a spirit of striving forward. The 1980s, when the poet lived, was a brand new era — a society utterly different from what had come before. The poet uses "the sea" as a symbol for ideals, "the mountains" for the many hardships, and "climbing" as a metaphor for arduous struggle. In the language of poetry, he tells us: the journey towards our ideals is long and rugged — but as long as we are unyielding through every setback, as long as we keep striving, in the end we will surely reach the far shore of our dreams.
