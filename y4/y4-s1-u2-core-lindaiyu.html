1 林lín黛dài玉yù一yí下xià船chuán，就jiù发fā现xiàn荣róng国guó府fǔ派pài来lái的de轿jiào子zi和hé拉lā行xíng李lǐ的de车chē辆liàng在zài岸àn边biān等děng待dài已yǐ久jiǔ。她tā以yǐ前qián常cháng听tīng母mǔ亲qīn说shuō，外wài祖zǔ母mǔ家jiā与yǔ别bié人rén家jiā不bù同tóng。这zhè几jǐ天tiān看kàn到dào的de这zhè几jǐ个gè三sān等děng仆pú妇fù，吃chī穿chuān已yǐ经jīng不bù一yì般bān，何hé况kuàng现xiàn在zài来lái到dào府fǔ里lǐ。因yīn此cǐ她tā步bù步bù留liú心xīn，时shí时shí在zài意yì，不bù肯kěn随suí便biàn多duō说shuō一yī句jù话huà，多duō行xíng一yī步bù路lù，就jiù怕pà被bèi人rén耻chǐ笑xiào。
1The moment Lin Daiyu stepped off the boat, she saw that the sedan chairs sent by the Rongguo Mansion, along with the carts for her luggage, had been waiting at the bank for some time. Her mother had often told her that her grandmother's household was unlike any other. The few maids of the lowest rank she had seen these past days already ate and dressed in a way well above the ordinary — let alone now that she had reached the great house itself. So she watched her every step, kept her thoughts always in check, and would not lightly say a word more or take a step beyond what was necessary, for fear of being mocked.
2 到dào了le荣róng国guó府fǔ，三sān四sì人rén争zhēng着zhe打dǎ开kāi帘lián子zi，有yǒu人rén向xiàng府fǔ里lǐ传chuán话huà：“林lín姑gū娘niáng到dào了le。”
2When she arrived at Rongguo Mansion, three or four people rushed to lift the curtain, and someone called out into the house: "Miss Lin has arrived!"
3 黛dài玉yù刚gāng一yī进jìn屋wū，只zhǐ见jiàn两liǎng个gè人rén搀chān扶fú一yī位wèi银yín发fā老lǎo婆pó婆po迎yíng了le上shàng来lái，就jiù知zhī是shì她tā外wài祖zǔ母mǔ。正zhèng想xiǎng拜bài见jiàn，外wài祖zǔ母mǔ早zǎo已yǐ将jiāng她tā一yī把bǎ搂lǒu入rù怀huái中zhōng，大dà哭kū起qǐ来lái。黛dài玉yù也yě跟gēn着zhe哭kū了le起qǐ来lái。众zhòng人rén慢màn慢màn解jiě劝quàn，黛dài玉yù才cái正zhèng式shì拜bài见jiàn贾jiǎ母mǔ和hé几jǐ位wèi亲qīn戚qī。贾jiǎ母mǔ问wèn黛dài玉yù读dú什shén么me书shū。黛dài玉yù说shuō：“只zhǐ是shì读dú了le《四sì书shū》，姐jiě姐jiě们men都dōu读dú了le什shén么me书shū？”贾jiǎ母mǔ说shuō：“不bù过guò是shì认rèn得de两liǎng个gè字zì，不bú是shì睁zhēng眼yǎn的de瞎xiā子zi罢bà了le！你nǐ以yǐ后hòu就jiù和hé几jǐ个gè姊zǐ妹mèi一yì起qǐ读dú书shū，一yì起qǐ玩wán。但dàn家jiā里lǐ有yǒu个gè‘混hùn世shì魔mó王wáng’，他tā不bù爱ài读dú书shū，很hěn调tiáo皮pí。要yào是shì他tā得dé罪zuì了le你nǐ，你nǐ不bú要yào理lǐ他tā，只zhǐ管guǎn告gào诉sù我wǒ，我wǒ来lái教jiào训xùn他tā。”黛dài玉yù点diǎn头tóu答dā应yìng。
3The moment Daiyu stepped into the room, she saw two people supporting a silver-haired old lady coming forward to greet her — and she knew at once it was her grandmother. Before she could pay her respects, her grandmother had already swept her into an embrace and burst into tears. Daiyu, too, began to weep. Only after the others gradually consoled them did Daiyu formally pay her respects to Grandmother Jia and the other relatives. Grandmother Jia asked what she had been studying. "Only the Four Books," Daiyu replied. "And my elder cousins — what have they read?" Grandmother Jia said: "Oh, they barely recognise a few characters — just enough not to be blind, that's all! From now on you'll study with your cousins, play with them. But there's a 'little devil' in this house — he doesn't care for his books, and he's very mischievous. If he gives you any trouble, just ignore him and tell me — I'll discipline him." Daiyu nodded in agreement.
4 黛dài玉yù在zài家jiā的de时shí候hòu就jiù常cháng听tīng母mǔ亲qīn说shuō，自zì己jǐ有yǒu个gè表biǎo兄xiōng，出chū生shēng的de时shí候hòu衔xián了le块kuài玉yù。他tā性xìng格gé顽wán劣liè，无wú奈nài外wài祖zǔ母mǔ溺nì爱ài，没méi有yǒu人rén敢gǎn管guǎn。黛dài玉yù心xīn中zhōng有yǒu些xiē疑yí惑huò：这zhè个ge宝bǎo玉yù，不bù知zhī道dào是shì怎zěn样yàng顽wán劣liè的de一yí个gè人rén，不bú见jiàn也yě罢bà。
4Back home, Daiyu had often heard her mother say that she had a cousin who had been born with a piece of jade in his mouth. He was unruly and hard to manage, but his grandmother doted on him, and no one dared check him. A doubt rose in Daiyu's heart: this Baoyu — what sort of wild creature could he be? Better, perhaps, not to meet him at all.
5 她tā正zhèng想xiǎng着zhe，只zhǐ听tīng外wài面miàn一yí阵zhèn脚jiǎo步bù响xiǎng，丫yā鬟huán进jìn来lái笑xiào着zhe说shuō：“宝bǎo玉yù来lái了le！”话huà未wèi报bào完wán，已yǐ进jìn来lái了le一yī位wèi年nián轻qīng的de公gōng子zi：头tóu戴dài紫zǐ金jīn冠guān，颈jǐng项xiàng系xì美měi玉yù。面miàn若ruò中zhōng秋qiū之zhī月yuè，色sè如rú春chūn晓xiǎo之zhī花huā，鬓bìn若ruò刀dāo裁cái，眉méi如rú墨mò画huà，面miàn如rú桃táo瓣bàn，目mù若ruò秋qiū波bō。黛dài玉yù大dà吃chī一yī惊jīng，心xīn想xiǎng：奇qí怪guài，倒dào像xiàng在zài哪nǎ里lǐ见jiàn过guò！
5Just as she was thinking this, footsteps sounded outside, and a maid came in smiling. "Master Baoyu is here!" Before she had finished, in walked a young gentleman: a violet-gold coronet on his head, a beautiful piece of jade hanging at his neck. His face was like the moon at mid-autumn, his complexion like flowers at spring's dawn; the hair at his temples was as cleanly cut as if shaped by a knife; his brows were drawn as in ink; his face was a peach petal; his eyes were like autumn waters. Daiyu was startled. How strange, she thought — it was as though she had seen him somewhere before.
6 宝bǎo玉yù向xiàng贾jiǎ母mǔ请qǐng安ān。贾jiǎ母mǔ笑xiào说shuō：“还hái不bù去qù见jiàn你nǐ妹mèi妹mèi！”宝bǎo玉yù早zǎo已yǐ看kàn见jiàn多duō了le一yí个gè姊zǐ妹mèi，忙máng来lái打dǎ招zhāo呼hū。他tā对duì黛dài玉yù细xì细xì打dǎ量liàng了le一yī番fān，笑xiào着zhe说shuō：“这zhè个ge妹mèi妹mèi我wǒ曾céng见jiàn过guò。”贾jiǎ母mǔ笑xiào了le：“又yòu胡hú说shuō，你nǐ何hé曾zēng见jiàn过guò她tā？”
6Baoyu paid his respects to Grandmother Jia. With a smile she said, "Now go and meet your cousin!" Baoyu had already noticed that there was a new young lady, and quickly came forward to greet her. He looked Daiyu over carefully, and said with a smile: "I have seen this cousin before." Grandmother Jia laughed: "Talking nonsense again — when have you ever seen her?"
7 宝bǎo玉yù笑xiào说shuō：“虽suī不bù曾céng见jiàn过guò她tā，但dàn我wǒ看kàn着zhe面miàn善shàn，心xīn里lǐ就jiù当dàng作zuò旧jiù相xiāng识shí，今jīn日rì也yě算suàn久jiǔ别bié重chóng逢féng。”贾jiǎ母mǔ笑xiào道dào：“更gèng好hǎo，更gèng好hǎo，这zhè样yàng你nǐ们men就jiù更gèng和hé睦mù了le。”宝bǎo玉yù走zǒu近jìn黛dài玉yù身shēn边biān坐zuò下xià，问wèn：“妹mèi妹mèi可kě曾zēng读dú书shū？”黛dài玉yù回huí答dá：“没méi读dú过guò什shén么me，只zhǐ上shàng了le一yì年nián学xué，认rèn得de几jǐ个gè字zì。”宝bǎo玉yù又yòu问wèn黛dài玉yù：“你nǐ也yě有yǒu玉yù吗ma？”大dà家jiā都dōu不bù明míng白bái。黛dài玉yù猜cāi想xiǎng他tā生shēng来lái有yǒu玉yù，所suǒ以yǐ问wèn自zì己jǐ有yǒu没méi有yǒu，就jiù答dá道dào：“没méi有yǒu。你nǐ那nà块kuài玉yù是shì极jí稀xī罕hǎn的de，怎zěn么me可kě能néng人rén人rén都dōu有yǒu。”宝bǎo玉yù听tīng了le，马mǎ上shàng摘zhāi下xià脖bó子zi上shàng的de那nà块kuài玉yù，狠hěn狠hěn地dì摔shuāi在zài地dì上shàng，骂mà道dào：“什shén么me东dōng西xī，连lián人rén的de高gāo低dī都dōu不bù分fēn，还hái说shuō‘通tōng灵líng’不bù‘通tōng灵líng’呢ne！我wǒ也yě不bú要yào这zhè破pò东dōng西xī了le！”吓xià得dé大dà家jiā拥yōng过guò去qù拾shí玉yù。贾jiǎ母mǔ急jí得dé搂lǒu着zhe宝bǎo玉yù说shuō：“你nǐ生shēng气qì，要yào打dǎ人rén骂mà人rén都dōu行xíng，何hé苦kǔ摔shuāi那nà命mìng根gēn子zi！”宝bǎo玉yù满mǎn面miàn泪lèi痕hén：“家jiā里lǐ姐jiě姐jiě妹mèi妹mèi都dōu没méi有yǒu，如rú今jīn来lái了le这zhè么me一yí个gè神shén仙xiān似shì的de妹mèi妹mèi也yě没méi有yǒu，可kě见jiàn这zhè不bú是shì个gè好hǎo东dōng西xī。”
7Baoyu replied with a smile: "Even though I haven't truly seen her before, her face seems familiar — in my heart she feels like an old acquaintance, and today is like a reunion after a long separation." Grandmother Jia laughed: "Better and better — that means you two will get along all the more harmoniously." Baoyu came over and sat down beside Daiyu, and asked, "Cousin, have you read any books?" Daiyu answered, "I haven't read much. I only studied for a year, and recognise a few characters." Baoyu then asked: "Do you have a piece of jade as well?" Nobody understood the question. Daiyu guessed that since he had been born with one, he was asking whether she had one too, and answered, "No, I don't. Your jade is something extraordinarily rare — how could anyone have one?" At this, Baoyu immediately tore the jade from his neck and dashed it furiously to the ground, cursing: "What sort of thing is this — it can't even tell high from low. And to think they call it 'spiritual'! I don't want this rubbish either!" Everyone was so alarmed they rushed forward to pick up the jade. Grandmother Jia, panicking, hugged Baoyu and said, "If you're angry, hit and curse all you like — but why throw down the very thing your life depends on?" Baoyu's face streamed with tears: "None of my elder or younger cousins has one — and now this fairy of a cousin doesn't have one either. That just shows it isn't a good thing."
8 贾jiǎ母mǔ忙máng哄hǒng他tā：“她tā原yuán本běn是shì有yǒu的de，你nǐ姑gū妈mā去qù世shì后hòu，她tā为wèi了le尽jìn孝xiào心xīn把bǎ玉yù拿ná去qù陪péi你nǐ姑gū妈mā了le。”说shuō着zhe，便biàn从cóng丫yā鬟huán手shǒu中zhōng接jiē过guò玉yù来lái，亲qīn自zì给gěi他tā戴dài上shàng。宝bǎo玉yù听tīng了le，也yě就jiù不bù说shuō什shén么me了le。
8Grandmother Jia quickly soothed him: "She did have one originally — but after your aunt passed away, she gave it up out of filial piety to be buried with her mother." As she spoke, she took the jade from the maid's hand and personally fastened it back on Baoyu. Hearing this, he said no more.
9 贾jiǎ母mǔ安ān置zhì了le黛dài玉yù的de住zhù宿sù后hòu，还hái派pài了le一yì些xiē丫yā鬟huán照zhào顾gù她tā。宝bǎo玉yù要yāo求qiú与yǔ黛dài玉yù相xiāng邻lín作zuò伴bàn，贾jiǎ母mǔ也yě同tóng意yì了le。
9Once Grandmother Jia had arranged Daiyu's lodgings, she also assigned several maids to look after her. Baoyu asked to live next to Daiyu, so they could keep each other company, and Grandmother Jia agreed.
