1 英yīng国guó媒méi体tǐ曾céng经jīng评píng出chū二èr十shí世shì纪jì人rén类lèi最zuì糟zāo糕gāo的de发fā明míng，“得dé奖jiǎng”的de正zhèng是shì人rén们men每měi天tiān大dà量liàng使shǐ用yòng的de塑sù料liào袋dài。
1The British media once ran a poll for the worst human invention of the twentieth century. The "winner" was none other than the plastic bag — the very thing we use every day in vast numbers.
塑sù料liào袋dài的de广guǎng泛fàn使shǐ用yòng
Plastic Bags Everywhere
2 塑sù料liào早zǎo在zài一yì百bǎi多duō年nián前qián就jiù发fā明míng了le，但dàn它tā的de飞fēi速sù发fā展zhǎn是shì从cóng二èr十shí世shì纪jì五wǔ十shí年nián代dài开kāi始shǐ的de。自zì那nà时shí起qǐ，塑sù料liào的de产chǎn量liàng几jī乎hū直zhí线xiàn上shàng升shēng。塑sù料liào在zài工gōng业yè生shēng产chǎn和hé人rén们men的de日rì常cháng生shēng活huó中zhōng，都dōu扮bàn演yǎn着zhuó重zhòng要yào的de角jué色sè。
2Plastic was invented more than a hundred years ago, but its rapid rise only began in the 1950s. Since then, plastic production has climbed in what is almost a straight line upward. Plastic now plays a major role both in industry and in everyday life.
3 我wǒ们men今jīn天tiān所suǒ熟shú悉xī的de一yí次cì性xìng塑sù料liào袋dài，也yě是shì在zài上shàng世shì纪jì五wǔ六liù十shí年nián代dài开kāi始shǐ出chū现xiàn的de。这zhè种zhǒng塑sù料liào袋dài因yīn为wèi廉lián价jià、卫wèi生shēng、方fāng便biàn和hé耐nài用yòng，很hěn快kuài便biàn得dé到dào广guǎng泛fàn使shǐ用yòng。
3The disposable plastic bags we all know today also first appeared in the 1950s and 60s. Because they were cheap, hygienic, convenient, and hard-wearing, they quickly came into widespread use.
4 在zài我wǒ国guó，大dà部bù分fèn人rén在zài超chāo市shì购gòu物wù时shí都dōu习xí惯guàn使shǐ用yòng超chāo市shì提tí供gōng的de塑sù料liào袋dài。根gēn据jù新xīn加jiā坡pō环huán境jìng理lǐ事shì会huì的de调diào查chá，去qù超chāo市shì购gòu物wù的de人rén中zhōng，使shǐ用yòng3至zhì5个gè塑sù料liào袋dài的de人rén占zhàn百bǎi分fēn之zhī三sān十shí五wǔ，有yǒu大dà约yuē一yí半bàn的de人rén每měi次cì使shǐ用yòng至zhì少shǎo3个gè塑sù料liào袋dài。本běn地dì每měi年nián使shǐ用yòng的de塑sù料liào袋dài达dá8.2亿yì个gè，如rú果guǒ把bǎ这zhè些xiē塑sù料liào袋dài铺pū平píng开kāi来lái，面miàn积jī相xiāng当dāng于yú126个gè滨bīn海hǎi湾wān花huā园yuán。
4In Singapore, most people are used to taking the plastic bags provided at supermarkets when they shop. According to a survey by the Singapore Environment Council, 35% of supermarket shoppers use between 3 and 5 plastic bags per trip, and about half use at least 3 each time. Singapore uses 820 million plastic bags a year — spread flat, they would cover an area equivalent to 126 Gardens by the Bay.
人rén们men每měi次cì上shàng超chāo市shì使shǐ用yòng多duō少shǎo个gè塑sù料liào袋dài
Bags Per Supermarket Trip
5 不bù用yòng塑sù料liào袋dài、自zì备bèi购gòu物wù袋dài 15%；不bù去qù超chāo市shì 3%；1–2个gè 33%；3–5个gè 35%；6个gè或huò以yǐ上shàng 14%。
5None (bring own bags): 15%. Don't go to supermarkets: 3%. 1–2 bags: 33%. 3–5 bags: 35%. 6 or more: 14%.
塑sù料liào垃lā圾jī的de危wēi害hài
The Harm of Plastic Waste
6 塑sù料liào袋dài给gěi人rén类lèi带dài来lái很hěn多duō方fāng便biàn，但dàn被bèi丢diū弃qì的de塑sù料liào袋dài也yě对duì环huán境jìng造zào成chéng严yán重zhòng的de污wū染rǎn。塑sù料liào属shǔ于yú不bù可kě降jiàng解jiě材cái料liào，会huì给gěi环huán境jìng带dài来lái很hěn大dà危wēi害hài。此cǐ外wài，人rén们men大dà多duō通tōng过guò填tián埋mái或huò焚fén烧shāo处chǔ理lǐ塑sù料liào垃lā圾jī，而ér这zhè两liǎng种zhǒng方fāng式shì都dōu不bù利lì于yú环huán保bǎo。
6Plastic bags bring huge convenience — but discarded ones cause serious environmental pollution. Plastic is a non-biodegradable material; it does lasting damage to the environment. On top of that, most plastic waste is dealt with by landfill or incineration, neither of which is good for the environment.
7 焚fén烧shāo塑sù料liào垃lā圾jī产chǎn生shēng的de有yǒu害hài烟yān尘chén和hé有yǒu毒dú气qì体tǐ，会huì污wū染rǎn空kōng气qì。塑sù料liào垃lā圾jī埋mái在zài地dì里lǐ200年nián也yě不bù一yí定dìng能néng腐fǔ烂làn、降jiàng解jiě，会huì严yán重zhòng污wū染rǎn土tǔ壤rǎng。河hé流liú和hé海hǎi洋yáng里lǐ的de塑sù料liào垃lā圾jī经jīng常cháng被bèi动dòng物wù当dàng作zuò食shí物wù吞tūn食shí，导dǎo致zhì动dòng物wù因yīn消xiāo化huà道dào堵dǔ塞sè而ér死sǐ亡wáng。
7Incinerating plastic releases harmful soot and toxic gases that pollute the air. Plastic buried in the ground may not rot or break down even after 200 years, severely contaminating the soil. Plastic in rivers and oceans is often mistaken for food by animals and swallowed, leading to deaths from blocked digestive tracts.
8 在zài我wǒ国guó，塑sù料liào制zhì品pǐn使shǐ用yòng量liàng非fēi常cháng大dà，其qí中zhōng将jiāng近jìn一yí半bàn是shì塑sù料liào袋dài。这zhè些xiē塑sù料liào制zhì品pǐn中zhōng的de绝jué大dà部bù分fèn最zuì后hòu都dōu变biàn成chéng了le塑sù料liào垃lā圾jī。
8Singapore uses a huge amount of plastic products — nearly half of which are plastic bags. The vast majority of these ultimately end up as plastic waste.
减jiǎn少shǎo塑sù料liào袋dài的de使shǐ用yòng
Cutting Down on Plastic Bags
9 为wèi了le减jiǎn少shǎo塑sù料liào袋dài的de危wēi害hài，一yì些xiē国guó家jiā纷fēn纷fēn采cǎi取qǔ措cuò施shī，遏è制zhì塑sù料liào袋dài的de大dà量liàng使shǐ用yòng。这zhè些xiē措cuò施shī包bāo括kuò让ràng消xiāo费fèi者zhě付fù费fèi购gòu买mǎi塑sù料liào袋dài、不bù提tí供gōng塑sù料liào袋dài等děng。此cǐ外wài，许xǔ多duō国guó家jiā开kāi始shǐ使shǐ用yòng可kě降jiàng解jiě材cái料liào制zhì造zào塑sù料liào袋dài，以yǐ减jiǎn少shǎo环huán境jìng污wū染rǎn。我wǒ国guó也yě倡chàng导dǎo人rén们men爱ài护hù环huán境jìng，逐zhú渐jiàn减jiǎn少shǎo塑sù料liào袋dài的de使shǐ用yòng。许xǔ多duō机jī构gòu采cǎi用yòng各gè种zhǒng方fāng式shì鼓gǔ励lì国guó人rén购gòu物wù时shí自zì备bèi购gòu物wù袋dài。一yì些xiē超chāo市shì、餐cān馆guǎn也yě通tōng过guò收shōu费fèi的de方fāng式shì，引yǐn导dǎo顾gù客kè少shǎo用yòng一yí次cì性xìng塑sù料liào袋dài。
9To reduce the harm of plastic bags, a number of countries have taken steps to curb their heavy use. These include charging consumers for bags, or not providing them at all. Many countries are now also using biodegradable materials to make plastic bags, cutting down on environmental pollution. Singapore, too, advocates caring for the environment and gradually reducing plastic bag use. Many organisations encourage Singaporeans to bring their own shopping bags, and some supermarkets and restaurants have introduced charges to steer customers away from disposable plastic bags.
10 塑sù料liào袋dài的de问wèn世shì，给gěi人rén们men的de生shēng活huó带dài来lái极jí大dà的de便biàn利lì。然rán而ér，随suí处chù丢diū弃qì的de塑sù料liào袋dài，也yě给gěi环huán境jìng造zào成chéng严yán重zhòng的de威wēi胁xié。如rú今jīn，当dāng人rén们men为wèi如rú何hé处chǔ理lǐ塑sù料liào袋dài问wèn题tí而ér煞shà费fèi苦kǔ心xīn时shí，心xīn里lǐ不bù禁jīn会huì问wèn：将jiāng来lái还hái会huì出chū现xiàn这zhè样yàng的de发fā明míng吗ma？
10The arrival of the plastic bag brought great convenience to daily life. Yet discarded plastic bags, thrown everywhere, also pose a serious threat to the environment. Today, as people rack their brains for how to deal with the plastic bag problem, one can't help but wonder: will the future bring more inventions like this one?
