《山中奇遇》 A Strange Encounter in the Mountains

1 前qián几jǐ年nián，我wǒ常cháng常cháng喜xǐ欢huān往wǎng山shān里lǐ跑pǎo，山shān里lǐ的de风fēng景jǐng总zǒng是shì深shēn深shēn地dì吸xī引yǐn着zhe我wǒ。每měi次cì进jìn山shān，总zǒng会huì遇yù到dào一yì些xiē奇qí事shì。
1In years past, I was often drawn to the mountains — their scenery pulled me in, deep and hard. Every time I went into them, something strange or wonderful would happen.
2 有yǒu一yī次cì，我wǒ和hé几jǐ个gè同tóng伴bàn去qù山shān里lǐ玩wán，一yī路lù上shàng总zǒng被bèi一yì些xiē野yě草cǎo挡dǎng住zhù去qù路lù。就jiù在zài寻xún路lù的de时shí候hòu，一yí个gè同tóng伴bàn惊jīng喜xǐ地dì喊hǎn起qǐ来lái：“看kàn，好hǎo漂piào亮liàng的de鸟niǎo蛋dàn！”几jǐ个gè人rén围wéi上shàng前qián去qù一yī瞧qiáo：三sān颗kē滚gǔn圆yuán的de小xiǎo鸟niǎo蛋dàn，光guāng彩cǎi夺duó目mù地dì躺tǎng在zài一yī堆duī枯kū草cǎo中zhōng。蛋dàn壳ké是shì天tiān蓝lán色sè的de，隐yǐn隐yǐn约yuē约yuē有yǒu一yì些xiē墨mò绿lǜ的de斑bān点diǎn。小xiǎo鸟niǎo蛋dàn像xiàng宝bǎo石shí一yí样yàng，漂piào亮liàng极jí了le！
2Once, I went into the mountains with a few companions. As we walked, tall wild grasses kept blocking our way. As we were searching for a path, one of my companions cried out in delight: "Look — what beautiful bird's eggs!" We all crowded around to have a look. Three perfectly round little eggs lay glowing in a patch of dry grass. Their shells were sky-blue, with faint, dark-green speckling. The little eggs looked just like jewels — stunningly beautiful!
3 三sān颗kē鸟niǎo蛋dàn被bèi一yí个gè同tóng伴bàn小xiǎo心xīn翼yì翼yì地dì装zhuāng进jìn了le口kǒu袋dài。另lìng一yí个gè同tóng伴bàn从cóng小xiǎo在zài山shān里lǐ长zhǎng大dà，他tā有yǒu些xiē紧jǐn张zhāng地dì说shuō：“还hái是shì把bǎ这zhè三sān个gè蛋dàn放fàng回huí原yuán处chù吧ba。”
3One of us carefully slipped the three eggs into his pocket. Another of our companions, who had grown up in the mountains, said nervously: "Better put these three eggs back where they were."
4 “为wèi什shén么me？”
4"Why?"
5 “等děng一yī会huì儿er雌cí鸟niǎo会huì来lái找zhǎo我wǒ们men的de。”
5"Because in a bit, the mother bird will come looking for us."
6 “哪nǎ有yǒu这zhè种zhǒng事shì？你nǐ的de想xiǎng象xiàng力lì太tài丰fēng富fù了le。”
6"As if that could happen — your imagination's too vivid."
7 “真zhēn的de，不bù骗piàn你nǐ们men。山shān里lǐ的de老lǎo人rén常cháng说shuō，谁shuí拿ná走zǒu荒huāng山shān野yě草cǎo里lǐ的de鸟niǎo蛋dàn，鸟niǎo儿ér是shì不bú会huì放fàng过guò他tā的de。”他tā说shuō得dé挺tǐng认rèn真zhēn，可kě谁shuí也yě不bù相xiāng信xìn他tā的de话huà。
7"I'm not kidding — really. The old folks in these mountains always say: whoever takes an egg from the wild grass, the bird won't let them off." He was in earnest, but no one believed him.
8 没méi走zǒu出chū二èr百bǎi米mǐ，奇qí事shì就jiù发fā生shēng了le。一yī只zhī白bái胸xiōng灰huī褐hè色sè羽yǔ毛máo的de小xiǎo鸟niǎo从cóng后hòu面miàn飞fēi了le过guò来lái，在zài我wǒ们men的de头tóu顶dǐng上shàng来lái回huí盘pán旋xuán，不bù断duàn发fā出chū一yī种zhǒng急jí促cù不bù安ān的de叫jiào声shēng。没méi多duō久jiǔ，又yòu飞fēi来lái了le第dì二èr只zhǐ，两liǎng只zhǐ鸟niǎo一yī高gāo一yī低dī，不bù停tíng地dì绕rào着zhe我wǒ们men飞fēi。
8Before we'd walked even two hundred metres, the strange thing began. A small bird with a white breast and greyish-brown feathers came flying up from behind, circling back and forth over our heads, crying out urgently and agitatedly. Before long, a second one came flying in, and the two of them — one higher, one lower — kept circling round us without stop.
9 大dà家jiā都dōu停tíng住zhù了le脚jiǎo步bù，呆dāi呆dāi地dì看kàn着zhe这zhè一yī对duì奇qí怪guài的de小xiǎo鸟niǎo。它tā们men越yuè飞fēi越yuè低dī，有yǒu时shí甚shèn至zhì差chà点diǎn儿ér扑pū到dào我wǒ们men脸liǎn上shàng来lái。它tā们men的de叫jiào声shēng也yě越yuè来lái越yuè急jí促cù，似sì乎hū在zài生shēng气qì地dì骂mà着zhe什shén么me。
9We all stopped in our tracks, staring in amazement at this strange pair of little birds. They flew lower and lower, sometimes nearly striking our faces. Their cries grew more and more urgent, as if they were angrily scolding us about something.
10 大dà约yuē过guò了le五wǔ分fēn钟zhōng，两liǎng只zhǐ鸟niǎo丝sī毫háo没méi有yǒu放fàng弃qì的de样yàng子zi，依yī然rán围wéi着zhe我wǒ们men急jí急jí地dì飞fēi着zhe，叫jiào着zhe。大dà家jiā面miàn面miàn相xiāng觑qù，不bù知zhī如rú何hé是shì好hǎo。这zhè时shí，山shān里lǐ长zhǎng大dà的de同tóng伴bàn突tū然rán喊hǎn起qǐ来lái：“还hái愣lèng着zhe做zuò什shén么me，快kuài把bǎ鸟niǎo蛋dàn还huán给gěi它tā们men！”
10After about five minutes, the two birds showed no sign of giving up — still circling, still crying. We looked at one another helplessly, not knowing what to do. Just then, our mountain-raised companion suddenly shouted: "What are you standing there for? Give them back their eggs, quick!"
11 拾shí蛋dàn的de同tóng伴bàn赶gǎn紧jǐn从cóng口kǒu袋dài里lǐ拿ná出chū鸟niǎo蛋dàn，慌huāng里lǐ慌huāng张zhāng地dì把bǎ它tā们men放fàng到dào一yī块kuài大dà石shí头tou上shàng。因yīn为wèi动dòng作zuò太tài快kuài，结jié果guǒ鸟niǎo蛋dàn全quán摔shuāi碎suì了le，透tòu明míng的de蛋dàn清qīng从cóng天tiān蓝lán色sè的de蛋dàn壳ké碎suì片piàn中zhōng无wú声shēng无wú息xī地dì流liú出chū来lái。我wǒ们men所suǒ有yǒu人rén都dōu傻shǎ了le眼yǎn。
11The companion who had taken the eggs hastily pulled them from his pocket, in a fluster, and set them onto a large rock. But he moved too fast — all three eggs broke. Clear egg white flowed silently out of the shards of sky-blue shell. Every one of us was dumbstruck.
12 两liǎng只zhǐ鸟niǎo停tíng落luò在zài那nà块kuài大dà石shí头tou上shàng。它tā们men呆dāi呆dāi地dì看kàn着zhe鸟niǎo蛋dàn的de碎suì片piàn，好hǎo像xiàng很hěn伤shāng心xīn的de样yàng子zi。
12The two birds landed on the rock. They stared blankly at the shattered eggs, their sorrow plain to see.
13 我wǒ们men重chóng新xīn上shàng路lù时shí，心xīn头tóu充chōng满mǎn愧kuì疚jiù。我wǒ们men在zài荒huāng草cǎo和hé乱luàn石shí中zhōng转zhuǎn了le半bàn天tiān，竟jìng迷mí失shī了le方fāng向xiàng，一yì直zhí到dào天tiān黑hēi才cái找zhǎo到dào一yī条tiáo路lù。走zǒu出chū大dà山shān后hòu，大dà家jiā都dōu说shuō这zhè是shì一yī次cì最zuì难nán忘wàng的de山shān中zhōng奇qí遇yù。
13When we set off again, guilt weighed heavy on our hearts. For hours we wandered through wild grass and scattered stones, losing our way, and only found a proper path after darkness had fallen. Once we were out of the mountains, we all agreed it was the most unforgettable encounter we'd ever had there.
