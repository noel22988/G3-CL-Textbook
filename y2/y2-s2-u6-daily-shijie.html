1 新xīn加jiā坡pō是shì古gǔ代dài海hǎi上shàng贸mào易yì的de中zhōng转zhuǎn站zhàn。很hěn多duō货huò物wù经jīng由yóu新xīn加jiā坡pō运yùn送sòng到dào世shì界jiè各gè地dì，东dōng西xī方fāng文wén化huà也yě在zài这zhè个ge过guò程chéng中zhōng得dé到dào传chuán播bō和hé交jiāo流liú。
1Singapore was a transit hub for ancient maritime trade. Many goods made their way to every corner of the world through Singapore — and along with them, Eastern and Western cultures spread, met, and exchanged.
海hǎi上shàng贸mào易yì的de中zhōng转zhuǎn站zhàn
A Transit Hub of Maritime Trade
2 丝sī绸chóu起qǐ源yuán于yú中zhōng国guó。人rén们men后hòu来lái通tōng过guò陆lù地dì和hé海hǎi上shàng的de丝sī绸chóu之zhī路lù，将jiāng它tā运yùn送sòng到dào西xī方fāng。西xī方fāng人rén普pǔ遍biàn把bǎ它tā视shì为wèi奢shē侈chǐ品pǐn。
2Silk originated in China. People later transported it westward along the overland and maritime Silk Roads. To Westerners, silk was widely seen as a luxury good.
3 中zhōng国guó的de瓷cí器qì主zhǔ要yào通tōng过guò海hǎi上shàng贸mào易yì运yùn输shū到dào东dōng南nán亚yà和hé欧ōu洲zhōu一yí带dài。瓷cí器qì的de流liú通tōng促cù进jìn了le各gè地dì的de文wén化huà交jiāo流liú。
3Chinese porcelain travelled mainly via maritime trade to Southeast Asia and Europe. The flow of porcelain helped drive cultural exchange between different regions.
4 香xiāng料liào盛shèng产chǎn于yú印yìn度dù和hé印yìn度dù尼ní西xī亚yà等děng地dì。除chú了le用yòng于yú烹pēng饪rèn，它tā还hái可kě以yǐ用yòng于yú宗zōng教jiào仪yí式shì、医yī药yào等děng方fāng面miàn。这zhè些xiē香xiāng料liào及jí其qí使shǐ用yòng方fāng法fǎ，大dà多duō随suí着zhe海hǎi上shàng贸mào易yì，经jīng由yóu新xīn加jiā坡pō传chuán播bō到dào世shì界jiè各gè地dì。
4Spices were abundant in places like India and Indonesia. Beyond cooking, they were used in religious rituals, medicine, and more. Most of these spices, and the ways of using them, spread to every corner of the world through maritime trade — passing through Singapore on the way.
5 一yì直zhí以yǐ来lái，新xīn加jiā坡pō都dōu秉bǐng持chí开kāi放fàng的de态tài度dù，与yǔ世shì界jiè各gè国guó保bǎo持chí密mì切qiè的de联lián系xì。今jīn天tiān，新xīn加jiā坡pō更gèng积jī极jí地dì融róng入rù国guó际jì社shè会huì，不bù仅jǐn向xiàng世shì界jiè各gè国guó学xué习xí，也yě分fēn享xiǎng自zì己jǐ的de经jīng验yàn。
5Singapore has always held to an open posture, maintaining close ties with countries around the world. Today, Singapore engages with the international community even more actively — not only learning from other nations, but also sharing its own experience.
全quán球qiú物wù流liú枢shū纽niǔ
A Global Logistics Hub
6 全quán球qiú化huà这zhè一yī发fā展zhǎn趋qū势shì促cù进jìn了le货huò物wù在zài世shì界jiè范fàn围wéi内nèi的de流liú通tōng。新xīn加jiā坡pō是shì世shì界jiè的de主zhǔ要yào港gǎng口kǒu之zhī一yī，她tā与yǔ全quán球qiú600多duō个gè港gǎng口kǒu相xiāng连lián，为wèi来lái往wǎng的de船chuán只zhī提tí供gōng一yì流liú的de服fú务wù。
6The trend of globalisation has accelerated the worldwide flow of goods. Singapore is one of the world's major ports — connected to more than 600 ports globally, providing first-class service to the ships that come and go.
多duō元yuán文wén化huà社shè会huì
A Multicultural Society
7 新xīn加jiā坡pō致zhì力lì于yú打dǎ造zào一yí个gè多duō元yuán文wén化huà的de社shè会huì。她tā不bù但dàn注zhù重zhòng加jiā强qiáng国guó人rén的de身shēn份fèn认rèn同tóng，也yě鼓gǔ励lì各gè种zhǒng族zú传chuán承chéng自zì己jǐ的de文wén化huà。多duō元yuán文wén化huà并bìng存cún这zhè一yī特tè质zhì，使shǐ新xīn加jiā坡pō能néng够gòu以yǐ开kāi放fàng的de心xīn胸xiōng接jiē纳nà世shì界jiè各gè地dì的de文wén化huà。
7Singapore is committed to building a multicultural society. She not only strengthens her people's sense of national identity, but also encourages each race to pass down its own culture. The fact that multiple cultures coexist allows Singapore to open her heart to cultures from around the world.
高gāo科kē技jì智zhì慧huì国guó
A High-Tech Smart Nation
8 2014年nián，新xīn加jiā坡pō勾gōu勒lè出chū智zhì慧huì国guó的de愿yuàn景jǐng。通tōng过guò充chōng分fèn利lì用yòng高gāo科kē技jì，推tuī动dòng社shè会huì、经jīng济jì和hé公gōng共gòng服fú务wù的de数shù码mǎ化huà。这zhè些xiē高gāo瞻zhān远yuǎn瞩zhǔ的de规guī划huà，不bù但dàn会huì让ràng国guó民mín受shòu惠huì，也yě会huì加jiā强qiáng新xīn加jiā坡pō与yǔ世shì界jiè的de联lián系xì。
8In 2014, Singapore sketched out her Smart Nation vision: using technology to the fullest to drive the digitalisation of society, the economy, and public services. These far-sighted plans benefit Singaporeans, and also deepen Singapore's connections to the world.
