Mood Journal
About This Column. Four diary entries reflecting on moments that call for self-reflection — body insecurity, growing distant from a friend, arguing with a sibling, and facing a crossroads.
日rì记jì一yī：身shēn材cái与yǔ自zì律lǜ
心xīn情qíng记jì录lù我wǒ一yì直zhí对duì自zì己jǐ的de身shēn材cái不bù满mǎn意yì，甚shèn至zhì有yǒu些xiē自zì卑bēi。每měi次cì看kàn到dào别bié人rén健jiàn美měi的de身shēn材cái，我wǒ都dōu非fēi常cháng羡xiàn慕mù。可kě是shì，我wǒ又yòu无wú法fǎ抗kàng拒jù零líng食shí的de诱yòu惑huò。我wǒ要yào做zuò一yí个gè有yǒu毅yì力lì的de人rén，坚jiān持chí运yùn动dòng，戒jiè掉diào爱ài吃chī零líng食shí的de坏huài习xí惯guàn。
Entry 1: Body Image and Self-Discipline
Journal Entry. I've never been happy with my body. Honestly, it makes me feel a little inferior. Every time I see someone with a toned figure, I'm so envious. But I just can't resist the temptation of snacks. I want to be someone with willpower — to stick with exercise and kick this snacking habit for good.
日rì记jì二èr：朋péng友yǒu之zhī间jiān
心xīn情qíng记jì录lù美měi星xīng是shì我wǒ的de好hǎo朋péng友yǒu。最zuì近jìn不bù知zhī怎zěn么me的de，我wǒ们men的de关guān系xì好hǎo像xiàng越yuè来lái越yuè疏shū远yuǎn了le。这zhè几jǐ天tiān，我wǒ发fā现xiàn她tā在zài课kè堂táng上shàng要yào么me一yī脸liǎn茫máng然rán，要yào么me打dǎ瞌kē睡shuì。我wǒ问wèn了le她tā几jǐ次cì，她tā总zǒng是shì避bì开kāi问wèn题tí。我wǒ明míng天tiān要yào跟gēn她tā好hǎo好hǎo谈tán一yī谈tán。
Entry 2: Between Friends
Journal Entry. Meixing is my good friend. Lately, for some reason, we seem to be drifting apart. These last few days I've noticed her in class staring blankly at nothing, or dozing off. I've asked her about it a few times, but she keeps deflecting. Tomorrow I'm going to sit her down and have a proper talk.
日rì记jì三sān：兄xiōng弟dì之zhī争zhēng
心xīn情qíng记jì录lù昨zuó天tiān，我wǒ和hé哥gē哥gē因yīn为wèi争zhēng着zhe用yòng电diàn脑nǎo而ér吵chǎo了le起qǐ来lái。哥gē哥gē嫌xián我wǒ电diàn脑nǎo用yòng得dé太tài久jiǔ，逼bī我wǒ把bǎ电diàn脑nǎo让ràng出chū来lái。太tài不bù讲jiǎng理lǐ了le！晚wǎn餐cān时shí，哥gē哥gē却què把bǎ我wǒ们men都dōu爱ài吃chī的de鸡jī腿tuǐ全quán让ràng给gěi我wǒ。既jì然rán他tā主zhǔ动dòng示shì好hǎo，那nà我wǒ也yě不bú要yào再zài生shēng他tā的de气qì了le。
Entry 3: A Fight with My Brother
Journal Entry. Yesterday, my brother and I got into a fight over the computer. He complained I'd been using it too long and forced me to hand it over. So unreasonable! But at dinner, he gave me all the chicken drumsticks we both love. Since he's made the first move to patch things up, I'll let my anger go.
日rì记jì四sì：梦mèng想xiǎng与yǔ选xuǎn择zé
心xīn情qíng记jì录lù最zuì近jìn，我wǒ对duì烹pēng饪rèn的de兴xìng趣qù越yuè来lái越yuè浓nóng厚hòu。马mǎ上shàng就jiù要yào选xuǎn中zhōng三sān的de科kē目mù了le。我wǒ想xiǎng选xuǎn修xiū营yíng养yǎng与yǔ食shí品pǐn科kē学xué，将jiāng来lái做zuò一yī名míng厨chú师shī。可kě是shì，爸bà爸bà是shì会kuài计jì师shī，他tā希xī望wàng我wǒ将jiāng来lái也yě朝cháo这zhè方fāng面miàn发fā展zhǎn。尽jǐn管guǎn我wǒ的de梦mèng想xiǎng不bù符fú合hé爸bà爸bà的de期qī望wàng，但dàn我wǒ还hái是shì想xiǎng按àn照zhào自zì己jǐ的de兴xìng趣qù来lái做zuò选xuǎn择zé。
Entry 4: Dreams and Choices
Journal Entry. I've been getting more and more into cooking lately. Secondary 3 subject selection is coming up soon. I want to take Nutrition and Food Science, and become a chef someday. But Dad's an accountant, and he's hoping I'll head in that direction too. Even though my dream isn't quite what Dad wants for me, I still want to choose based on what I actually love.
