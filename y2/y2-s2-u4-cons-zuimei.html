1 小xiǎo学xué时shí，一yī场cháng车chē祸huò导dǎo致zhì我wǒ的de脖bó子zi歪wāi向xiàng右yòu边biān。从cóng那nà以yǐ后hòu，我wǒ变biàn得dé特tè别bié敏mǐn感gǎn。有yǒu一yī次cì，当dāng全quán班bān朗lǎng读dú“鹅é，鹅é，鹅é，曲qū项xiàng向xiàng天tiān歌gē”时shí，我wǒ觉jué得de同tóng学xué们men的de目mù光guāng都dōu落luò在zài我wǒ歪wāi着zhe的de脖bó子zi上shàng，让ràng我wǒ非fēi常cháng难nán受shòu。
1In primary school, a car accident left my neck tilted permanently to the right. After that, I became very self-conscious. Once, as our class was reading aloud the line "Goose, goose, goose — neck curving up, singing to the sky," I felt every classmate's eyes fall on my crooked neck. It was unbearable.
2 升shēng上shàng中zhōng学xué后hòu，身shēn边biān的de同tóng学xué都dōu在zài社shè交jiāo媒méi体tǐ上shàng分fēn享xiǎng自zì己jǐ和hé朋péng友yǒu的de照zhào片piān。我wǒ只zhǐ能néng用yòng沉chén默mò和hé独dú来lái独dú往wǎng武wǔ装zhuāng自zì己jǐ，摄shè像xiàng头tóu成chéng了le我wǒ最zuì大dà的de假jiǎ想xiǎng敌dí。因yīn为wèi不bù管guǎn我wǒ怎zěn么me打dǎ扮bàn，也yě掩yǎn饰shì不bù了liǎo自zì己jǐ的de缺quē陷xiàn。
2In secondary school, all my classmates were posting photos of themselves and their friends on social media. I armed myself with silence and kept to myself — the camera lens became the enemy I most feared. Because no matter how I dressed up, I couldn't hide my flaw.
3 一yì天tiān，喜xǐ爱ài摄shè影yǐng的de级jí任rèn老lǎo师shī——陈chén老lǎo师shī，提tí议yì办bàn一yí个gè“展zhǎn现xiàn最zuì美měi的de自zì己jǐ”照zhào片piān展zhǎn。她tā会huì为wèi每měi个gè同tóng学xué拍pāi一yī张zhāng个gè人rén照zhào，然rán后hòu贴tiē在zài布bù告gào栏lán上shàng。大dà家jiā都dōu兴xìng致zhì勃bó勃bó，叽jī叽jī喳zhā喳zhā地dì讨tǎo论lùn着zhe摆bǎi什shén么me姿zī势shì好hǎo。我wǒ的de心xīn却què沉chén到dào了le谷gǔ底dǐ。同tóng学xué们men一yí个gè个gè站zhàn到dào教jiào室shì前qián，大dà方fāng地dì摆bǎi出chū各gè种zhǒng姿zī势shì让ràng陈chén老lǎo师shī拍pāi照zhào。
3One day, our form teacher Ms Chen — a photography enthusiast — proposed a "Show Your Most Beautiful Self" photo exhibition. She would take an individual portrait of every student and put them all up on the classroom noticeboard. Everyone was excited, chattering about what poses they'd strike. My own heart sank to the bottom. One by one my classmates went up to the front of the classroom and confidently struck poses for Ms Chen's camera.
4 “宜yí欣xīn，快kuài来lái拍pāi照zhào！”我wǒ最zuì不bù想xiǎng听tīng到dào的de那nà句jù话huà还hái是shì响xiǎng起qǐ了le。我wǒ慢màn吞tūn吞tūn地dì走zǒu上shàng前qián，拘jū束shù地dì站zhàn在zài讲jiǎng台tái上shàng，心xīn里lǐ一yī片piàn慌huāng乱luàn。原yuán本běn嘈cáo杂zá的de教jiào室shì突tū然rán变biàn得dé无wú比bǐ安ān静jìng。同tóng学xué们men都dōu注zhù视shì着zhe我wǒ，这zhè让ràng我wǒ变biàn得dé更gèng加jiā不bù安ān，脸liǎn红hóng得dé发fā烫tàng。这zhè时shí，陈chén老lǎo师shī走zǒu过guò来lái问wèn道dào：“宜yí欣xīn，你nǐ觉jué得de你nǐ最zuì美měi的de姿zī势shì是shì什shén么me？”
4"Yixin, come on, your turn!" The words I'd been dreading most rang out anyway. Slowly I walked forward and stood stiffly at the front, my heart in a mess. The classroom, a moment earlier full of noise, was suddenly completely silent. Everyone was watching me. It only made me more anxious — my face burned red hot. Then Ms Chen came over and asked, "Yixin, what do you think is your most beautiful pose?"
5 “我wǒ……我wǒ没méi有yǒu什shén么me美měi的de姿zī势shì。”我wǒ沮jǔ丧sàng极jí了le。
5"I… I don't have one," I said, utterly dejected.
6 陈chén老lǎo师shī笑xiào着zhe大dà声shēng说shuō：“你nǐ错cuò了le，这zhè个ge世shì界jiè上shàng每měi个gè人rén都dōu有yǒu自zì己jǐ最zuì美měi丽lì的de姿zī势shì，都dōu可kě以yǐ为wèi这zhè个ge世shì界jiè留liú下xià最zuì美měi的de镜jìng头tóu！”她tā接jiē着zhe对duì大dà家jiā说shuō：“我wǒ们men一yì起qǐ帮bāng宜yí欣xīn设shè计jì一yí个gè最zuì美měi的de姿zī势shì。”
6Ms Chen smiled and said loudly, "You're wrong! Everyone in this world has their own most beautiful pose — everyone can leave behind the loveliest of moments for this world." Then she turned to the class: "Let's all help Yixin design her most beautiful pose together."
7 陈chén老lǎo师shī一yī说shuō完wán，同tóng学xué们men便biàn七qī嘴zuǐ八bā舌shé地dì提tí建jiàn议yì。“宜yí欣xīn的de脸liǎn型xíng美měi，可kě以yǐ侧cè拍pāi！”“可kě以yǐ摆bǎi出chū遥yáo望wàng远yuǎn方fāng的de姿zī势shì！”热rè心xīn的de甜tián甜tián直zhí接jiē跑pǎo过guò来lái，让ràng我wǒ用yòng右yòu手shǒu托tuō住zhù右yòu腮sāi，再zài用yòng左zuǒ手shǒu托tuō着zhe右yòu臂bì，做zuò出chū若ruò有yǒu所suǒ思sī的de样yàng子zi。我wǒ一yī抬tái头tóu，“咔kā嚓chā”一yī声shēng，这zhè个ge姿zī势shì定dìng格gé在zài陈chén老lǎo师shī的de相xiàng机jī里lǐ。
7The moment Ms Chen finished, suggestions came flying from every direction. "Yixin has a lovely face shape — shoot her in profile!" "Do a pose gazing off into the distance!" Warm-hearted Tiantian rushed up, showed me how to rest my right cheek in my right hand and prop my right arm with my left, giving me a pensive, thoughtful look. As I lifted my head — "click" — the pose was captured in Ms Chen's camera.
8 隔gé天tiān早zǎo上shàng，我wǒ一yī进jìn教jiào室shì就jiù看kàn到dào同tóng学xué们men围wéi在zài布bù告gào栏lán前qián。甜tián甜tián一yī看kàn到dào我wǒ便biàn把bǎ我wǒ拉lā过guò去qù，说shuō道dào：“宜yí欣xīn，快kuài看kàn，你nǐ的de照zhào片piān多duō好hǎo看kàn！”其qí他tā同tóng学xué也yě纷fēn纷fēn点diǎn头tóu附fù和hè：“是shì啊a，宜yí欣xīn真zhēn美měi，而ér且qiě看kàn起qǐ来lái好hǎo聪cōng慧huì啊a！”这zhè时shí，不bù知zhī是shì谁shuí开kāi始shǐ鼓gǔ起qǐ掌zhǎng来lái，渐jiàn渐jiàn地dì，掌zhǎng声shēng连lián成chéng一yī片piàn。同tóng学xué们men的de话huà语yǔ和hé掌zhǎng声shēng令lìng我wǒ感gǎn动dòng不bù已yǐ，不bù知zhī不bù觉jué间jiān，泪lèi水shuǐ模mó糊hú了le我wǒ的de视shì线xiàn。
8The next morning, the moment I walked into the classroom, I saw my classmates gathered around the noticeboard. Tiantian spotted me and pulled me over: "Yixin, come see — your photo looks so lovely!" The others nodded along: "Yes — Yixin, you're genuinely beautiful. And you look so thoughtful too!" At that moment, someone started to clap. Bit by bit, the applause grew until it filled the room. Their words and clapping moved me deeply. Before I knew it, my vision had blurred with tears.
9 那nà张zhāng照zhào片piān和hé同tóng学xué们men的de肯kěn定dìng不bù但dàn帮bāng我wǒ找zhǎo到dào最zuì美měi的de姿zī势shì，也yě帮bāng我wǒ找zhǎo回huí了le最zuì宝bǎo贵guì的de信xìn心xīn和hé勇yǒng气qì。原yuán来lái，我wǒ也yě可kě以yǐ很hěn美měi！我wǒ开kāi始shǐ突tū破pò自zì己jǐ的de心xīn防fáng，主zhǔ动dòng与yǔ同tóng学xué交jiāo流liú，还hái结jié交jiāo了le许xǔ多duō好hǎo朋péng友yǒu。
9That photo — and the affirmation from my classmates — didn't just help me find my most beautiful pose. It helped me reclaim my most precious confidence and courage. It turned out: I too could be beautiful. I began to let down the walls I'd put up, started reaching out to my classmates, and made many good friends.
10 其qí实shí，每měi个gè人rén或huò多duō或huò少shǎo都dōu有yǒu不bù完wán美měi的de地dì方fāng。但dàn是shì，我wǒ们men都dōu可kě以yǐ找zhǎo到dào自zì己jǐ最zuì美měi的de姿zī势shì，都dōu可kě以yǐ活huó得dé自zì信xìn，活huó出chū精jīng彩cǎi。
10The truth is, everyone has their imperfections, to one degree or another. But every one of us can find our own most beautiful pose — every one of us can live with confidence, and live a life that shines.
