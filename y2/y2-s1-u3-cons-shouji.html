1 你nǐ多duō久jiǔ换huàn一yī部bù手shǒu机jī？四sì年nián，两liǎng年nián，一yì年nián？你nǐ知zhī道dào吗ma？那nà些xiē被bèi我wǒ们men丢diū弃qì的de手shǒu机jī，大dà部bù分fèn都dōu变biàn成chéng了le电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī。
1How often do you change your phone? Every four years? Two? Every year? Did you know — most of the phones we throw away end up as e-waste.
2 电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī指zhǐ的de是shì被bèi废fèi弃qì的de电diàn器qì或huò电diàn子zi产chǎn品pǐn。据jù统tǒng计jì，在zài这zhè些xiē电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī中zhōng，资zī讯xùn科kē技jì产chǎn品pǐn占zhàn百bǎi分fēn之zhī九jiǔ，家jiā用yòng电diàn器qì占zhàn百bǎi分fēn之zhī七qī十shí六liù。
2E-waste refers to discarded electrical appliances and electronic products. Statistics show that IT products make up 9% of e-waste, and household appliances 76%.

What the World's E-Waste Is Made Of
3 大dà型xíng电diàn器qì 24%；制zhì冷lěng、取qǔ暖nuǎn电diàn器qì 20%；电diàn子zi显xiǎn示shì屏píng 13.5%；资zī讯xùn科kē技jì产chǎn品pǐn 9%；照zhào明míng产chǎn品pǐn 1.5%；小xiǎo型xíng电diàn器qì 32%。
3Large appliances: 24%. Refrigeration and heating equipment: 20%. Screens and displays: 13.5%. IT products: 9%. Lighting: 1.5%. Small appliances: 32%.
4 随suí着zhe科kē技jì日rì新xīn月yuè异yì，可kě供gōng选xuǎn择zé的de电diàn子zi产chǎn品pǐn越yuè来lái越yuè多duō，人rén们men更gēng新xīn电diàn子zi产chǎn品pǐn的de速sù度dù越yuè来lái越yuè快kuài，电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī也yě逐zhú年nián增zēng多duō。统tǒng计jì显xiǎn示shì，2014年nián全quán球qiú电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī总zǒng量liàng为wèi4440万wàn吨dūn，到dào了le2019年nián，增zēng加jiā了le百bǎi分fēn之zhī二èr十shí一yī，预yù计jì在zài2030年nián将jiāng达dá到dào约yuē7400万wàn吨dūn。电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī总zǒng量liàng在zài短duǎn短duǎn的de16年nián内nèi几jī乎hū会huì翻fān一yī番fān。
4As technology advances by the day, people have more and more electronic products to choose from, and replace them faster than ever — so e-waste grows year by year. In 2014, global e-waste totalled 44.4 million tonnes. By 2019, this had risen by 21%, and it is projected to reach about 74 million tonnes by 2030. In just 16 short years, the total will have nearly doubled.
5 电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī泛fàn滥làn成chéng灾zāi的de原yuán因yīn主zhǔ要yào有yǒu两liǎng个gè：一yī是shì电diàn子zi产chǎn品pǐn易yì损sǔn坏huài，使shǐ用yòng寿shòu命mìng短duǎn，而ér且qiě维wéi修xiū途tú径jìng有yǒu限xiàn；二èr是shì人rén们men喜xǐ新xīn厌yàn旧jiù，对duì电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī的de危wēi害hài缺quē乏fá认rèn识shí，因yīn此cǐ不bù断duàn追zhuī求qiú新xīn产chǎn品pǐn，随suí意yì丢diū弃qì旧jiù的de电diàn器qì和hé电diàn子zi产chǎn品pǐn，结jié果guǒ产chǎn生shēng更gèng多duō的de电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī。
5There are two main reasons why e-waste has become a crisis. First: electronics break easily, have short lifespans, and there are few avenues for repair. Second: people love the new and tire of the old, and lack awareness of the damage e-waste causes — so they keep chasing the latest products and casually throw out the old, producing more and more e-waste in the process.
6 其qí实shí大dà部bù分fèn电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī都dōu可kě以yǐ再zài循xún环huán利lì用yòng。一yī部bù智zhì能néng手shǒu机jī的de材cái料liào通tōng常cháng包bāo含hán60种zhǒng金jīn属shǔ元yuán素sù，里lǐ头tou的de贵guì重zhòng金jīn属shǔ，如rú黄huáng金jīn、白bái银yín等děng可kě以yǐ再zài循xún环huán利lì用yòng。数shù据jù显xiǎn示shì，如rú果guǒ把bǎ2016年nián废fèi弃qì手shǒu机jī中zhōng的de金jīn属shǔ全quán部bù回huí收shōu，价jià值zhí可kě达dá95亿yì美měi元yuán。
6In fact, most e-waste can be recycled. The materials in a smartphone typically include 60 different metallic elements, with precious metals like gold and silver that can be reclaimed. If all the metals in the phones thrown away in 2016 had been recovered, their value could have reached US$9.5 billion.
7 2019年nián，全quán球qiú可kě回huí收shōu电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī的de价jià值zhí估gū计jì高gāo达dá570亿yì美měi元yuán，超chāo过guò许xǔ多duō国guó家jiā一yì年nián的de国guó内nèi生shēng产chǎn总zǒng值zhí。遗yí憾hàn的de是shì，这zhè些xiē电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī大dà部bù分fèn都dōu被bèi送sòng进jìn了le填tián埋mái场chǎng，得dé到dào再zài循xún环huán利lì用yòng的de不bú到dào百bǎi分fēn之zhī二èr十shí。
7In 2019, the value of recoverable e-waste worldwide was estimated at US$57 billion — more than many countries' entire annual GDP. Regrettably, most of this e-waste still ends up in landfill. Less than 20% is actually recycled.
8 电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī含hán有yǒu有yǒu毒dú和hé有yǒu害hài的de物wù质zhì，需xū要yào谨jǐn慎shèn处chǔ理lǐ。电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī被bèi填tián埋mái后hòu，有yǒu毒dú物wù质zhì如rú汞gǒng、铅qiān等děng重zhòng金jīn属shǔ会huì污wū染rǎn土tǔ壤rǎng和hé地dì下xià水shuǐ源yuán，影yǐng响xiǎng动dòng植zhí物wù和hé人rén类lèi的de健jiàn康kāng。
8E-waste contains toxic and harmful substances and must be handled carefully. Once buried in landfill, heavy metals like mercury and lead leach into the soil and groundwater, harming plants, animals, and human health.
9 如rú今jīn，越yuè来lái越yuè多duō的de国guó家jiā开kāi始shǐ关guān注zhù电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī的de回huí收shōu问wèn题tí。我wǒ国guó陆lù续xù在zài全quán岛dǎo300多duō个gè地dì点diǎn设shè置zhì电diàn子zǐ垃lā圾jī回huí收shōu箱xiāng，收shōu集jí公gōng众zhòng投tóu放fàng的de废fèi弃qì电diàn子zi产chǎn品pǐn，再zài交jiāo给gěi专zhuān人rén集jí中zhōng处chǔ理lǐ，进jìn行xíng再zài循xún环huán利lì用yòng。
9Today, more and more countries are paying attention to e-waste recycling. Singapore has progressively set up e-waste collection bins at more than 300 locations across the island, gathering used electronics that the public drops off, then passing them to specialists for centralised recycling.
10 下xià次cì想xiǎng要yào更gēng换huàn手shǒu机jī时shí，请qǐng你nǐ三sān思sī而ér行xíng。如rú果guǒ买mǎi了le新xīn手shǒu机jī，记jì得de一yí定dìng要yào妥tuǒ善shàn处chǔ理lǐ旧jiù手shǒu机jī。
10Next time you're thinking of changing your phone, think twice. And if you do buy a new one, remember: dispose of the old one properly.
