1 “我wǒ最zuì讨tǎo厌yàn蒋jiǎng妍yán了le！”这zhè句jù话huà憋biē在zài我wǒ心xīn里lǐ很hěn久jiǔ了le！
1"I cannot stand Jiang Yan!" — I'd been bottling that up inside for the longest time!
2 蒋jiǎng妍yán是shì我wǒ的de室shì友yǒu。虽suī然rán她tā学xué习xí很hěn勤qín奋fèn，但dàn是shì她tā的de一yì些xiē“坏huài习xí惯guàn”，让ràng我wǒ们men几jǐ个gè和hé她tā同tóng住zhù一yí个gè宿sù舍shè的de人rén都dōu讨tǎo厌yàn她tā。她tā每měi晚wǎn都dōu苦kǔ读dú到dào十shí二èr点diǎn，刺cì眼yǎn的de灯dēng光guāng让ràng我wǒ们men无wú法fǎ入rù睡shuì。更gèng让ràng人rén讨tǎo厌yàn的de是shì，我wǒ们men每měi天tiān清qīng晨chén都dōu被bèi她tā的de闹nào钟zhōng吵chǎo醒xǐng！
2Jiang Yan is my roommate. She studies hard, I'll give her that, but some of her "bad habits" made the rest of us sharing the dorm with her want to tear our hair out. Every night she burnt the midnight oil until twelve — her glaring lamp kept the rest of us from sleeping. Even worse, every single morning we got blasted awake by her alarm clock!
3 我wǒ们men委wěi婉wǎn地dì提tí醒xǐng了le她tā多duō次cì，她tā都dōu没méi有yǒu意yì识shí到dào自zì己jǐ的de问wèn题tí。有yǒu一yì天tiān早zǎo晨chén，美měi梦mèng再zài次cì被bèi闹nào钟zhōng吵chǎo醒xǐng，我wǒ朝cháo她tā喊hǎn道dào：“蒋jiǎng妍yán，你nǐ真zhēn的de很hěn讨tǎo厌yàn！这zhè个ge宿sù舍shè不bú是shì你nǐ一yí个gè人rén的de！”
3We'd dropped tactful hints plenty of times, but she just didn't get it. One morning, jolted yet again out of a good dream by her alarm, I finally snapped at her: "Jiang Yan, you are SO annoying! This dorm isn't just for you!"
4 “我wǒ——”蒋jiǎng妍yán看kàn着zhe我wǒ怒nù气qì冲chōng冲chōng的de样yàng子zi，一yī句jù话huà也yě不bù敢gǎn说shuō。这zhè以yǐ后hòu，我wǒ们men俩liǎ彻chè底dǐ成chéng了le“冤yuān家jiā”。本běn来lái我wǒ们men住zhù在zài同tóng一yī个gè宿sù舍shè，抬tái头tóu不bú见jiàn低dī头tóu见jiàn，现xiàn在zài却què谁shuí也yě不bù搭dā理lǐ谁shuí，各gè走zǒu各gè的de路lù。直zhí到dào一yí个gè周zhōu六liù晚wǎn上shàng……
4"I—" Jiang Yan looked at my furious face and didn't dare say another word. After that, she and I became completely at odds. Even though we shared the same dorm and ran into each other constantly, now neither of us would so much as acknowledge the other — we each went our own way. Until, one Saturday evening…
5 那nà天tiān，两liǎng个gè室shì友yǒu回huí家jiā了le，留liú下xià我wǒ们men俩liǎ共gòng处chǔ一yī室shì。我wǒ们men各gè自zì做zuò事shì，谁shuí也yě不bù理lǐ谁shuí。房fáng间jiān里lǐ安ān静jìng得dé可kě以yǐ听tīng到dào彼bǐ此cǐ心xīn跳tiào的de声shēng音yīn。忽hū然rán，门mén外wài传zhuàn来lái扭niǔ动dòng门mén把bà手shǒu的de轻qīng响xiǎng。我wǒ和hé蒋jiǎng妍yán都dōu吓xià了le一yī跳tiào，同tóng时shí朝cháo门mén的de方fāng向xiàng望wàng去qù。门mén慢màn慢màn裂liè开kāi一yī条tiáo缝fèng，一yí个gè人rén影yǐng站zhàn在zài门mén外wài，看kàn不bù清qīng样yàng子zi，像xiàng是shì一yí个gè长cháng发fà披pī散sǎn的de女nǚ生shēng。
5That day, the other two roommates had gone home, leaving just the two of us. We each did our own thing, neither acknowledging the other. It was so quiet in the room you could hear each other's heartbeats. Suddenly — a light sound at the door, like someone was turning the handle. Jiang Yan and I both jumped, and our heads whipped round to the door at the same moment. The door slowly opened just a crack — a figure stood on the other side. We couldn't make out the face clearly; it seemed to be a girl with long, loose hair.
6 “谁shuí？”蒋jiǎng妍yán问wèn。
6"Who's there?" Jiang Yan asked.
7 奇qí怪guài的de是shì，那nà女nǚ生shēng没méi有yǒu出chū声shēng，也yě没méi有yǒu继jì续xù推tuī门mén，而ér是shì微wēi微wēi仰yǎng起qǐ脸liǎn扫sǎo了le我wǒ们men一yī眼yǎn。我wǒ看kàn得dé很hěn清qīng楚chǔ，她tā表biǎo情qíng古gǔ怪guài地dì冲chōng我wǒ笑xiào了le一yí下xià，随suí即jí掩yǎn上shàng门mén。
7Strangely, the girl didn't say a word, didn't push the door open any further. She just tilted her face up slightly and swept a glance at the two of us. I saw it very clearly — with an odd expression, she gave me a smile. Then she quietly pulled the door shut.
8 我wǒ和hé蒋jiǎng妍yán飞fēi快kuài地dì对duì视shì了le一yī眼yǎn，在zài彼bǐ此cǐ的de眼yǎn睛jīng里lǐ都dōu看kàn到dào了le恐kǒng惧jù。我wǒ鼓gǔ起qǐ勇yǒng气qì拉lā开kāi门mén，小xiǎo心xīn地dì探tàn出chū身shēn望wàng了le望wàng，走zǒu廊láng空kōng荡dàng荡dàng的de，什shén么me也yě没méi有yǒu。我wǒ锁suǒ上shàng门mén，冲chōng蒋jiǎng妍yán摇yáo了le摇yáo头tóu，便biàn赶gǎn紧jǐn熄xī灯dēng上shàng床chuáng。
8Jiang Yan and I shot each other a rapid glance — in each other's eyes we both saw fear. Gathering my courage, I pulled the door open and cautiously leaned out to look. The corridor was empty. There was nothing. I locked the door, shook my head at Jiang Yan, and rushed to turn off the lights and get into bed.
9 熄xī灯dēng后hòu大dà约yuē半bàn小xiǎo时shí，黑hēi暗àn中zhōng，蒋jiǎng妍yán问wèn了le一yī句jù：“你nǐ看kàn见jiàn了le吗ma？”
9About half an hour after the lights went out, in the dark, Jiang Yan asked, "Did you see that?"
10 “我wǒ看kàn见jiàn了le。好hǎo可kě怕pà啊a！”我wǒ的de声shēng音yīn有yǒu些xiē颤chàn抖dǒu。
10"I saw it. That was so scary!" My voice trembled slightly.
11 她tā说shuō：“我wǒ也yě看kàn见jiàn了le。你nǐ觉jué得de那nà是shì什shén么me？”我wǒ隐yǐn约yuē看kàn见jiàn蒋jiǎng妍yán缩suō在zài被bèi子zi里lǐ瑟sè瑟sè发fā抖dǒu。
11She said, "I saw it too. What do you think it was?" I could just make out Jiang Yan huddled under her blanket, shivering.
12 蒋jiǎng妍yán接jiē着zhe说shuō：“其qí实shí我wǒ挺tǐng喜xǐ欢huān看kàn鬼guǐ故gù事shì的de，可kě没méi想xiǎng到dào真zhēn有yǒu恐kǒng怖bù事shì件jiàn发fā生shēng时shí，居jū然rán这zhè么me可kě怕pà……”
12Jiang Yan went on: "Actually, I really like reading ghost stories — but I never imagined that when something genuinely creepy happens in real life, it'd be this terrifying…"
13 “你nǐ也yě喜xǐ欢huān读dú鬼guǐ故gù事shì？”我wǒ打dǎ断duàn她tā的de话huà。
13"You like ghost stories too?" I cut in.
14 “当dāng然rán啦la。”
14"Of course!"
15 真zhēn没méi想xiǎng到dào，那nà一yī夜yè，我wǒ竟jìng然rán和hé蒋jiǎng妍yán聊liáo了le个gè通tōng宵xiāo。我wǒ们men发fā现xiàn彼bǐ此cǐ的de共gòng同tóng点diǎn还hái挺tǐng多duō的de，蒋jiǎng妍yán也yě为wèi之zhī前qián的de事shì情qíng跟gēn我wǒ道dào了le歉qiàn。第dì二èr天tiān，我wǒ把bǎ两liǎng本běn恐kǒng怖bù小xiǎo说shuō借jiè给gěi蒋jiǎng妍yán，心xīn中zhōng那nà个ge“讨tǎo厌yàn鬼guǐ”早zǎo就jiù不bú见jiàn了le。
15I couldn't believe it — that night, Jiang Yan and I ended up chatting right through till dawn. We discovered we had quite a lot in common, and she even apologised for everything before. The next day, I lent her two horror novels. The "annoying roommate" in my head had long since vanished.
16 几jǐ天tiān后hòu，我wǒ在zài宿sù舍shè楼lóu下xià遇yù到dào那nà个ge“女nǚ鬼guǐ”。原yuán来lái她tā是shì新xīn来lái的de同tóng学xué，就jiù住zhù在zài楼lóu上shàng，那nà天tiān走zǒu错cuò了le宿sù舍shè。多duō么me惊jīng悚sǒng而ér美měi丽lì的de误wù会huì啊a！
16A few days later, I ran into our "ghost girl" downstairs. Turns out she was a new student who lived on the floor above — that night she'd simply come to the wrong dorm. What a gloriously spine-chilling misunderstanding it had been!
17 因yīn为wèi这zhè起qǐ“恐kǒng怖bù事shì件jiàn”，我wǒ和hé蒋jiǎng妍yán变biàn成chéng了le好hǎo朋péng友yǒu。其qí实shí，人rén与yǔ人rén相xiāng处chǔ，总zǒng会huì出chū现xiàn这zhè样yàng那nà样yàng的de问wèn题tí。很hěn多duō时shí候hòu，只zhǐ要yào多duō点diǎn交jiāo流liú，就jiù可kě以yǐ解jiě决jué这zhè些xiē问wèn题tí，人rén们men之zhī间jiān的de关guān系xì也yě会huì变biàn得dé更gèng和hé谐xié。
17Because of that "horror incident," Jiang Yan and I became close friends. The truth is, when people live together, there are always issues of one kind or another. Often, all it takes is a little more communication to sort them out — and to make our relationships much more harmonious.
