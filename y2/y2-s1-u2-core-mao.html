1 我wǒ养yǎng过guò几jǐ次cì猫māo，结jié局jú都dōu不bù好hǎo，尤yóu其qí是shì最zuì后hòu一yī次cì，让ràng我wǒ至zhì今jīn想xiǎng起qǐ仍réng愧kuì疚jiù不bù已yǐ。
1I've kept cats a few times. Each time ended badly — especially the last, which still fills me with remorse whenever I think of it.
2 那nà年nián冬dōng天tiān的de一yí个gè早zǎo晨chén，家jiā人rén发fā现xiàn门mén口kǒu趴pā着zhe一yī只zhī小xiǎo猫māo。它tā毛máo色sè花huā白bái，没méi有yǒu光guāng泽zé，瘦shòu瘦shòu小xiǎo小xiǎo的de，一yì点diǎn儿ér都dōu不bù好hǎo看kàn。正zhèng是shì寒hán冬dōng天tiān气qì，我wǒ们men如rú不bù收shōu养yǎng，它tā多duō半bàn会huì冻dòng死sǐ。家jiā里lǐ的de帮bāng佣yōng张zhāng妈mā把bǎ它tā抱bào了le进jìn来lái，每měi天tiān给gěi它tā饭fàn吃chī，不bù过guò大dà家jiā都dōu不bù太tài喜xǐ欢huān它tā。它tā不bù活huó泼pō，一yī副fù忧yōu郁yù的de样yàng子zi。连lián女nǚ儿ér那nà样yàng爱ài猫māo的de人rén，对duì它tā也yě不bù愿yuàn多duō加jiā理lǐ睬cǎi。
2One winter morning that year, someone at home noticed a little cat lying at our doorstep. Its fur was patchy white and without any sheen, and it was thin and small — not at all pretty. It was the dead of winter; if we didn't take it in, it would most likely freeze to death. Our housekeeper, Auntie Zhang, carried it inside and fed it every day, but none of us was especially fond of it. It wasn't lively — it always looked somehow melancholy. Even my daughter, who so loved cats, couldn't be bothered to pay it much attention.
3 过guò了le一yī段duàn时shí间jiān，它tā变biàn胖pàng了le，但dàn仍réng然rán不bù活huó泼pō。大dà家jiā在zài院yuàn子zi里lǐ聊liáo天tiān时shí，它tā常cháng趴pā在zài椅yǐ子zi旁páng，时shí而ér懒lǎn懒lǎn地dì打dǎ个gè哈hā欠qiàn。有yǒu一yī晚wǎn，天tiān气qì很hěn冷lěng，它tā竟jìng钻zuān到dào火huǒ炉lú底dǐ下xià取qǔ暖nuǎn，结jié果guǒ毛máo被bèi烧shāo掉diào好hǎo几jǐ块kuài，显xiǎn得de更gèng难nán看kàn了le。
3After a while, it grew plumper, but still wasn't lively. When we all sat chatting in the courtyard, it would often lie beside a chair and, now and then, give a lazy yawn. One very cold night, it crawled right under the stove to warm itself — and ended up with several patches of fur burnt off, leaving it uglier than before.
4 春chūn天tiān来lái了le，它tā变biàn得dé更gèng胖pàng了le，却què从cóng来lái没méi见jiàn过guò它tā去qù捉zhuō老lǎo鼠shǔ。它tā就jiù那nà样yàng终zhōng日rì懒lǎn洋yáng洋yáng地dì趴pā着zhe，显xiǎn得de无wú精jīng打dǎ采cǎi。
4Spring came, and it grew still plumper — yet we never once saw it catch a mouse. Day after day, it just lay around listlessly, looking utterly apathetic.
5 一yì天tiān，妻qī子zǐ买mǎi了le一yī对duì黄huáng鸟niǎo，挂guà在zài院yuàn子zi里lǐ，鸟niǎo儿ér叽jī叽jī啾jiū啾jiū叫jiào得dé很hěn好hǎo听tīng。那nà只zhǐ猫māo对duì这zhè两liǎng只zhǐ鸟niǎo似sì乎hū也yě特tè别bié注zhù意yì，常cháng常cháng跳tiào到dào桌zhuō上shàng，仰yǎng着zhe头tóu，凝níng望wàng着zhe鸟niǎo笼lóng，仿fǎng佛fú在zài想xiǎng着zhe什shén么me。妻qī子zǐ看kàn见jiàn，赶gǎn紧jǐn提tí醒xǐng：“张zhāng妈mā，留liú心xīn猫māo，它tā会huì吃chī鸟niǎo呢ne。”张zhāng妈mā便biàn跑pǎo来lái把bǎ猫māo赶gǎn走zǒu。但dàn隔gé了le一yī会huì儿er，它tā又yòu跳tiào上shàng桌zhuō子zi凝níng望wàng着zhe鸟niǎo笼lóng。
5One day, my wife bought a pair of yellow finches and hung them in the courtyard. Their chirping and tweeting was lovely to hear. The cat seemed to take particular interest in the two birds — it would often jump onto the table, lift its head, and stare up at the cage, as if lost in some thought. When my wife saw this, she quickly warned, "Auntie Zhang, watch that cat — it'll eat the birds." Auntie Zhang would come running to shoo it away. But after a while, the cat would be back on the table, staring up at the cage again.
6 有yǒu一yì天tiān下xià楼lóu时shí，我wǒ突tū然rán听tīng见jiàn张zhāng妈mā喊hǎn道dào：“鸟niǎo死sǐ了le一yī只zhī，一yī条tiáo腿tuǐ没méi了le，笼lóng板bǎn上shàng都dōu是shì血xuè。”我wǒ跑pǎo去qù一yī看kàn，果guǒ然rán，一yī只zhī鸟niǎo死sǐ了le，羽yǔ毛máo松sōng散sǎn着zhe。我wǒ很hěn愤fèn怒nù，直zhí接jiē做zuò出chū了le判pàn断duàn：“一yí定dìng是shì猫māo，一yí定dìng是shì猫māo！”于yú是shì便biàn立lì刻kè去qù找zhǎo它tā。
6One day, as I was coming downstairs, I suddenly heard Auntie Zhang cry out: "One of the birds is dead — a leg is missing, and there's blood all over the bottom of the cage!" I rushed over. Sure enough, one bird was dead, its feathers in disarray. Furious, I jumped straight to the conclusion: "It must have been the cat! It has to be the cat!" And I went to find it at once.
7 大dà家jiā都dōu去qù找zhǎo这zhè只zhǐ可kě恶wù的de猫māo，找zhǎo了le半bàn天tiān，却què没méi找zhǎo到dào。正zhèng当dāng我wǒ们men以yǐ为wéi猫māo“畏wèi罪zuì潜qián逃táo”的de时shí候hòu，女nǚ儿ér在zài楼lóu上shàng找zhǎo到dào了le它tā。
7Everyone searched for that loathsome cat. We searched and searched, but it was nowhere to be found. Just as we were all concluding that the cat had "fled from its crime," my daughter found it upstairs.
8 它tā趴pā在zài露lù台tái上shàng晒shài太tài阳yáng，嘴zuǐ里lǐ好hǎo像xiàng在zài吃chī着zhe什shén么me。它tā一yí定dìng是shì在zài吃chī那nà只zhǐ黄huáng鸟niǎo的de腿tuǐ！我wǒ一yì时shí怒nù气qì冲chōng天tiān，拿ná起qǐ门mén旁páng的de一yī根gēn木mù棒bàng，追zhuī过guò去qù狠hěn狠hěn地dì打dǎ了le它tā一yí下xià。它tā“喵miāo”地dì惨cǎn叫jiào了le一yī声shēng，便biàn狼láng狈bèi地dì逃táo走zǒu了le。
8It was lying on the balcony, sunning itself, seemingly chewing something. It must be eating that yellow finch's leg! My fury rose like smoke to the heavens. I snatched up a wooden stick from beside the door, chased after it, and struck it hard. With a piteous "meow!" it fled, a miserable sight.
9 过guò了le几jǐ天tiān，张zhāng妈mā在zài楼lóu下xià又yòu喊hǎn道dào：“猫māo，猫māo！又yòu来lái吃chī鸟niǎo了le。”就jiù在zài那nà一yī刻kè，我wǒ看kàn见jiàn一yī只zhī黑hēi猫māo飞fēi快kuài地dì跳tiào过guò露lù台tái，嘴zuǐ里lǐ衔xián着zhe一yī只zhī黄huáng鸟niǎo。我wǒ一yí下xià明míng白bái了le：是shì我wǒ搞gǎo错cuò了le。
9A few days later, Auntie Zhang called out from downstairs: "Cat! Cat! It's come to eat the bird again!" At that very moment, I saw a black cat leap across the balcony, a yellow finch held in its beak. All at once I understood — I had got it wrong.
10 我wǒ心xīn里lǐ十shí分fēn难nán过guò。自zì己jǐ没méi弄nòng清qīng楚chǔ，冤yuān枉wǎng了le一yī只zhī无wú法fǎ为wèi自zì己jǐ辩biàn解jiě的de猫māo。
10My heart ached. I hadn't understood what had really happened, and had wronged a cat who could not speak up for itself.
11 一yì年nián过guò去qù了le，我wǒ家jiā的de猫māo始shǐ终zhōng没méi有yǒu回huí来lái。我wǒ猜cāi想xiǎng它tā已yǐ经jīng失shī踪zōng，或huò是shì死sǐ掉diào了le。对duì于yú这zhè只zhǐ猫māo，我wǒ心xīn中zhōng的de愧kuì疚jiù一yì点diǎn儿ér都dōu没méi有yǒu消xiāo减jiǎn，估gū计jì这zhè辈bèi子zi都dōu无wú法fǎ消xiāo除chú了le。偶ǒu尔ěr午wǔ夜yè梦mèng回huí，我wǒ隐yǐn约yuē还hái能néng听tīng到dào那nà声shēng悲bēi楚chǔ的de“喵miāo”。
11A year passed, and our cat never came back. I suppose it was lost, or dead. The guilt I carry for it has not lessened one bit — and I doubt it will be gone from me in this lifetime. Now and then, in the middle of the night, I wake and can still faintly hear that sorrowful "meow."
