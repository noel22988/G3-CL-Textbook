1 雨yǔ树shù，我wǒ们men的de树shù，我wǒ们men无wú处chǔ不bù在zài的de风fēng景jǐng。
1The rain tree — our tree. Our landscape, wherever we go.
2 不bù论lùn何hé时shí，当dāng你nǐ从cóng远yuǎn处chù看kàn她tā，她tā都dōu像xiàng一yī把bǎ大dà伞sǎn，静jìng静jìng地dì为wéi人rén们men遮zhē阳yáng挡dǎng雨yǔ。每měi天tiān早zǎo上shàng，太tài阳yáng出chū来lái后hòu，她tā把bǎ休xiū息xī了le一yī夜yè的de叶yè子zi逐zhú一yī展zhǎn开kāi，然rán后hòu在zài蓝lán天tiān下xià姿zī态tài万wàn千qiān地dì站zhàn出chū一yī路lù风fēng景jǐng。每měi天tiān傍bàng晚wǎn，在zài与yǔ太tài阳yáng亲qīn密mì接jiē触chù一yì天tiān后hòu，不bù知zhī是shì不bú是shì累lèi了le，她tā会huì把bǎ叶yè子zi轻qīng轻qīng合hé上shàng。下xià雨yǔ前qián，天tiān色sè转zhuǎn黑hēi，她tā便biàn从cóng容róng地dì把bǎ翠cuì绿lǜ的de叶yè子zi关guān上shàng，等děng待dài风fēng雨yǔ的de来lái临lín。所suǒ以yǐ，她tā有yǒu一yí个gè非fēi常cháng浪làng漫màn的de名míng字zì——雨yǔ树shù。
2From afar, at any hour, she looks like a great umbrella, quietly shading people from sun and rain. Each morning, once the sun has risen, she unfolds her leaves one by one after a night's rest, and stands beneath the blue sky in a thousand graceful poses, carving out a stretch of scenery. Each evening, after a day in close contact with the sun — perhaps weary — she gently folds her leaves up. Before the rain, when the sky turns dark, she calmly closes her emerald leaves and waits for the wind and rain to arrive. And so she has a very romantic name: the rain tree.
3 如rú果guǒ你nǐ住zhù的de地dì方fāng比bǐ雨yǔ树shù高gāo，那nà么me，当dāng她tā的de花huā季jì来lái临lín时shí，你nǐ居jū高gāo临lín下xià，可kě以yǐ看kàn到dào大dà片piàn粉fěn红hóng的de花huā海hǎi。在zài清qīng风fēng里lǐ，花huā儿ér轻qīng轻qīng柔róu柔róu地dì摇yáo摆bǎi，随suí着zhe叶yè子zi发fā出chū的de沙shā沙shā声shēng响xiǎng，晃huàng动dòng成chéng一yī波bō波bō诗shī意yì的de粉fěn红hóng之zhī浪làng，令lìng人rén心xīn旷kuàng神shén怡yí。
3If you live higher up than the rain tree, then when flowering season comes, looking down you see a great pink sea of blossoms. In a gentle breeze, the flowers sway softly; with the rustling of the leaves, they ripple into waves of poetic pink, refreshing both heart and spirit.
4 雨yǔ树shù，是shì新xīn加jiā坡pō无wú处chǔ不bù在zài的de风fēng景jǐng。空kōng旷kuàng的de停tíng车chē场chǎng里lǐ，她tā为wèi晒shài在zài烈liè日rì下xià的de汽qì车chē遮zhē阳yáng；宽kuān阔kuò的de草cǎo场chǎng边biān，她tā把bǎ炎yán阳yáng遮zhē挡dǎng成chéng柔róu柔róu的de凉liáng意yì；长cháng长cháng的de道dào路lù两liǎng旁páng，她tā用yòng枝zhī干gàn繁fán叶yè合hé拢lǒng成chéng一yī片piàn美měi丽lì的de林lín荫yīn大dà道dào。
4The rain tree is a landscape woven through all of Singapore. In open car parks, she shades cars baking in the fierce sun. At the edge of broad playing fields, she softens the blazing heat into a gentle coolness. Along long stretches of road, her spreading boughs close in overhead to form beautiful tree-lined avenues.
5 近jìn年nián来lái我wǒ经jīng常cháng出chū国guó，每měi次cì在zài前qián往wǎng樟zhāng宜yí机jī场chǎng的de东dōng海hǎi岸àn公gōng园yuán大dà道dào上shàng，雨yǔ树shù总zǒng是shì默mò默mò地dì耸sǒng立lì在zài一yī旁páng给gěi我wǒ祝zhù福fú。望wàng着zhe我wǒ渐jiàn行xíng渐jiàn远yuǎn的de背bèi影yǐng，她tā似sì乎hū知zhī道dào我wǒ一yí定dìng会huì回huí来lái，总zǒng在zài身shēn后hòu温wēn柔róu地dì提tí醒xǐng：“出chū国guó在zài外wài，自zì己jǐ保bǎo重zhòng！”每měi当dāng我wǒ双shuāng足zú踏tà上shàng家jiā乡xiāng的de土tǔ地dì，她tā又yòu以yǐ等děng待dài孩hái子zi归guī来lái的de注zhù视shì，温wēn柔róu地dì对duì我wǒ张zhāng开kāi欢huān迎yíng的de双shuāng臂bì，让ràng我wǒ倍bèi感gǎn家jiā的de温wēn馨xīn。
5In recent years I've been travelling abroad often. Every time I'm on the East Coast Parkway heading to Changi Airport, the rain tree stands silently at the roadside giving me her blessing. Watching my figure recede into the distance, she seems to know that I'll definitely come back — always, softly, behind me, reminding: "Travel safely, look after yourself out there." And every time my feet touch the soil of home again, with a gaze like a parent watching for a child's return, she gently opens her arms in welcome, filling me with the warmth of home.
6 我wǒ很hěn欣xīn赏shǎng雨yǔ树shù有yǒu别bié于yú其qí他tā树shù种zhǒng的de千qiān姿zī百bǎi态tài。有yǒu时shí候hòu我wǒ站zhàn在zài树shù下xià，出chū神shén地dì看kàn着zhe她tā粗cū糙cāo的de枝zhī干gàn。看kàn着zhe，看kàn着zhe，她tā竟jìng然rán变biàn成chéng了le姿zī态tài婆pó娑suō的de舞wǔ蹈dǎo员yuán。雨yǔ树shù的de枝zhī干gàn看kàn似shì随suí意yì生shēng长zhǎng，却què可kě以yǐ长zhǎng出chū一yì些xiē非fēi常cháng特tè别bié的de姿zī势shì：或huò似shì楚chǔ楚chǔ可kě人rén的de美měi女nǚ；或huò如rú撑chēng起qǐ半bàn边biān天tiān的de壮zhuàng汉hàn；或huò像xiàng顽wán童tóng一yì般bān跟gēn鸟niǎo儿ér玩wán捉zhuō迷mí藏cáng。她tā褐hè色sè的de根gēn也yě常cháng常cháng随suí性xìng地dì从cóng泥ní土tǔ里lǐ冒mào出chū来lái，向xiàng四sì处chù伸shēn展zhǎn。
6I love how the rain tree, unlike other kinds of tree, takes on a thousand different forms. Sometimes I stand beneath her, lost in looking at her rough-barked limbs. Look long enough, and she becomes a swaying dancer. Her branches seem to grow every which way, and yet they form the most particular poses: sometimes like a delicate beauty; sometimes like a strong man holding up half the sky; sometimes like a playful child playing hide-and-seek with birds. Her brown roots, too, often poke up out of the soil as they please, stretching out in all directions.
7 雨yǔ树shù非fēi常cháng大dà方fāng地dì让ràng许xǔ许xǔ多duō多duō的de植zhí物wù生shēng长zhǎng在zài她tā的de身shēn上shàng，彼bǐ此cǐ同tóng甘gān共gòng苦kǔ，茁zhuó壮zhuàng成chéng长zhǎng。雨yǔ树shù与yǔ她tā这zhè些xiē“孩hái子zi们men”，不bù就jiù像xiàng新xīn加jiā坡pō与yǔ新xīn移yí民mín一yí样yàng吗ma？彼bǐ此cǐ包bāo容róng、接jiē纳nà、齐qí心xīn、奉fèng献xiàn，为wèi美měi化huà家jiā园yuán共gòng同tóng努nǔ力lì。
7The rain tree is generous — she lets all sorts of other plants grow on her body, and together they share the sweet and the bitter, thriving side by side. Aren't the rain tree and her "children" rather like Singapore and its new immigrants? Tolerating each other, accepting each other, pulling together, giving freely — working side by side to make our home more beautiful.
8 雨yǔ树shù无wú怨yuàn无wú悔huǐ地dì承chéng受shòu着zháo风fēng吹chuī日rì晒shài雨yǔ淋lín，只zhǐ为wèi给gěi我wǒ们men一yī路lù风fēng景jǐng，一yī程chéng舒shū适shì，一yī生shēng温wēn暖nuǎn。
8Without resentment or regret, the rain tree endures the wind and sun and rain — all to give us our scenery along the way, our comfort on the journey, warmth across a lifetime.
9 雨yǔ树shù，你nǐ姿zī态tài万wàn千qiān，包bāo容róng、奉fèng献xiàn，你nǐ是shì我wǒ们men的de树shù，我wǒ们men无wú处chǔ不bù在zài的de风fēng景jǐng！
9Rain tree — you of the thousand poses, of tolerance, of giving — you are our tree, our landscape, wherever we go!
