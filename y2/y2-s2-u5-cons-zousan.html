《如果走散了》 If We Get Separated

语速 Speed 0.9× (set before play)

1 母mǔ亲qīn去qù年nián患huàn上shàng了le失shī智zhì症zhèng，不bú适shì合hé独dú居jū，于yú是shì我wǒ特tè地dì把bǎ她tā接jiē过guò来lái一yì起qǐ住zhù，还hái请qǐng了le一yī位wèi女nǚ佣yōng来lái照zhào顾gù她tā。原yuán以yǐ为wéi一yī切qiè安ān排pái得dé很hěn周zhōu全quán，没méi想xiǎng到dào还hái是shì出chū了le问wèn题tí。
1My mother was diagnosed with dementia last year, and living alone was no longer safe for her. So I made a point of bringing her to live with us, and hired a domestic helper to care for her. I thought I'd arranged everything properly — but something still went wrong.
2 那nà天tiān工gōng作zuò时shí接jiē到dào妻qī子zǐ打dǎ来lái的de电diàn话huà：“妈mā妈mā不bú见jiàn了le！”我wǒ一yī挂guà了le电diàn话huà便biàn飞fēi奔bēn回huí家jiā。
2That day at work, I got a call from my wife: "Ma has gone missing!" The moment I hung up, I rushed home.
3 我wǒ在zài组zǔ屋wū楼lóu下xià看kàn见jiàn女nǚ佣yōng和hé妻qī子zǐ，两liǎng人rén神shén色sè凝níng重zhòng。妻qī子zǐ说shuō：“我wǒ打dǎ电diàn话huà给gěi小xiǎo姑gū了le，妈mā妈mā没méi去qù找zhǎo她tā。她tā很hěn担dān心xīn，说shuō要yào过guò来lái一yì起qǐ找zhǎo妈mā妈mā。”
3Downstairs in front of our HDB block I found our helper and my wife, their faces grave. My wife said, "I've called your sister — Ma didn't go to her place. She's very worried, she's coming over to help look."
4 我wǒ们men四sì处chù寻xún找zhǎo，却què始shǐ终zhōng不bú见jiàn母mǔ亲qīn的de踪zōng影yǐng。不bù久jiǔ，妹mèi妹mèi也yě来lái了le，跟gēn着zhe一yì起qǐ找zhǎo，她tā还hái在zài社shè交jiāo媒méi体tǐ上shàng发fā了le寻xún人rén启qǐ事shì。
4We searched everywhere, but there was no sign of Ma. Before long, my sister arrived to help search, and she also put up a missing-person notice on social media.
5 随suí着zhe时shí间jiān的de流liú逝shì，我wǒ的de心xīn情qíng也yě越yuè来lái越yuè沉chén重zhòng。正zhèng当dāng我wǒ要yào报bào警jǐng时shí，手shǒu机jī响xiǎng了le。
5As time slipped by, my heart grew heavier and heavier. Just as I was about to call the police, my phone rang.
6 电diàn话huà是shì我wǒ小xiǎo时shí候hòu的de邻lín居jū方fāng阿ā姨yí打dǎ来lái的de。“阿ā达dá，我wǒ在zài网wǎng上shàng看kàn到dào你nǐ在zài找zhǎo妈mā妈mā。她tā回huí到dào我wǒ们men这zhè个ge组zǔ屋wū区qū了le。”
6It was from Auntie Fang, our neighbour from when I was a child. "Ah Da, I saw on the internet that you're looking for your mum. She's come back to our old HDB estate."
7 我wǒ们men连lián忙máng开kāi车chē前qián往wǎng北běi部bù，那nà是shì我wǒ小xiǎo时shí候hòu居jū住zhù的de地dì方fāng。途tú中zhōng，妹mèi妹mèi突tū然rán说shuō：“妈mā妈mā一yí定dìng是shì去qù了le那nà个ge游yóu乐lè场chǎng。”
7We quickly drove north — that's where I'd lived as a child. On the way, my sister suddenly said, "Ma must have gone to that playground."
8 “哪nǎ个ge游yóu乐lè场chǎng？”我wǒ不bù解jiě地dì问wèn。
8"Which playground?" I asked, puzzled.
9 “就jiù是shì我wǒ们men小xiǎo时shí候hòu常cháng去qù玩wán的de那nà个ge。那nà时shí妈mā妈mā带dài我wǒ们men上shàng巴bā刹shā，总zǒng会huì提tí醒xǐng我wǒ们men，如rú果guǒ走zǒu散sàn了le，就jiù到dào游yóu乐lè场chǎng会huì合hé。”我wǒ的de脑nǎo海hǎi里lǐ顿dùn时shí浮fú现xiàn出chū以yǐ前qián和hé妹mèi妹mèi在zài游yóu乐lè场chǎng嬉xī戏xì的de画huà面miàn，那nà里lǐ是shì我wǒ们men儿ér时shí的de乐lè园yuán。那nà时shí候hòu，母mǔ亲qīn常cháng会huì坐zuò在zài游yóu乐lè场chǎng边biān的de长zhǎng椅yǐ上shàng，一yì边biān看kàn我wǒ们men玩wán耍shuǎ，一yì边biān和hé邻lín居jū话huà家jiā常cháng。
9"The one we used to go to all the time when we were little. Whenever Ma took us to the wet market, she'd always remind us: if we got separated, we were to meet her at the playground." An image flooded my mind — my sister and I, playing at that playground. It had been the paradise of our childhood. Back then, Ma would often sit on the bench beside the playground, watching us play while chatting with the neighbours.
10 妹mèi妹mèi猜cāi得dé没méi错cuò，妈mā妈mā果guǒ然rán在zài那nà个ge游yóu乐lè场chǎng，她tā正zhèng和hé几jǐ位wèi老lǎo邻lín居jū聊liáo天tiān。
10My sister had guessed right — Ma was indeed at the playground, chatting away with some of the old neighbours.
11 母mǔ亲qīn看kàn到dào我wǒ们men，慈cí祥xiáng地dì笑xiào着zhe说shuō：“阿ā达dá、阿ā妹mèi，你nǐ们men刚gāng刚gāng跑pǎo去qù哪nǎ里lǐ了le？你nǐ们men看kàn，这zhè是shì方fāng阿ā姨yí给gěi我wǒ买mǎi的de咖gā喱lí卜bo。我wǒ知zhī道dào你nǐ们men爱ài吃chī，给gěi你nǐ们men留liú着zhe呢ne！还hái好hǎo你nǐ们men记jì得de来lái游yóu乐lè场chǎng会huì合hé。你nǐ们men先xiān去qù游yóu乐lè场chǎng玩wán一yī玩wán吧ba，我wǒ跟gēn邻lín居jū们men聊liáo聊liáo天tiān。”
11Seeing us, Ma smiled her kindly smile: "Ah Da, Ah Mei, where did the two of you run off to? Look — Auntie Fang bought me some curry puffs. I know you love them, I've saved some for you. Good thing you remembered to meet me at the playground. Go and play for a bit first — I'll keep chatting with the neighbours."
12 我wǒ和hé妹mèi妹mèi不bù约yuē而ér同tóng地dì望wàng向xiàng游yóu乐lè场chǎng。依yī然rán是shì那nà艘sōu气qì派pài十shí足zú的de大dà船chuán，上shàng面miàn耸sǒng立lì着zhe高gāo高gāo的de桅wéi杆gān。虽suī然rán大dà船chuán显xiǎn得de有yǒu些xiē老lǎo旧jiù，但dàn桅wéi杆gān上shàng仍réng然rán挂guà着zhe俏qiào皮pí的de红hóng色sè船chuán帆fān。两liǎng个gè孩hái子zi围wéi着zhe波bō浪làng滑huá梯tī，你nǐ追zhuī我wǒ赶gǎn。旁páng边biān两liǎng个gè年nián纪jì小xiǎo一yī点diǎn的de孩hái子zi正zhèng开kāi心xīn地dì荡dàng着zhe秋qiū千qiān。随suí着zhe他tā们men的de一yì起qǐ一yī落luò，秋qiū千qiān发fā出chū咿yī咿yī呀yā呀ya的de声shēng音yīn。不bù远yuǎn处chù有yǒu一yī条tiáo长zhǎng椅yǐ，两liǎng位wèi妈mā妈mā坐zuò在zài那nà里lǐ聊liáo天tiān。她tā们men不bù时shí转zhuǎn头tóu看kàn一yī看kàn游yóu乐lè场chǎng上shàng的de孩hái童tóng，显xiǎn得de轻qīng松sōng自zì在zài。
12Without needing to say a word, my sister and I both looked towards the playground. There was still that grand old ship, a tall mast rising from its deck. The ship looked a bit weatherworn, but the cheeky red sail still hung from the mast. Two children chased each other around the wave slide. Beside them, two smaller children swung on the swings, happy as anything — the swings creaking softly with every back-and-forth. Not far off was a bench where two mothers sat chatting, glancing now and then at the children on the playground, looking perfectly at ease.
13 望wàng着zhe这zhè个ge充chōng满mǎn欢huān乐lè与yǔ温wēn馨xīn的de游yóu乐lè场chǎng，我wǒ再zài次cì想xiǎng起qǐ了le童tóng年nián的de美měi好hǎo时shí光guāng。虽suī然rán游yóu乐lè场chǎng的de色sè彩cǎi已yǐ经jīng不bù如rú往wǎng日rì鲜xiān艳yàn，但dàn它tā仍réng然rán承chéng载zài着zhe我wǒ们men的de快kuài乐lè，也yě寄jì托tuō着zhe母mǔ亲qīn对duì那nà段duàn岁suì月yuè的de怀huái念niàn。
13Looking at this playground, so full of joy and warmth, my childhood days came back to me. The colours of the playground had faded from what they once were, but it still held all of our happiness — and carried with it, too, my mother's longing for that time.
14 我wǒ看kàn了le看kàn妹mèi妹mèi，她tā转zhuǎn过guò头tóu去qù迎yíng着zhe晚wǎn风fēng，似sì乎hū想xiǎng让ràng风fēng吹chuī干gàn眼yǎn角jiǎo的de泪lèi痕hén。我wǒ的de心xīn中zhōng也yě有yǒu所suǒ触chù动dòng，之zhī前qián的de焦jiāo虑lǜ随suí之zhī消xiāo散sàn无wú踪zōng。我wǒ走zǒu到dào母mǔ亲qīn身shēn边biān，柔róu声shēng地dì对duì她tā说shuō道dào：“妈mā，我wǒ们men回huí家jiā吧ba。”
14I glanced at my sister. She had turned her face into the evening breeze, as if letting the wind dry the traces of tears at the corners of her eyes. My heart, too, was moved — and all the anxiety from before simply vanished. I walked over to Ma and said softly, "Ma, let's go home."
