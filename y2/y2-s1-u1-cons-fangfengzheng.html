《放风筝》 Flying a Kite

语速 Speed 0.9× (set before play)

生词 Vocabulary · 9 words

1 星xīng期qī二èr晚wǎn上shàng，我wǒ在zài同tóng学xué家jiā做zuò小xiǎo组zǔ作zuò业yè，因yīn为wèi太tài投tóu入rù，没méi有yǒu注zhù意yì时shí间jiān。十shí点diǎn半bàn左zuǒ右yòu，我wǒ才cái发fā现xiàn手shǒu机jī里lǐ竟jìng然rán有yǒu五wǔ个gè未wèi接jiē来lái电diàn！糟zāo糕gāo，都dōu是shì妈mā妈mā打dǎ来lái的de！
1Tuesday evening, I was at a classmate's house working on a group project. I was so absorbed I didn't notice the time. Around 10.30, I suddenly saw five missed calls on my phone! Oh no — they were all from Mum!
2 我wǒ赶gǎn紧jǐn收shōu拾shí东dōng西xī离lí开kāi。一yī回huí到dào家jiā，我wǒ就jiù看kàn到dào妈mā妈mā和hé爸bà爸bà坐zuò在zài沙shā发fā上shàng。妈mā妈mā紧jǐn皱zhòu眉méi头tou，表biǎo情qíng严yán肃sù；爸bà爸bà则zé两liǎng眼yǎn布bù满mǎn血xuè丝sī，神shén色sè焦jiāo虑lǜ。妈mā妈mā一yī见jiàn到dào我wǒ，就jiù一yī脸liǎn不bú悦yuè地dì责zé问wèn我wǒ：“你nǐ为wèi什shén么me这zhè么me晚wǎn才cái回huí家jiā？连lián电diàn话huà也yě不bù接jiē？”爸bà爸bà连lián忙máng拍pāi拍pāi妈mā妈mā安ān抚fǔ她tā，关guān心xīn地dì问wèn我wǒ：“怎zěn么me这zhè么me晚wǎn才cái回huí家jiā？吃chī晚wǎn餐cān了le吗ma？”
2I quickly packed up and left. The moment I got home, I saw Mum and Dad sitting on the sofa. Mum had her brows tightly furrowed and a stern expression; Dad's eyes were bloodshot, his face anxious. The instant Mum saw me, she snapped: "Why are you home so late? And why aren't you answering your phone?" Dad patted her shoulder to calm her down, then asked with concern, "How come you're back so late? Have you had dinner?"
3 我wǒ想xiǎng解jiě释shì，妈mā妈mā却què不bù听tīng，继jì续xù责zé备bèi我wǒ。我wǒ干gān脆cuì一yī句jù话huà也yě不bù说shuō，快kuài步bù走zǒu进jìn房fáng间jiān，重chóng重chóng地dì把bǎ门mén关guān上shàng。我wǒ感gǎn到dào很hěn委wěi屈qū。我wǒ又yòu不bú是shì故gù意yì晚wǎn回huí家jiā。妈mā妈mā根gēn本běn不bù听tīng我wǒ解jiě释shì，真zhēn是shì太tài不bù讲jiǎng理lǐ了le！
3I tried to explain, but Mum wouldn't listen — she just kept scolding. So I simply said nothing, strode off to my room, and slammed the door shut. I felt so wronged. I hadn't come home late on purpose. Mum wouldn't even hear me out — it was so unreasonable!
4 接jiē下xià来lái的de几jǐ天tiān，我wǒ和hé妈mā妈mā一yì直zhí冷lěng战zhàn。爸bà爸bà劝quàn我wǒ向xiàng妈mā妈mā道dào歉qiàn，我wǒ不bù愿yuàn意yì。妈mā妈mā也yě有yǒu错cuò，凭píng什shén么me要yào我wǒ先xiān道dào歉qiàn？
4For the next few days, Mum and I were in a cold war. Dad urged me to apologise to her, but I refused. Mum was in the wrong too — why should I be the one to apologise first?
5 星xīng期qī天tiān早zǎo上shàng，爸bà爸bà说shuō一yì起qǐ去qù海hǎi边biān放fàng风fēng筝zhēng。我wǒ本běn来lái很hěn高gāo兴xìng，但dàn是shì听tīng到dào妈mā妈mā也yě要yào去qù，心xīn里lǐ就jiù有yǒu些xiē别biè扭niǔ。我wǒ还hái没méi和hé妈mā妈mā和hé好hǎo，她tā怎zěn么me也yě要yào去qù？
5Sunday morning, Dad suggested we all go to the beach to fly a kite. I was excited at first — but when I heard Mum was coming too, I felt uncomfortable. I hadn't made up with her yet; why was she coming?
6 来lái到dào海hǎi边biān，妈mā妈mā帮bāng我wǒ整zhěng理lǐ好hǎo风fēng筝zhēng，把bǎ它tā交jiāo给gěi我wǒ，自zì己jǐ则zé拉lā着zháo风fēng筝zhēng线xiàn走zǒu到dào不bù远yuǎn处chù。我wǒ举jǔ起qǐ风fēng筝zhēng向xiàng前qián跑pǎo，手shǒu一yī松sōng，风fēng筝zhēng便biàn摇yáo摇yáo晃huàng晃huǎng地dì飞fēi上shàng了le天tiān空kōng。
6At the beach, Mum got the kite ready for me, handed it over, then walked a little distance away holding the kite string. I lifted the kite, ran forward, let go — and the kite wobbled up into the sky.
7 妈mā妈mā微wēi笑xiào着zhe将jiāng风fēng筝zhēng线xiàn放fàng到dào我wǒ手shǒu里lǐ，让ràng我wǒ来lái放fàng，我wǒ也yě尴gān尬gà地dì对duì她tā笑xiào了le笑xiào。突tū然rán一yí阵zhèn风fēng吹chuī来lái，风fēng筝zhēng开kāi始shǐ在zài空kōng中zhōng上shàng下xià摇yáo摆bǎi。我wǒ连lián忙máng把bǎ风fēng筝zhēng线xiàn往wǎng回huí拉lā，想xiǎng不bú到dào风fēng筝zhēng却què像xiàng个gè不bù听tīng话huà的de孩hái子zi，用yòng力lì挣zhèng脱tuō。线xiàn断duàn了le，风fēng筝zhēng越yuè飘piāo越yuè远yuǎn，最zuì后hòu落luò在zài矮ǎi树shù丛cóng中zhōng。
7Mum smiled and placed the string in my hand, letting me fly it. I gave her an awkward smile back. Suddenly a gust of wind came, and the kite began to bob wildly. I quickly pulled in the string — but the kite, like a disobedient child, yanked itself free. The string snapped, the kite drifted further and further away, and finally came down in a patch of low bushes.
8 妈mā妈mā赶gǎn紧jǐn走zǒu向xiàng树shù丛cóng帮bāng我wǒ捡jiǎn风fēng筝zhēng。爸bà爸bà慈cí爱ài地dì看kàn着zhe我wǒ说shuō：“是shì妈mā妈mā提tí议yì出chū来lái放fàng风fēng筝zhēng的de。她tā说shuō你nǐ考kǎo试shì压yā力lì大dà，需xū要yào解jiě压yā。”我wǒ有yǒu点diǎn儿ér惊jīng讶yà，妈mā妈mā最zuì不bù喜xǐ欢huān晒shài太tài阳yáng，担dān心xīn脸liǎn上shàng长zhǎng斑bān，怎zěn么me会huì主zhǔ动dòng说shuō要yào放fàng风fēng筝zhēng？
8Mum hurried over to the bushes to retrieve the kite for me. Dad looked at me with tender eyes and said, "It was Mum's idea to come fly kites. She said you've been under a lot of exam stress and needed to unwind." I was a little surprised. Mum hates being out in the sun — she worries about getting sun spots on her face — why would she have been the one to suggest this?
9 我wǒ和hé爸bà爸bà跟gēn了le过guò去qù。妈mā妈mā弯wān着zhe腰yāo，聚jù精jīng会huì神shén地dì解jiě着zhe缠chán绕rào在zài树shù丛cóng中zhōng的de风fēng筝zhēng线xiàn，额é头tóu上shàng都dōu是shì豆dòu大dà的de汗hàn珠zhū。爸bà爸bà搂lǒu着zhe我wǒ的de肩jiān膀bǎng说shuō：“你nǐ想xiǎng想xiǎng，自zì己jǐ像xiàng不bù像xiàng风fēng筝zhēng？风fēng筝zhēng线xiàn握wò在zài他tā人rén手shǒu里lǐ，风fēng筝zhēng觉jué得de不bù自zì由yóu，总zǒng是shì想xiǎng挣zhèng脱tuō；挣zhèng脱tuō后hòu，它tā虽suī然rán有yǒu了le自zì由yóu，却què再zài也yě无wú法fǎ高gāo飞fēi了le！”
9Dad and I followed over. Mum was bent over, intently working to untangle the kite string from the bushes, beads of sweat on her forehead as big as soybeans. Dad put his arm around my shoulders and said, "Think about it — aren't you a bit like the kite? The string is in someone else's hands; the kite feels unfree, always wanting to break loose. Once it does break loose — yes, it has freedom — but it can never fly high again."
10 爸bà爸bà的de话huà让ràng我wǒ如rú梦mèng初chū醒xǐng。是shì啊a，孩hái子zi不bù就jiù是shì父fù母mǔ手shǒu中zhōng的de风fēng筝zhēng吗ma？
10Dad's words woke me as if from a dream. Yes — isn't a child just like a kite in a parent's hand?
