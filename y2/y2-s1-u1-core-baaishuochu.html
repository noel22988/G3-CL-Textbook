1 女nǚ儿ér常cháng常cháng称chēng赞zàn我wǒ。我wǒ文wén章zhāng写xiě好hǎo了le，念niàn给gěi她tā听tīng，她tā总zǒng是shì带dài着zhe崇chóng拜bài的de眼yǎn神shén，再zài三sān赞zàn叹tàn：“妈mā，您nín写xiě得dé真zhēn好hǎo！您nín真zhēn的de好hǎo棒bàng！”之zhī后hòu她tā还hái会huì把bǎ文wén章zhāng拿ná过guò去qù再zài看kàn一yī遍biàn，一yī副fù爱ài不bù释shì手shǒu的de模mú样yàng，让ràng我wǒ很hěn有yǒu成chéng就jiù感gǎn。
1My daughter is always praising me. When I finish writing an article and read it to her, she always looks up with admiring eyes and exclaims over and over, "Mum, this is so good! You're amazing!" Then she'll take the article and read it again, looking like she can't put it down — it gives me such a sense of accomplishment.
2 她tā哥gē哥gē与yǔ她tā截jié然rán相xiāng反fǎn。要yào是shì把bǎ文wén章zhāng念niàn给gěi他tā听tīng，一yí定dìng会huì碰pèng钉dīng子zi，他tā会huì边biān逃táo边biān说shuō：“我wǒ不bù想xiǎng听tīng，千qiān万wàn别bié念niàn给gěi我wǒ听tīng。”
2Her older brother is the complete opposite. If I tried to read my articles to him, I'd get nothing but a brush-off. He'd back away saying, "I don't want to hear it — whatever you do, don't read it to me."
3 偶ǒu尔ěr买mǎi了le新xīn衣yī服fú，我wǒ穿chuān上shàng后hòu，女nǚ儿ér总zǒng会huì夸kuā赞zàn：“妈mā，您nín的de身shēn材cái真zhēn好hǎo，穿chuān上shàng这zhè件jiàn衣yī服fú真zhēn好hǎo看kàn！”看kàn到dào女nǚ儿ér双shuāng眼yǎn含hán笑xiào，一yī脸liǎn欣xīn赏shǎng的de样yàng子zi，我wǒ的de心xīn情qíng也yě变biàn得dé无wú比bǐ愉yú快kuài。
3When I occasionally buy a new outfit and try it on, my daughter always raves, "Mum, you look great — this outfit really suits you!" Seeing her smiling eyes and that look of genuine admiration, my mood lifts no end.
4 她tā的dī哥gē哥gē却què经jīng常cháng泼pō冷lěng水shuǐ：“妈mā，您nín别bié相xiāng信xìn妹mèi妹mèi的de甜tián言yán蜜mì语yǔ，她tā是shì在zài拍pāi马mǎ屁pì！”
4Her brother, meanwhile, is forever throwing cold water on it: "Mum, don't fall for your daughter's sweet talk — she's just buttering you up!"
5 不bù管guǎn怎zěn么me说shuō，相xiāng比bǐ之zhī下xià，女nǚ儿ér充chōng满mǎn感gǎn情qíng的de话huà语yǔ肯kěn定dìng更gèng让ràng人rén受shòu用yòng。我wǒ的de抽chōu屉tì里lǐ装zhuāng满mǎn了le女nǚ儿ér写xiě给gěi我wǒ的de各gè式shì各gè样yàng的de卡kǎ片piàn。有yǒu时shí我wǒ工gōng作zuò了le一yī整zhěng天tiān，精jīng疲pí力lì竭jié地dì回huí到dào家jiā，看kàn到dào女nǚ儿ér的de卡kǎ片piàn，就jiù觉jué得de再zài辛xīn苦kǔ也yě是shì值zhí得de的de。
5Whatever he says, her warm, affectionate words are much nicer to hear. My drawers are filled with cards of every kind that she has made for me. Sometimes, when I come home utterly exhausted after a long day at work, seeing one of her cards makes me feel that every bit of hard work was worth it.
6 那nà年nián，父fù亲qīn过guò世shì，母mǔ亲qīn一yí下xià子zi衰shuāi老lǎo了le。她tā每měi天tiān心xīn情qíng抑yì郁yù，沉chén默mò寡guǎ言yán。为wèi了le解jiě除chú她tā的de寂jì寞mò，我wǒ接jiē她tā来lái和hé我wǒ们men同tóng住zhù。母mǔ亲qīn来lái了le后hòu，不bù愿yuàn闲xián着zhe，每měi天tiān抢qiǎng着zhe帮bāng我wǒ煮zhǔ饭fàn做zuò菜cài，打dǎ理lǐ家jiā务wù，大dà大dà减jiǎn轻qīng了le我wǒ的de负fù担dān。
6The year my father passed away, my mother seemed to age overnight. Every day she was sunk in gloom, saying little. To ease her loneliness, I brought her to live with us. Once she moved in, she refused to sit idle — every day she insisted on helping with the cooking and housework, taking a huge load off me.
7 一yì天tiān中zhōng午wǔ，我wǒ开kāi车chē回huí家jiā吃chī午wǔ餐cān。我wǒ一yì边biān开kāi车chē，一yì边biān想xiǎng：有yǒu母mǔ亲qīn在zài的de日rì子zi，实shí在zài是shì太tài幸xìng福fú了le，可kě我wǒ从cóng来lái没méi向xiàng她tā表biǎo达dá过guò感gǎn激jī之zhī情qíng，总zǒng觉jué得de难nán以yǐ启qǐ齿chǐ。我wǒ是shì不bú是shì应yīng该gāi学xué学xué女nǚ儿ér，勇yǒng敢gǎn地dì向xiàng母mǔ亲qīn表biǎo达dá感gǎn谢xiè，表biǎo达dá爱ài呢ne？
7One afternoon, as I was driving home for lunch, I thought to myself: having Mum with us has been such a blessing — yet I've never once told her how grateful I am. The words always seem so hard to say. Shouldn't I learn from my daughter, and work up the courage to tell Mum how thankful I am, how much I love her?
8 我wǒ下xià定dìng决jué心xīn，今jīn天tiān一yí定dìng要yào把bǎ爱ài说shuō出chū来lái。一yī路lù上shàng，我wǒ不bù断duàn给gěi自zì己jǐ打dǎ气qì：不bú要yào怕pà，一yī进jìn门mén就jiù开kāi口kǒu。
8I made up my mind: today, I would say it out loud. The whole drive home, I kept building myself up: don't be afraid — say it the moment you step through the door.
9 没méi想xiǎng到dào，推tuī开kāi家jiā门mén，一yī看kàn到dào母mǔ亲qīn，我wǒ还hái是shì觉jué得de不bù好hǎo意yì思sī，打dǎ了le退tuì堂táng鼓gǔ。我wǒ有yǒu些xiē懊ào恼nǎo，但dàn仍réng安ān慰wèi自zì己jǐ：没méi关guān系xì，第dì一yī次cì总zǒng是shì最zuì难nán的de。跨kuà过guò这zhè一yī关guān，以yǐ后hòu就jiù简jiǎn单dān了le。
9But no — the moment I opened the door and saw Mum, I felt awkward and chickened out. I was exasperated with myself, but consoled myself: it's fine, the first time is always the hardest. Get past this, and it'll be easy from here on.
10 吃chī饭fàn时shí，我wǒ一yì直zhí在zài伺sì机jī行xíng动dòng。母mǔ亲qīn看kàn出chū我wǒ心xīn不bù在zài焉yān，问wèn道dào：“你nǐ今jīn天tiān怎zěn么me了le？看kàn起qǐ来lái怪guài怪guài的de。”
10All through lunch, I was watching for the right moment. Mum noticed I was preoccupied and asked, "What's with you today? You seem odd."
11 我wǒ开kāi始shǐ佩pèi服fú女nǚ儿ér，她tā怎zěn么me能néng那nà么me自zì然rán地dì把bǎ爱ài说shuō出chū口kǒu！饭fàn快kuài吃chī完wán了le，我wǒ心xīn想xiǎng：我wǒ不bù能néng再zài错cuò过guò机jī会huì了le。我wǒ低dī着zhe头tóu，破pò天tiān荒huāng第dì一yī次cì说shuō道dào：“妈mā，我wǒ……我wǒ觉jué得de自zì己jǐ好hǎo幸xìng福fú！谢xiè谢xiè您nín！我wǒ……爱ài您nín！”
11I was beginning to admire my daughter — how does she say these things of love so naturally? The meal was almost over, and I thought: I can't let another chance slip by. Head down, for the first time in my life, I said: "Mum, I… I feel so lucky. Thank you. I… I love you."
12 我wǒ不bù敢gǎn看kàn母mǔ亲qīn的de表biǎo情qíng，说shuō完wán就jiù直zhí奔bēn书shū房fáng，拿ná起qǐ包bāo，仓cāng促cù地dì夺duó门mén而ér出chū，心xīn情qíng比bǐ当dāng年nián参cān加jiā毕bì业yè考kǎo试shì还hái紧jǐn张zhāng。
12I couldn't bring myself to look at her face. The moment I'd said it I rushed to the study, grabbed my bag, and hurried out the door — more nervous than I'd been on the day of my final exams.
13 那nà天tiān傍bàng晚wǎn下xià班bān回huí家jiā，我wǒ一yī进jìn门mén就jiù听tīng见jiàn母mǔ亲qīn的de歌gē声shēng。她tā一yì边biān在zài厨chú房fáng忙máng着zhe准zhǔn备bèi晚wǎn餐cān，一yì边biān唱chàng着zhe歌gē。自zì从cóng父fù亲qīn过guò世shì后hòu，我wǒ还hái是shì第dì一yī次cì听tīng到dào这zhè熟shú悉xī的de歌gē声shēng。
13That evening, I came home from work and as soon as I walked in, I heard Mum singing. She was bustling about in the kitchen making dinner, a song on her lips. It was the first time I'd heard that familiar voice in song since Dad had passed.
