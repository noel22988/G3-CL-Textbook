助zhù养yǎng计jì划huà
活huó动dòng简jiǎn介jiè你nǐ想xiǎng为wèi保bǎo护hù野yě生shēng动dòng物wù尽jǐn一yī份fèn力lì吗ma？你nǐ可kě以yǐ参cān加jiā新xīn加jiā坡pō动dòng物wù园yuán推tuī出chū的de野yě生shēng动dòng物wù助zhù养yǎng计jì划huà，通tōng过guò捐juān款kuǎn，献xiàn出chū你nǐ的de爱ài心xīn。
捐juān款kuǎn用yòng途tú拯zhěng救jiù野yě生shēng动dòng物wù。 购gòu买mǎi医yī疗liáo设shè备bèi，为wèi动dòng物wù提tí供gōng更gèng好hǎo的de护hù理lǐ。 购gòu买mǎi食shí物wù，为wèi动dòng物wù提tí供gōng高gāo品pǐn质zhì的dì饮yǐn食shí。 作zuò为wéi活huó动dòng经jīng费fèi，为wèi动dòng物wù设shè计jì活huó动dòng，丰fēng富fù它tā们men的de生shēng活huó。 支zhī持chí实shí地dì调diào查chá研yán究jiū，开kāi展zhǎn野yě生shēng动dòng物wù保bǎo育yù计jì划huà。
助zhù养yǎng期qī限xiàn及jí优yōu惠huì为wéi期qī一yì年nián。助zhù养yǎng者zhě可kě获huò得dé证zhèng书shū、动dòng物wù园yuán门mén票piào、购gòu物wù折zhé扣kòu券quàn等děng。
主zhǔ题tí关guān爱ài动dòng物wù，从cóng你nǐ我wǒ做zuò起qǐ。请qǐng选xuǎn择zé你nǐ想xiǎng助zhù养yǎng的de动dòng物wù。
The Adoption Programme
About the Programme. Would you like to do your part to protect wild animals? Join the Wildlife Adoption Programme run by the Singapore Zoo and show your care by donating.
How Your Donation Is Used. To rescue wildlife. To purchase medical equipment so animals get better care. To buy food that gives animals high-quality nutrition. As activity funding, to design enrichment that makes their lives more stimulating. To support field research and run wildlife conservation programmes.
Duration & Perks. One-year adoption. Adopters receive a certificate, zoo admission tickets, shopping discount vouchers, and more.
Theme. Caring for animals starts with you and me. Pick the animal you'd like to adopt.

Orangutan — Ah Ming
Profile. Ah Ming has long, powerful limbs. He's quick and nimble, darting through the trees with ease. Friendly and warm-natured, Ah Ming even serves as a Goodwill Ambassador for the Singapore Zoo.

African Penguins — Banjie & Beila
Profile. Banjie and Beila love to waddle around exploring new things. Every African penguin's plumage markings are one-of-a-kind — we can tell Banjie and Beila apart by the patterns on their chests.

Aldabra Giant Tortoise — Keke
Profile. Keke is a little shy, preferring to rest in the shade. She's a big eater — though she weighs 120 kg, the moment food appears, she moves surprisingly fast.

Sun Bear — Xiao Ha
Profile. Xiao Ha is very mischievous — he's forever play-wrestling with his mates. His favourite thing is sweet food: offer him a bunch of grapes and he'll happily cooperate with the keepers. He may be small, but he's fierce — don't underestimate him.
