① 市shì政zhèng区qū
感gǎn受shòu艺yì术shù魅mèi力lì市shì政zhèng区qū是shì艺yì术shù爱ài好hào者zhě的de乐lè园yuán，这zhè里lǐ除chú了le维wéi多duō利lì亚yà剧jù院yuàn与yǔ音yīn乐yuè厅tīng、亚yà洲zhōu文wén明míng博bó物wù馆guǎn，还hái有yǒu收shōu藏cáng了le9000多duō件jiàn艺yì术shù品pǐn的de新xīn加jiā坡pō国guó家jiā美měi术shù馆guǎn。这zhè些xiē建jiàn筑zhù历lì史shǐ悠yōu久jiǔ，是shì新xīn加jiā坡pō文wén化huà遗yí产chǎn的de重zhòng要yào组zǔ成chéng部bù分fèn。
推tuī荐jiàn欣xīn赏shǎng艺yì术shù、感gǎn受shòu历lì史shǐ
1. Civic District
Feel the charm of art. The Civic District is a paradise for art lovers. Besides Victoria Theatre and Concert Hall and the Asian Civilisations Museum, it is home to the National Gallery Singapore, which holds a collection of more than 9,000 artworks. These historic buildings are an important part of Singapore's cultural heritage.
Recommended for. Appreciating art and soaking up history
② 老lǎo巴bā刹shā
品pǐn尝cháng特tè色sè美měi食shí老lǎo巴bā刹shā建jiàn于yú19世shì纪jì，历lì史shǐ上shàng曾céng多duō次cì重chóng建jiàn，1973年nián被bèi列liè为wèi国guó家jiā古gǔ迹jì。它tā像xiàng一yī位wèi百bǎi岁suì老lǎo人rén，安ān详xiáng地dì坐zuò在zài市shì中zhōng心xīn，欢huān迎yíng着zhe各gè族zú同tóng胞bāo和hé来lái自zì世shì界jiè各gè地dì的de游yóu客kè。这zhè里lǐ24小xiǎo时shí开kāi放fàng，售shòu卖mài各gè族zú美měi食shí，鸡jī饭fàn、沙shā爹diē、印yìn度dù煎jiān饼bǐng等děng应yīng有yǒu尽jìn有yǒu，体tǐ现xiàn了le不bù同tóng文wén化huà的de融róng合hé。
推tuī荐jiàn品pǐn尝cháng美měi食shí、体tǐ验yàn文wén化huà
2. Lau Pa Sat
Taste signature local food. Built in the 19th century and rebuilt several times over the years, Lau Pa Sat was gazetted as a national monument in 1973. Like a serene centenarian sitting peacefully in the city centre, it welcomes Singaporeans of every race alongside visitors from around the world. Open 24 hours, its stalls sell food from every community — chicken rice, satay, prata and more — all reflecting the blending of different cultures.
Recommended for. Tasting local food and experiencing different cultures
③ 甘gān榜bǎng格gé南nán
连lián接jiē过guò去qù与yǔ现xiàn在zài甘gān榜bǎng格gé南nán有yǒu着zhe浓nóng浓nóng的de马mǎ来lái文wén化huà特tè色sè。这zhè里lǐ以yǐ前qián是shì马mǎ来lái皇huáng宫gōng的de所suǒ在zài地dì。圆yuán顶dǐng的de苏sū丹dān回huí教jiào堂táng就jiù像xiàng童tóng话huà中zhōng的de城chéng堡bǎo。邻lín近jìn的de哈hā芝zhī巷xiàng随suí处chù可kě见jiàn时shí尚shàng小xiǎo店diàn和hé彩cǎi绘huì壁bì画huà，难nán怪guài年nián轻qīng人rén会huì流liú连lián忘wàng返fǎn。
推tuī荐jiàn体tǐ验yàn文wén化huà、娱yú乐lè休xiū闲xián
3. Kampong Glam
A bridge between past and present. Kampong Glam is rich in Malay culture. Once the site of the Malay palace, it is home to the domed Sultan Mosque, which rises like a castle out of a fairytale. Nearby Haji Lane is full of trendy boutiques and colourful murals — no wonder young people love to linger there.
Recommended for. Cultural experiences and casual exploring
④ 如rú切qiè、加jiā东dōng
探tàn索suǒ娘niáng惹rě文wén化huà如rú切qiè、加jiā东dōng有yǒu许xǔ多duō色sè彩cǎi缤bīn纷fēn的de店diàn屋wū，是shì保bǎo存cún完wán好hǎo的de多duō元yuán文wén化huà街jiē区qū。这zhè里lǐ售shòu卖mài各gè种zhǒng娘niáng惹rě美měi食shí和hé服fú饰shì，许xǔ多duō介jiè绍shào娘niáng惹rě文wén化huà的de片piān子zi都dōu在zài这zhè里lǐ拍pāi摄shè。1993年nián，这zhè一yī街jiē区qū成chéng为wéi指zhǐ定dìng的de旧jiù屋wū保bǎo留liú区qū。
推tuī荐jiàn体tǐ验yàn文wén化huà、观guān赏shǎng建jiàn筑zhù
4. Joo Chiat and Katong
Explore Peranakan culture. Joo Chiat and Katong are known for their colourful shophouses — well-preserved multicultural districts where you can find Peranakan food and traditional clothing. Many films about Peranakan culture have been shot here. In 1993, the area was officially designated a conservation district.
Recommended for. Cultural experiences and architecture
⑤ 樟zhāng宜yí机jī场chǎng
通tōng往wǎng世shì界jiè各gè地dì樟zhāng宜yí机jī场chǎng世shì界jiè闻wén名míng，2019年nián的de载zài客kè量liàng超chāo过guò6500万wàn人rén次cì，平píng均jūn每měi80秒miǎo就jiù有yǒu一yī架jià飞fēi机jī起qǐ降jiàng，十shí分fēn繁fán忙máng。这zhè里lǐ有yǒu许xǔ多duō休xiū闲xián娱yú乐lè的de好hǎo去qù处chù，其qí中zhōng包bāo括kuò如rú宝bǎo石shí一yì般bān闪shǎn耀yào的de星xīng耀yào樟zhāng宜yí。
推tuī荐jiàn娱yú乐lè休xiū闲xián、品pǐn尝cháng美měi食shí
5. Changi Airport
A gateway to the world. Changi Airport is world-famous. It handled more than 65 million passengers in 2019 — an aircraft taking off or landing every 80 seconds on average. Beyond travel, the airport has many spots for leisure and entertainment, including the jewel-like Jewel Changi.
Recommended for. Leisure, entertainment, and food
