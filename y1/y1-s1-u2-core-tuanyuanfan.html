1 时shí间jiān真zhēn是shì个gè贼zéi，而ér且qiě还hái是shì个gè神shén偷tōu。今jīn年nián妈mā妈mā头tóu上shàng的de白bái发fà看kàn起qǐ来lái更gèng多duō了le，眼yǎn角jiǎo也yě添tiān了le新xīn的de鱼yú尾wěi纹wén。
1Time really is a thief — and a very sneaky one at that. This year, Mum seemed to have more grey hairs than before, and new crow's feet at the corners of her eyes.
2 年nián尾wěi的de街jiē市shì上shàng到dào处chù都dōu是shì红hóng色sè的de装zhuāng饰shì。送sòng走zǒu圣shèng诞dàn老lǎo人rén，过guò了le元yuán旦dàn，农nóng历lì新xīn年nián也yě悄qiāo悄qiāo地dì走zǒu近jìn了le。家jiā家jiā户hù户hù都dōu开kāi始shǐ忙máng碌lù起qǐ来lái，大dà扫sǎo除chú，买mǎi年nián货huò，准zhǔn备bèi除chú夕xī夜yè的de团tuán圆yuán饭fàn。
2The year-end markets were covered in red decorations. After Santa had gone and the New Year had passed, Lunar New Year was quietly approaching too. Every household grew busy — spring-cleaning, buying festive provisions, and preparing for the reunion dinner on New Year's Eve.
3 几jǐ天tiān前qián，妈mā妈mā买mǎi年nián货huò时shí看kàn起qǐ来lái不bú是shì很hěn开kāi心xīn，铁tiě青qīng着zhe脸liǎn，像xiàng全quán天tiān下xià的de人rén都dōu得dé罪zuì了le她tā似shì的de。回huí家jiā处chǔ理lǐ鱼yú时shí，妈mā妈mā也yě一yì直zhí皱zhòu着zhe眉méi，此cǐ时shí的de她tā和hé墙qiáng上shàng新xīn贴tiē的de“年nián年nián有yǒu余yú”显xiǎn得de格gé格gé不bù入rù。鱼yú是shì做zuò团tuán圆yuán饭fàn时shí要yào用yòng的de，现xiàn在zài先xiān处chǔ理lǐ，冻dòng起qǐ来lái备bèi用yòng。妈mā妈mā说shuō，鱼yú要yào早zǎo点diǎn儿ér买mǎi，不bù然rán越yuè靠kào近jìn农nóng历lì新xīn年nián越yuè贵guì。
3A few days earlier, Mum hadn't seemed happy while shopping for festive supplies. Her face was like thunder, as though the whole world had offended her. When she got home and prepared the fish, she was frowning the whole time — she seemed completely out of place next to the freshly-pasted "may each year bring abundance" banner on the wall. The fish was for the reunion dinner — prepared ahead of time and frozen for later. Mum said fish had to be bought early, or it got more expensive as Lunar New Year approached.
4 我wǒ知zhī道dào妈mā妈mā是shì因yīn为wèi姐jiě姐jiě的de事shì心xīn里lǐ在zài闹nào别biè扭niǔ。去qù年nián年nián初chū，姐jiě姐jiě结jié婚hūn，嫁jià到dào英yīng国guó去qù了le。上shàng个gè月yuè视shì讯xùn时shí她tā告gào诉sù我wǒ们men一yī件jiàn事shì，话huà说shuō得dé吞tūn吞tūn吐tǔ吐tǔ的de：“爸bà、妈mā，对duì不bù起qǐ……看kàn来lái今jīn年nián……没méi办bàn法fǎ跟gēn你nǐ们men一yì起qǐ吃chī团tuán圆yuán饭fàn了le。”
4I knew Mum was upset about my elder sister. Early last year, my sister had got married and moved to England. In a video call the previous month, she had told us something, hesitating as she spoke: "Dad, Mum, I'm sorry… it looks like this year… I won't be able to have the reunion dinner with you."
5 “嗯ń。”
5"Mm."
6 “这zhè里lǐ……假jià期qī不bù好hǎo拿ná。我wǒ提tí前qián跟gēn你nǐ们men拜bài年nián！”
6"Here… it's hard to get time off. Let me wish you a happy New Year in advance!"
7 “好hǎo吧ba。”
7"Alright then."
8 一yí个gè“嗯ń”，一yí个gè“好hǎo吧ba”，其qí实shí也yě只zhǐ能néng这zhè样yàng了le。妈mā妈mā一yí定dìng在zài想xiǎng，女nǚ儿ér可kě能néng以yǐ后hòu都dōu不bù能néng回huí来lái过guò年nián了le。沉chén默mò寡guǎ言yán的de爸bà爸bà还hái是shì那nà副fù酷kù酷kù的de表biǎo情qíng。
8A "mm" and an "alright then" — that was really all there was to say. Mum must have been thinking that her daughter might never come home for New Year again. Dad, who was a man of few words, kept his usual cool expression.
9 “我wǒ们men会huì给gěi你nǐ们men一yí个gè大dà大dà的de电diàn子zi红hóng包bāo！”姐jiě姐jiě和hé姐jiě夫fū的de承chéng诺nuò中zhōng带dài着zhe几jǐ分fēn歉qiàn意yì。
9"We'll send you a big electronic red packet!" my sister and her husband promised, with a note of apology in their voices.
10 这zhè是shì姐jiě姐jiě第dì一yī次cì不bù和hé爸bà妈mā一yì起qǐ过guò新xīn年nián。除chú了le姐jiě姐jiě，正zhèng在zài当dāng兵bīng的dī哥gē哥gē因yīn为wèi出chū国guó受shòu训xùn，也yě不bù能néng回huí来lái吃chī团tuán圆yuán饭fàn。少shǎo了le他tā们men两liǎng个gè，团tuán圆yuán饭fàn也yě会huì冷lěng清qīng不bù少shǎo。
10It was the first time my sister wouldn't be home for New Year. And besides her, my brother — who was doing his national service — was away on overseas training and couldn't make the reunion dinner either. Without the two of them, the table would feel much quieter.
11 几jǐ年nián前qián，我wǒ们men家jiā的de团tuán圆yuán饭fàn已yǐ经jīng改gǎi为wéi吃chī火huǒ锅guō。因yīn为wèi妈mā妈mā觉jué得de，一yī家jiā人rén围wéi炉lú很hěn热rè闹nào，而ér且qiě吃chī不bù完wán的de食shí物wù也yě不bú会huì浪làng费fèi。每měi年nián，妈mā妈mā除chú了le准zhǔn备bèi火huǒ锅guō料liào，还hái会huì煮zhǔ几jǐ道dào菜cài，有yǒu鱼yú，也yě有yǒu象xiàng征zhēng“发fā财cái”的de发fà菜cài。
11A few years ago, our family had switched to having steamboat for the reunion dinner. Mum felt it was livelier having everyone gathered around the pot, and any leftover food wouldn't go to waste. Every year, besides the steamboat ingredients, she would also cook a few dishes — including fish, and 发菜 (fàcài), a kind of black moss whose name sounds like "getting rich".
12 火huǒ锅guō的de热rè气qì蒸zhēng腾téng起qǐ来lái，我wǒ们men的de团tuán圆yuán饭fàn开kāi始shǐ了le。我wǒ一yī边biān帮bāng爸bà妈mā夹jiā菜cài，一yī边biān不bù停tíng地dì讲jiǎng着zhe学xué校xiào里lǐ的de种zhǒng种zhǒng新xīn闻wén旧jiù事shì，希xī望wàng我wǒ的de叽jī叽jī喳zhā喳zhā能néng够gòu冲chōng淡dàn哥gē哥gē姐jiě姐jiě的de缺quē席xí带dài来lái的de冷lěng清qīng。
12Steam rose from the steamboat, and our reunion dinner began. As I helped Mum and Dad to food, I chattered away about every bit of news and gossip from school — hoping my chattering might soften the quietness my brother and sister's absence had left behind.
13 突tū然rán，一yī阵zhèn悦yuè耳ěr的de门mén铃líng声shēng响xiǎng起qǐ。大dà家jiā的de目mù光guāng齐qí齐qí投tóu向xiàng门mén口kǒu，家jiā里lǐ的de贵guì宾bīn狗gǒu小xiǎo瘦shòu开kāi心xīn地dì叫jiào了le起qǐ来lái，摇yáo着zhe尾wěi巴ba，冲chōng向xiàng大dà门mén。
13Suddenly, the doorbell chimed. All our eyes turned towards the door at once. Xiao Shou, our family's poodle, barked happily and rushed to the door, wagging his tail.
14 火huǒ锅guō还hái在zài滚gǔn着zhe，似sì乎hū在zài期qī待dài着zhe全quán家jiā的de大dà团tuán圆yuán。
14The steamboat was still bubbling — as if it, too, was waiting for the whole family to be reunited.
