《幸运饺子》 The Lucky Dumpling

语速 Speed 0.9× (set before play)

生词 Vocabulary · 4 words

红hóng枣zǎo
red date (jujube)
儿ér媳xí妇fù
daughter-in-law (son's wife)
莫mò名míng其qí妙miào
baffling; inexplicable; can't make heads or tails of it
疑yí惑huò
puzzled; confused; to doubt
1 每měi年nián的de大dà年nián三sān十shí，我wǒ们men全quán家jiā都dū会huì围wéi坐zuò在zài一yì起qǐ包bāo饺jiǎo子zi。大dà年nián三sān十shí吃chī饺jiǎo子zi是shì中zhōng国guó北běi方fāng的de传chuán统tǒng。我wǒ喜xǐ欢huān吃chī饺jiǎo子zi，更gèng喜xǐ欢huān吃chī到dào幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi时shí的de那nà份fèn喜xǐ悦yuè。
1Every Lunar New Year's Eve, our whole family sits down together to make dumplings. Eating dumplings on New Year's Eve is a tradition in northern China. I love eating dumplings — and I love the happiness of finding the lucky dumpling even more.
2 今jīn年nián的de大dà年nián三sān十shí，我wǒ们men全quán家jiā人rén又yòu坐zuò在zài一yì起qǐ包bāo饺jiǎo子zi。姑gū姑gū的de手shǒu最zuì巧qiǎo，包bāo的de饺jiǎo子zi最zuì漂piào亮liàng。她tā拿ná了le一yī颗kē红hóng枣zǎo，包bāo进jìn其qí中zhōng一yí个gè饺jiǎo子zi里lǐ，笑xiào着zhe说shuō：“谁shuí要yào是shì吃chī到dào这zhè个ge幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi，今jīn年nián就jiù会huì心xīn想xiǎng事shì成chéng，大dà吉jí大dà利lì！”我wǒ想xiǎng偷tōu偷tōu在zài幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi上shàng做zuò个gè记jì号hào，却què被bèi姑gū姑gū打dǎ了le手shǒu。
2This New Year's Eve, our whole family sat down to make dumplings once again. My aunt has the most skilful hands — her dumplings are the prettiest. She took a red date, wrapped it inside one of the dumplings, and said with a smile, "Whoever eats this lucky dumpling will have all their wishes come true this year, and great good fortune!" I tried to secretly mark the lucky dumpling, but my aunt swatted my hand away.
3 我wǒ紧jǐn盯dīng着zhe幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi不bù放fàng。饺jiǎo子zi煮zhǔ好hǎo上shàng桌zhuō了le，妈mā妈mā招zhāo呼hū大dà家jiā吃chī饺jiǎo子zi。我wǒ赶gǎn紧jǐn把bǎ幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi夹jiā到dào奶nǎi奶nǎi的de碗wǎn里lǐ，因yīn为wèi奶nǎi奶nǎi向xiàng来lái最zuì疼téng爱ài我wǒ。奶nǎi奶nǎi虽suī然rán视shì力lì不bù好hǎo，但dàn还hái是shì发fā现xiàn了le。她tā慈cí爱ài地dì对duì我wǒ眨zhǎ眨zhǎ眼yǎn，然rán后hòu把bǎ幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi夹jiā到dào妈mā妈mā的de碗wǎn里lǐ。奶nǎi奶nǎi对duì全quán家jiā人rén说shuō：“儿ér媳xí妇fù既jì要yào工gōng作zuò，又yòu要yào照zhào顾gù一yī家jiā老lǎo小xiǎo，是shì这zhè个ge家jiā里lǐ最zuì辛xīn苦kǔ的de人rén。希xī望wàng她tā在zài新xīn的de一yī年nián天tiān天tiān幸xìng福fú快kuài乐lè！” 听tīng了le奶nǎi奶nǎi的de话huà，妈mā妈mā显xiǎn得de很hěn感gǎn动dòng。
3I kept my eyes fixed on the lucky dumpling. When the dumplings were cooked and brought to the table, Mum called everyone to eat. I quickly picked up the lucky dumpling and put it in Grandma's bowl, because Grandma has always loved me the most. Grandma's eyesight isn't good, but she noticed. She gave me a loving wink, then picked up the lucky dumpling and placed it in Mum's bowl. Grandma said to the whole family, "Your daughter-in-law both works and looks after the whole family — she's the one who works hardest in this house. I hope every day brings her happiness in the new year!" Hearing Grandma's words, Mum looked deeply moved.
4 妈mā妈mā趁chèn姑gū姑gū不bù注zhù意yì，又yòu和hé姑gū姑gū换huàn了le碗wǎn。姑gū姑gū是shì个gè细xì心xīn的de人rén，马mǎ上shàng就jiù发fā现xiàn了le碗wǎn里lǐ的de幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi。她tā一yī句jù话huà也yě没méi说shuō，只zhǐ是shì感gǎn激jī地dì看kàn着zhe妈mā妈mā。
4When my aunt wasn't looking, Mum swapped bowls with her. My aunt is observant — she noticed the lucky dumpling in her bowl right away. She didn't say a word, but looked at Mum with gratitude.
5 突tū然rán，我wǒ大dà叫jiào一yī声shēng：“我wǒ吃chī到dào幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi了le！”我wǒ感gǎn到dào莫mò名míng其qí妙miào，这zhè饺jiǎo子zi怎zěn么me又yòu回huí来lái了le呢ne？我wǒ正zhèng疑yí惑huò着zhe，姑gū姑gū拍pāi拍pāi我wǒ的de肩jiān膀bǎng，亲qīn切qiè地dì说shuō：“你nǐ年nián纪jì最zuì小xiǎo，是shì我wǒ们men家jiā最zuì大dà的de希xī望wàng，祝zhù你nǐ学xué业yè进jìn步bù，健jiàn康kāng成chéng长zhǎng！”这zhè时shí，我wǒ才cái恍huǎng然rán大dà悟wù，原yuán来lái是shì姑gū姑gū偷tōu偷tōu地dì把bǎ幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi放fàng到dào了le我wǒ的de碗wǎn里lǐ。
5Suddenly, I shouted, "I got the lucky dumpling!" I was baffled — how had the dumpling come back to me? As I wondered, my aunt patted my shoulder and said warmly, "You're the youngest — you're the hope of our family. I wish you good progress in your studies and a healthy life ahead!" Only then did I realise: my aunt had quietly slipped the lucky dumpling into my bowl.
6 这zhè时shí，爸bà爸bà装zhuāng出chū可kě怜lián的de样yàng子zi，说shuō：“怎zěn么me没méi有yǒu人rén给gěi我wǒ幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi？” 爸bà爸bà的de话huà音yīn刚gāng落luò，大dà家jiā立lì刻kè把bǎ自zì己jǐ碗wǎn里lǐ的de饺jiǎo子zi夹jiā到dào他tā的de碗wǎn里lǐ。望wàng着zhe满mǎn满mǎn一yī碗wǎn的de“幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi” ，爸bà爸bà笑xiào了le，一yī家jiā人rén都dōu笑xiào了le。
6Just then, Dad put on a pitiful face and said, "How come no one is giving me a lucky dumpling?" The moment Dad finished speaking, everyone picked up dumplings from their own bowls and dropped them into his. Looking at his bowl full of "lucky dumplings," Dad laughed — and the whole family laughed with him.
7 其qí实shí，吃chī没méi吃chī到dào幸xìng运yùn饺jiǎo子zi并bìng不bú重zhòng要yào，重zhòng要yào的de是shì，我wǒ们men一yī家jiā人rén能néng够gòu开kāi开kāi心xīn心xīn地dì团tuán聚jù在zài一yì起qǐ。
7In the end, whether or not you eat the lucky dumpling isn't what matters. What matters is that our family can be happily together.
