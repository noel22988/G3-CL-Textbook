1 1986年nián7月yuè，电diàn视shì剧jù《芝zhī麻má绿lǜ豆dòu》开kāi始shǐ播bō出chū，总zǒng共gòng播bō出chū了le510集jí，播bō放fàng时shí间jiān长zhǎng达dá三sān年nián。剧jù情qíng讲jiǎng述shù的de虽suī然rán是shì发fā生shēng在zài邻lín里lǐ之zhī间jiān微wēi不bù足zú道dào的de日rì常cháng小xiǎo事shì，却què牵qiān动dòng了le电diàn视shì机jī前qián许xǔ多duō观guān众zhòng的de心xīn。随suí着zhe《芝zhī麻má绿lǜ豆dòu》的de播bō出chū，剧jù中zhōng的de主zhǔ题tí曲qū《小xiǎo人rén物wù的de心xīn声shēng》也yě走zǒu进jìn了le新xīn加jiā坡pō的de千qiān家jiā万wàn户hù。三sān十shí多duō年nián后hòu的de今jīn天tiān，这zhè首shǒu新xīn谣yáo歌gē曲qǔ仍réng然rán能néng够gòu激jī励lì人rén心xīn。
1In July 1986, the TV drama "Neighbours" (《芝麻绿豆》) began airing. It ran for 510 episodes over three years. Though the plots were about the trivial daily happenings between neighbours, the stories tugged at the hearts of countless viewers in front of their television sets. As "Neighbours" aired, its theme song Voices of Everyday People (《小人物的心声》) also found its way into households all across Singapore. More than thirty years later, this xinyao song still has the power to stir people's hearts.
2 《小xiǎo人rén物wù的de心xīn声shēng》不bù仅jǐn歌gē词cí浅qiǎn白bái、朗lǎng朗lǎng上shàng口kǒu，而ér且qiě意yì义yì深shēn刻kè。年nián轻qīng一yí代dài或huò许xǔ根gēn本běn没méi听tīng说shuō过guò《芝zhī麻má绿lǜ豆dòu》这zhè部bù电diàn视shì剧jù，但dàn对duì剧jù中zhōng的de主zhǔ题tí曲qū一yì点diǎn儿ér也yě不bù陌mò生shēng。在zài国guó庆qìng庆qìng典diǎn上shàng，联lián合hé学xué校xiào合hé唱chàng团tuán的de年nián轻qīng学xué子zǐ们men常cháng常cháng带dài领lǐng观guān众zhòng唱chàng响xiǎng这zhè首shǒu歌gē。
2Voices of Everyday People has lyrics that are simple, easy to sing along with, and yet deeply meaningful. The younger generation may never have heard of the drama "Neighbours", but they know its theme song well. At the National Day Parade, young students from the Combined Schools Choir often lead the audience in singing this very song.
3 李lǐ显xiǎn龙lóng总zǒng理lǐ在zài2014年nián的de国guó庆qìng群qún众zhòng大dà会huì上shàng也yě提tí到dào：“新xīn谣yáo歌gē曲qǔ中zhōng，我wǒ最zuì喜xǐ欢huān的de是shì《小xiǎo人rén物wù的de心xīn声shēng》，也yě最zuì熟shú悉xī。在zài国guó庆qìng庆qìng典diǎn上shàng很hěn高gāo兴xìng有yǒu机jī会huì和hé大dà家jiā一yì起qǐ唱chàng，因yīn为wèi它tā很hěn有yǒu意yì思si，小xiǎo人rén物wù也yě有yǒu小xiǎo人rén物wù的de贡gòng献xiàn。”
3Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong also mentioned at the 2014 National Day Rally: "Of all the xinyao songs, my favourite — and the one I know best — is Voices of Everyday People. I was very happy to have a chance to sing it together with everyone at the National Day Parade, because its message is so meaningful: even everyday people have their own contribution to make."
4 “也yě许xǔ我wǒ一yí个gè人rén，不bù能néng成chéng就jiù一yī番fān大dà事shì业yè，但dàn我wǒ尽jìn力lì贡gòng献xiàn一yī份fèn微wēi薄bó的de力lì量liàng……”《小xiǎo人rén物wù的de心xīn声shēng》唱chàng出chū了le新xīn加jiā坡pō人rén的de感gǎn受shòu。半bàn个gè世shì纪jì以yǐ来lái，一yí代dài代dài新xīn加jiā坡pō人rén勤qín奋fèn努nǔ力lì，每měi个gè小xiǎo人rén物wù都dōu积jī极jí打dǎ拼pīn。如rú果guǒ没méi有yǒu他tā们men的de奉fèng献xiàn和hé付fù出chū，就jiù没méi有yǒu今jīn天tiān繁fán荣róng的de新xīn加jiā坡pō。
4"Perhaps I alone cannot achieve anything great — but I give what little strength I have…" Voices of Everyday People gives voice to what Singaporeans feel. For half a century, generation after generation of Singaporeans have worked hard; every ordinary person has pressed on, doing their part. Without their devotion and effort, there would be no prosperous Singapore today.
5 无wú论lùn从cóng事shì什shén么me职zhí业yè，无wú论lùn地dì位wèi高gāo低dī，只zhǐ要yào敬jìng业yè乐lè业yè，平píng凡fán的de小xiǎo人rén物wù也yě能néng发fā出chū耀yào眼yǎn的de光guāng芒máng，创chuàng造zào出chū非fēi凡fán的de成chéng就jiù。
5Whatever the job, whatever the station in life — as long as one takes pride in and enjoys one's work, even ordinary people can shine brightly and achieve extraordinary things.
