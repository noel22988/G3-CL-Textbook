1 工gōng艺yì设shè计jì课kè的de老lǎo师shī布bù置zhì了le假jià期qī作zuò业yè，让ràng我wǒ们men设shè计jì一yí个gè别bié具jù特tè色sè的de建jiàn筑zhù模mó型xíng。正zhèng当dāng我wǒ伤shāng脑nǎo筋jīn的de时shí候hòu，爸bà爸bà说shuō，他tā可kě以yǐ带dài我wǒ去qù拜bài访fǎng一yī位wèi有yǒu名míng的de建jiàn筑zhù大dà师shī。
1My Design and Technology teacher had set us a holiday project: design a distinctive architectural model. Just as I was stumped for ideas, Dad said he could take me to visit a famous master architect.
2 爸bà爸bà带dài我wǒ来lái到dào中zhōng央yāng商shāng业yè区qū，我wǒ们men沿yán着zhe新xīn加jiā坡pō河hé漫màn步bù。走zǒu着zhe走zǒu着zhe，爸bà爸bà指zhǐ着zhe不bù远yuǎn处chù一yī座zuò外wài观guān独dú特tè的de大dà楼lóu问wèn我wǒ：“那nà座zuò大dà楼lóu的de外wài观guān像xiàng一yí个gè汉hàn字zì，你nǐ能néng看kàn出chū是shì什shén么me字zì吗ma？”我wǒ绞jiǎo尽jìn脑nǎo汁zhī，还hái是shì想xiǎng不bú到dào。爸bà爸bà得dé意yì地dì说shuō：“是shì繁fán体tǐ字zì‘貝bèi’。古gǔ时shí候hòu人rén们men曾céng经jīng把bǎ贝bèi壳ké当dàng作zuò钱qián来lái使shǐ用yòng，所suǒ以yǐ用yòng‘贝bèi’做zuò偏piān旁páng的de字zì，很hěn多duō都dōu跟gēn钱qián财cái有yǒu关guān。你nǐ知zhī道dào吗ma？这zhè座zuò大dà楼lóu就jiù是shì华huá侨qiáo银yín行háng大dà楼lóu。”“贝bèi”代dài表biǎo钱qián，银yín行háng不bù就jiù是shì与yǔ钱qián打dǎ交jiāo道dào吗ma？想xiǎng到dào这zhè里lǐ，我wǒ由yóu衷zhōng佩pèi服fú设shè计jì师shī的de巧qiǎo思sī。不bù过guò，建jiàn筑zhù大dà师shī该gāi不bú会huì住zhù在zài银yín行háng大dà楼lóu里lǐ吧ba？爸bà爸bà似sì乎hū看kàn出chū我wǒ的de疑yí惑huò，笑xiào着zhe说shuō：“别bié急jí，我wǒ们men先xiān去qù来lái福fú士shì城chéng吃chī午wǔ餐cān。”
2Dad took me to the Central Business District, and we strolled along the Singapore River. As we walked, he pointed at a strikingly unusual building in the distance and asked: "That building's shape looks like a Chinese character — can you tell which one?" I racked my brain but couldn't figure it out. Dad said triumphantly, "It's the traditional character 貝 (bèi, 'cowrie shell'). In ancient times, people used cowrie shells as money — which is why many characters with 贝 as a radical relate to wealth or money. Do you know? That's the OCBC Centre." 贝 stands for money, and what else is a bank about but money? The thought of it made me genuinely admire the architect's cleverness. But surely the master architect didn't live inside a bank? As if reading my confusion, Dad smiled and said, "Don't worry — let's go to Raffles City for lunch first."
3 我wǒ们men沿yán着zhe桥qiáo北běi路lù走zǒu，不bù久jiǔ就jiù看kàn到dào远yuǎn处chù的de来lái福fú士shì城chéng大dà楼lóu。这zhè座zuò楼lóu从cóng一yí面miàn看kàn是shì个gè圆yuán柱zhù体tǐ，从cóng另lìng一yí面miàn看kàn又yòu是shì个gè长cháng方fāng体tǐ，别bié具jù一yī格gé。吃chī完wán午wǔ餐cān，爸bà爸bà还hái要yào走zǒu一yī走zǒu。
3We walked along North Bridge Road and soon Raffles City came into view. From one angle it appeared as a cylinder; from another, a rectangular block — truly one of a kind. After lunch, Dad wanted to keep walking.
4 我wǒ们men离lí开kāi来lái福fú士shì城chéng，顺shùn着zhe美měi芝zhī路lù来lái到dào新xīn门mén广guǎng场chǎng。这zhè里lǐ有yǒu两liǎng座zuò摩mó天tiān大dà楼lóu，好hǎo像xiàng两liǎng扇shàn打dǎ开kāi的de大dà门mén。大dà楼lóu形xíng状zhuàng特tè别bié，看kàn上shàng去qù就jiù像xiàng薄báo薄báo的de刀dāo片piàn一yí样yàng。爸bà爸bà说shuō，有yǒu人rén认rèn为wéi大dà楼lóu设shè计jì成chéng这zhè种zhǒng形xíng状zhuàng，有yǒu消xiāo除chú灾zāi祸huò的de用yòng意yì。虽suī然rán这zhè只zhǐ是shì传chuán言yán，却què让ràng我wǒ对duì建jiàn筑zhù设shè计jì产chǎn生shēng了le更gèng强qiáng的de好hào奇qí心xīn。
4We left Raffles City and walked down Beach Road to The Gateway. Two skyscrapers stood there like a great pair of open doors. Their shape was unusual — from certain angles they looked like thin razor blades. Dad said some people believe the design was meant to ward off bad fortune. Though it was just folklore, it made me even more curious about architecture.
5 我wǒ问wèn爸bà爸bà那nà位wèi建jiàn筑zhù大dà师shī住zhù在zài哪nǎ里lǐ，他tā却què说shuō：“你nǐ已yǐ经jīng见jiàn过guò他tā了le，他tā就jiù是shì贝bèi聿yù铭míng先xiān生shēng。这zhè三sān座zuò建jiàn筑zhù都dōu是shì他tā设shè计jì的de。”我wǒ这zhè才cái恍huǎng然rán大dà悟wù，原yuán来lái爸bà爸bà希xī望wàng我wǒ从cóng这zhè位wèi大dà师shī的de建jiàn筑zhù中zhōng获huò得dé设shè计jì的de灵líng感gǎn。
5I asked Dad where the master architect lived. He replied: "You've already met him — it's I. M. Pei. All three of these buildings are his designs." Only then did I realise what Dad had been doing — he'd wanted me to find design inspiration in this master's work.
6 回huí到dào家jiā后hòu，通tōng过guò查chá找zhǎo资zī料liào，我wǒ对duì贝bèi聿yù铭míng先xiān生shēng有yǒu了le更gèng多duō的de了liǎo解jiě。他tā是shì一yī名míng建jiàn筑zhù设shè计jì大dà师shī，他tā的de很hěn多duō建jiàn筑zhù今jīn天tiān都dōu已yǐ经jīng成chéng为wéi知zhī名míng的de地dì标biāo。但dàn是shì，他tā设shè计jì的de不bù少shǎo建jiàn筑zhù也yě曾céng遭zāo到dào质zhì疑yí和hé抨pēng击jī。面miàn对duì挫cuò折zhé，他tā这zhè样yàng说shuō：“我wǒ和hé我wǒ的de建jiàn筑zhù都dōu像xiàng竹zhú子zi，再zài大dà的de风fēng雨yǔ也yě只zhǐ是shì弯wān弯wān腰yāo而ér已yǐ。”
6Back home, I looked him up and learned more about I. M. Pei. He is a true master of architectural design, and many of his buildings are world-famous landmarks today. But quite a few of his designs have also faced serious doubt and criticism. Of such setbacks, he once said: "Both I and my buildings are like bamboo — however fierce the wind and rain, we only bend with them."
7 这zhè次cì建jiàn筑zhù之zhī旅lǚ，不bù但dàn让ràng我wǒ对duì新xīn加jiā坡pō的de建jiàn筑zhù有yǒu了le新xīn的de认rèn识shí，也yě让ràng我wǒ从cóng这zhè位wèi建jiàn筑zhù大dà师shī身shēn上shàng学xué到dào很hěn多duō。
7This architectural journey not only gave me a new appreciation for Singapore's buildings — it also taught me a great deal from this master architect.
