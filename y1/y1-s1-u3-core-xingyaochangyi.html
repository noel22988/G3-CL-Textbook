《魅力四射 星耀樟宜》 Dazzling Jewel Changi

语速 Speed 0.9× (set before play)
1 星xīng耀yào樟zhāng宜yí在zài2019年nián10月yuè18日rì正zhèng式shì开kāi幕mù，我wǒ们men又yòu多duō了le一yí个gè休xiū闲xián娱yú乐lè的de好hǎo去qù处chù。不bù过guò，如rú果guǒ你nǐ以yǐ为wéi它tā只zhǐ是shì一yí个gè普pǔ通tōng的de购gòu物wù中zhōng心xīn，那nà就jiù太tài小xiǎo看kàn它tā啦la！
1Jewel Changi officially opened on 18 October 2019, giving us another great place to relax and have fun. But if you think it's just an ordinary shopping mall, you're seriously underestimating it!
令lìng人rén赞zàn叹tàn的de外wài形xíng设shè计jì
A stunning exterior design
2 从cóng空kōng中zhōng看kàn，闪shǎn亮liàng的de星xīng耀yào樟zhāng宜yí像xiàng一yī颗kē宝bǎo石shí在zài阳yáng光guāng下xià闪shǎn耀yào。它tā的de外wài壳ké由yóu大dà约yuē1万wàn8000根gēn钢gāng铁tiě制zhì成chéng的de横héng梁liáng构gòu成chéng，覆fù盖gài着zhe9000多duō片piàn特tè制zhì的de玻bō璃lí，每měi一yī片piàn都dōu是shì独dú一yī无wú二èr的de。
2Seen from above, Jewel Changi sparkles in the sunlight like a gemstone. Its shell is made up of roughly 18,000 steel beams, covered by more than 9,000 specially-made glass panels — each one unique.
世shì界jiè上shàng最zuì高gāo的de室shì内nèi瀑pù布bù
The world's tallest indoor waterfall
3 “雨yǔ漩xuán涡wō”是shì星xīng耀yào樟zhāng宜yí最zuì吸xī引yǐn人rén的de地dì方fāng。它tā高gāo40米mǐ，从cóng五wǔ楼lóu一yì直zhí到dào地dì下xià二èr楼lóu，是shì目mù前qián世shì界jiè上shàng最zuì高gāo的de室shì内nèi瀑pù布bù。“雨yǔ漩xuán涡wō”每měi天tiān使shǐ用yòng多duō达dá50万wàn公gōng升shēng的de水shuǐ，这zhè些xiē水shuǐ通tōng过guò水shuǐ管guǎn可kě循xún环huán使shǐ用yòng，因yīn此cǐ并bìng不bú会huì造zào成chéng浪làng费fèi。
3The Rain Vortex is Jewel Changi's biggest draw. At 40 metres tall, plunging from the fifth floor all the way down to basement two, it is currently the world's tallest indoor waterfall. It uses up to 500,000 litres of water each day, but the water circulates through pipes and is reused — so nothing goes to waste.
建jiàn在zài机jī场chǎng的de室shì内nèi绿lǜ洲zhōu
An indoor oasis inside the airport
4 “森sēn林lín谷gǔ”中zhōng的de树shù木mù超chāo过guò2000棵kē，低dī矮ǎi的de灌guàn木mù则zé超chāo过guò10万wàn株zhū。这zhè里lǐ大dà约yuē有yǒu120个gè植zhí物wù品pǐn种zhǒng，它tā们men来lái自zì世shì界jiè各gè地dì，其qí中zhōng有yǒu两liǎng棵kē超chāo过guò100岁suì的de橄gǎn榄lǎn树shù就jiù来lái自zì西xī班bān牙yá。各gè国guó的de访fǎng客kè在zài这zhè里lǐ就jiù能néng够gòu感gǎn受shòu我wǒ们men这zhè个ge花huā园yuán城chéng市shì的de魅mèi力lì。
4The Forest Valley holds more than 2,000 trees and over 100,000 shrubs. Around 120 plant species are represented here, gathered from all over the world — including two olive trees from Spain, both more than 100 years old. Visitors from every country get to feel the charm of our Garden City without leaving the airport.
别bié具jù一yī格gé的de游yóu乐lè设shè施shī
One-of-a-kind attractions
5 星xīng耀yào樟zhāng宜yí也yě有yǒu不bù少shǎo好hǎo玩wán又yòu刺cì激jī的de游yóu乐lè设shè施shī。比bǐ如rú，不bù时shí喷pēn出chū云yún雾wù的de天tiān悬xuán桥qiáo；以yǐ花huā园yuán为wéi主zhǔ题tí的de镜jìng子zi迷mí宫gōng；有yǒu趣qù又yòu特tè别bié的de“天tiān空kōng之zhī网wǎng”。“天tiān空kōng之zhī网wǎng”包bāo括kuò步bù行xíng网wǎng和hé蹦bèng跳tiào网wǎng。步bù行xíng网wǎng是shì一yī条tiáo50米mǐ长zhǎng的de网wǎng状zhuàng步bù道dào，距jù离lí地dì面miàn25米mǐ高gāo，走zǒu起qǐ来lái摇yáo摇yáo晃huàng晃huǎng的de，需xū要yào几jǐ分fēn胆dǎn量liàng；蹦bèng跳tiào网wǎng则zé有yǒu250米mǐ长zhǎng，在zài上shàng面miàn蹦bèng跳tiào时shí，有yǒu一yī种zhǒng飞fēi起qǐ来lái的de感gǎn觉jué。
5Jewel Changi has plenty of fun and thrilling attractions too: the Canopy Bridge, which occasionally releases billowing mist; the Mirror Maze, set in a garden theme; and the fascinating Sky Nets, which include a Walking Net and a Bouncing Net. The Walking Net is a 50-metre-long net walkway suspended 25 metres above the ground — it wobbles as you walk across it, so you'll need a bit of courage. The 250-metre Bouncing Net lets you jump along its surface, giving you the feeling of flying.
多duō种zhǒng选xuǎn择zé的de各gè地dì美měi食shí
A world of food choices
6 作zuò为wéi一yí个gè时shí尚shàng休xiū闲xián中zhōng心xīn，星xīng耀yào樟zhāng宜yí当dāng然rán少shào不bù了liǎo美měi食shí。这zhè里lǐ有yǒu200多duō家jiā商shāng店diàn，其qí中zhōng不bù少shǎo和hé“吃chī”有yǒu关guān，零líng食shí店diàn、甜tián品pǐn店diàn、饮yǐn料liào店diàn和hé特tè色sè餐cān馆guǎn应yīng有yǒu尽jìn有yǒu。在zài这zhè里lǐ，你nǐ可kě以yǐ吃chī到dào世shì界jiè各gè地dì的de美měi食shí，有yǒu的de甚shèn至zhì是shì本běn地dì难nán得dé一yī见jiàn的de。这zhè些xiē美měi食shí使shǐ星xīng耀yào樟zhāng宜yí更gèng具jù吸xī引yǐn力lì。
6As a stylish lifestyle destination, Jewel Changi naturally doesn't skimp on food. Among its 200-plus shops, many are food-related — snack stalls, dessert shops, drink kiosks, and speciality restaurants, with something for every craving. You can find cuisine from around the world here, some of which is rarely seen anywhere else in Singapore. The food is part of what makes Jewel Changi so appealing.
7 时shí代dài在zài发fā展zhǎn，我wǒ们men的de机jī场chǎng也yě需xū要yào与yǔ时shí俱jù进jìn，不bù断duàn创chuàng新xīn。星xīng耀yào樟zhāng宜yí不bù仅jǐn为wèi国guó人rén和hé旅lǚ客kè提tí供gōng了le一yí个gè放fàng松sōng身shēn心xīn的de好hǎo去qù处chù，而ér且qiě也yě让ràng樟zhāng宜yí机jī场chǎng这zhè一yī世shì界jiè级jí的de航háng空kōng中zhōng心xīn变biàn得dé更gèng加jiā迷mí人rén，更gèng有yǒu魅mèi力lì！
7Times are changing, and our airport needs to move with the times, constantly innovating. Jewel Changi not only gives locals and travellers a great place to unwind — it also makes Changi Airport, already a world-class aviation hub, even more enchanting and full of character.
