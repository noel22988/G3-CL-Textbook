Eight Good Habits to Cultivate
Respect and Treasure Time. An inch of time is worth an inch of gold — and an inch of gold cannot buy an inch of time.
Read Diligently. Opening a book always brings benefit; wherever you go, let books be your companion.
Never Give Up. Dripping water wears through stone — keep at it, and there will always be something to gain.
Keep Your Promises. A single word should be worth a thousand pieces of gold — say it, and you must follow through.
Live Healthily. Health isn't everything — but without health, there's nothing.
Make a Plan. Success begins with planning — without a plan, you're heading for failure.
Be Honest. A little more honesty brings a little more trust.
Treat Others with Courtesy. Treating others with courtesy is the most basic requirement of any relationship.
