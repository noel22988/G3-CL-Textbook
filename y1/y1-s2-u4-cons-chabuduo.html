1 有yǒu这zhè么me一yí个gè人rén，他tā姓xìng差chà，名míng不bù多duō。你nǐ一yí定dìng见jiàn过guò他tā，一yí定dìng听tīng别bié人rén谈tán起qǐ过guò他tā。
1There's a man whose surname is "Close," and whose given name is "Enough." You've definitely seen him before — you've definitely heard people talk about him.
2 差chà不bu多duō先xiān生shēng的de相xiàng貌mào和hé你nǐ和hé我wǒ都dōu差chà不bu多duō。他tā有yǒu一yī双shuāng眼yǎn睛jīng，但dàn看kàn得dé不bù太tài清qīng楚chǔ；有yǒu两liǎng只zhǐ耳ěr朵duǒ，但dàn听tīng得dé不bù太tài清qīng楚chǔ；有yǒu鼻bí子zi和hé嘴zuǐ，但dàn他tā对duì于yú气qì味wèi和hé口kǒu味wèi都dōu不bù太tài讲jiǎng究jiū。他tā的de脑nǎo子zi也yě不bù小xiǎo，但dàn他tā的de记jì性xìng却què不bù太tài好hǎo。他tā常cháng常cháng说shuō：“凡fán事shì只zhǐ要yào差chà不bu多duō就jiù好hǎo了le，何hé必bì太tài精jīng明míng呢ne？”
2Mr. Close-Enough's face looks more or less like yours and mine. He has two eyes, but doesn't see very clearly; two ears, but doesn't hear very clearly; a nose and a mouth, but he isn't very particular about smells or tastes. His brain isn't small either — but his memory isn't very good. His favourite saying: "As long as things are close enough, that's good enough. Why bother being too precise?"
3 他tā小xiǎo的de时shí候hòu，妈mā妈mā叫jiào他tā去qù买mǎi红hóng糖táng，他tā买mǎi了le白bái糖táng回huí来lái。妈mā妈mā骂mà他tā，他tā摇yáo摇yáo头tóu说shuō：“红hóng糖táng白bái糖táng不bú是shì差chà不bu多duō吗ma？”
3When he was little, his mother sent him out to buy brown sugar, and he came back with white sugar. When she scolded him, he just shook his head and said: "Brown sugar, white sugar — aren't they close enough?"
4 后hòu来lái他tā在zài一yì家jiā店diàn里lǐ做zuò事shì，在zài记jì账zhàng时shí常cháng常cháng把bǎ“十shí”写xiě成chéng“千qiān”，把bǎ“千qiān”写xiě成chéng“十shí”，害hài得dé店diàn里lǐ亏kuī了le不bù少shǎo钱qián。老lǎo板bǎn气qì得dé骂mà他tā，他tā却què笑xiào嘻xī嘻xī地dì说shuō：“‘千qiān’字zì比bǐ‘十shí’字zì只zhǐ多duō一yī小xiǎo撇piē，不bú是shì差chà不bu多duō吗ma？”
4Later he worked at a shop, and kept writing "ten" (十) as "thousand" (千) and "thousand" as "ten" in the ledgers — causing the shop to lose a considerable sum. When the boss scolded him in a fury, he just grinned and said: "The character 'thousand' is only one little stroke more than 'ten' — aren't they close enough?"
5 有yǒu一yì天tiān，他tā为wèi了le一yī件jiàn重zhòng要yào的de事shì，要yào乘chéng船chuán到dào外wài地dì去qù。他tā慢màn条tiáo斯sī理lǐ地dì走zǒu到dào码mǎ头tóu，结jié果guǒ迟chí了le两liǎng分fēn钟zhōng，轮lún船chuán已yǐ经jīng起qǐ航háng了le。他tā没méi有yǒu办bàn法fǎ，望wàng着zhe远yuǎn去qù的de轮lún船chuán，摇yáo摇yáo头tóu道dào：“只zhǐ好hǎo明míng天tiān再zài走zǒu了le，今jīn天tiān走zǒu同tóng明míng天tiān走zǒu，也yě还hái差chà不bu多duō。可kě是shì轮lún船chuán公gōng司sī未wèi免miǎn太tài认rèn真zhēn了le。8点diǎn30分fēn开kāi船chuán，同tóng8点diǎn32分fēn开kāi船chuán，不bú是shì差chà不bu多duō吗ma？”他tā一yí面miàn说shuō，一yí面miàn慢màn慢màn地dì走zǒu回huí家jiā，心xīn里lǐ不bù明míng白bái为wèi什shén么me轮lún船chuán不bù肯kěn等děng他tā两liǎng分fēn钟zhōng。
5One day, he needed to take a boat out of town on an important matter. He strolled leisurely to the dock — and arrived two minutes late. The boat had already set sail. Helpless, he gazed at the departing steamer, shook his head, and said: "I'll just have to go tomorrow instead. Leaving today or leaving tomorrow — it's close enough. But the steamship company is just too serious about this. Sailing at 8:30 or sailing at 8:32 — aren't they close enough?" He said all this as he walked slowly home, unable to understand why the steamer wouldn't wait two minutes for him.
6 有yǒu一yì天tiān，他tā忽hū然rán得dé了le急jí病bìng，赶gǎn快kuài叫jiào家jiā人rén去qù请qǐng东dōng街jiē的de汪wāng大dà夫fū。家jiā人rén急jí急jí忙máng忙máng地dì跑pǎo去qù，一yì时shí找zhǎo不bù着zhe东dōng街jiē的de汪wāng大dà夫fū，却què把bǎ西xī街jiē的de兽shòu医yī王wáng大dà夫fū请qǐng来lái了le。差chà不bu多duō先xiān生shēng躺tǎng在zài床chuáng上shàng，知zhī道dào找zhǎo错cuò了le人rén，但dàn病bìng急jí了le，身shēn上shàng痛tòng苦kǔ，心xīn里lǐ焦jiāo急jí，等děng不bù得dé了le。他tā想xiǎng：好hǎo在zài王wáng大dà夫fū同tóng汪wāng大dà夫fū也yě差chà不bu多duō，让ràng他tā试shì试shì看kàn吧ba。于yú是shì这zhè位wèi王wáng大dà夫fū走zǒu近jìn床chuáng前qián，用yòng医yī牛niú的de方fāng法fǎ给gěi差chà不bu多duō先xiān生shēng治zhì病bìng。结jié果guǒ不bú到dào一yí个gè小xiǎo时shí，差chà不bu多duō先xiān生shēng就jiù断duàn气qì了le。
6One day, he suddenly came down with a serious illness, and hurriedly told his family to fetch Dr. Wang (汪) from East Street. The family rushed out, but couldn't find Dr. Wang right away — so they brought back Dr. Wang (王) the veterinarian from West Street instead. Lying on his sickbed, Mr. Close-Enough realised they'd got the wrong man, but his condition was critical, his body was in pain, his heart anxious — he couldn't wait. Well, he thought, vet Wang and Dr. Wang are close enough, so let's see what he can do. And so this Dr. Wang stepped up to the bed and treated Mr. Close-Enough with the methods he used on cattle. In under an hour, Mr. Close-Enough had breathed his last.
7 差chà不bu多duō先xiān生shēng临lín死sǐ前qián说shuō：“活huó人rén同tóng死sǐ人rén也yě差chà……差chà不bu多duō。凡fán事shì只zhǐ要yào差chà……差chà不bu多duō就jiù好hǎo了le，何hé……何hé必bì太tài……太tài认rèn真zhēn呢ne？”
7Just before he died, Mr. Close-Enough gasped: "Living people and dead people… are also close… close enough. As long as things are close… close enough, it's fine. Why… why be so… so serious?"
