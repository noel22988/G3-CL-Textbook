1 根gēn据jù陆lù路lù交jiāo通tōng管guǎn理lǐ局jú昨zuó天tiān在zài网wǎng站zhàn上shàng公gōng布bù的de最zuì新xīn数shù据jù，截jié至zhì今jīn年nián6月yuè，全quán国guó地dì铁tiě列liè车chē平píng均jūn每měi行xíng驶shǐ约yuē140万wàn公gōng里lǐ，只zhǐ出chū现xiàn一yī次cì超chāo过guò五wǔ分fēn钟zhōng的de延yán误wù。第dì一yī季jì度dù地dì铁tiě运yùn行xíng的de可kě靠kào度dù有yǒu所suǒ提tí升shēng。地dì铁tiě网wǎng络luò系xì统tǒng运yùn行xíng稳wěn定dìng，得dé归guī功gōng于yú一yī群qún默mò默mò付fù出chū的de幕mù后hòu英yīng雄xióng。
1According to the latest figures released by the Land Transport Authority on their website yesterday, as of June this year, MRT trains across the island travel on average around 1.4 million kilometres between delays of over five minutes. Reliability of train operations improved in the first quarter. The smooth running of the MRT network owes much to a group of quiet heroes working behind the scenes.
2 38岁suì的de工gōng程chéng维wéi修xiū经jīng理lǐ张zhāng少shǎo华huá就jiù是shì这zhè群qún幕mù后hòu英yīng雄xióng中zhōng的de一yī位wèi。他tā两liǎng年nián前qián加jiā入rù地dì铁tiě公gōng司sī时shí，地dì铁tiě服fú务wù质zhì量liàng及jí可kě靠kào度dù都dōu备bèi受shòu批pī评píng。为wèi了le改gǎi进jìn地dì铁tiě的de服fú务wù质zhì量liàng，张zhāng少shǎo华huá将jiāng多duō年nián积jī累lěi的de工gōng作zuò经jīng验yàn全quán部bù贡gòng献xiàn出chū来lái。虽suī然rán在zài工gōng作zuò中zhōng面miàn临lín许xǔ多duō挑tiǎo战zhàn，但dàn是shì他tā明míng知zhī山shān有yǒu虎hǔ，偏piān向xiàng虎hǔ山shān行xíng，不bù怕pà困kùn难nán，勇yǒng往wǎng直zhí前qián。
2Zhang Shaohua, 38, a senior maintenance engineer, is one of these behind-the-scenes heroes. When he joined the MRT company two years ago, the quality and reliability of train services were both under heavy criticism. To improve service quality, Zhang Shaohua has poured in every bit of the experience he has built up over the years. Though he has faced many challenges in his work, he knowingly walks toward the tiger's mountain — never fearing hardship, always pressing ahead.
3 晚wǎn上shàng10点diǎn30分fēn是shì大dà部bù分fèn人rén上shàng床chuáng休xiū息xī的de时shí间jiān，却què是shì张zhāng少shǎo华huá漏lòu夜yè工gōng作zuò的de开kāi始shǐ。11点diǎn，张zhāng少shǎo华huá开kāi始shǐ与yǔ其qí他tā四sì个gè车chē厂chǎng的de同tóng事shì协xié调tiáo，检jiǎn查chá并bìng确què认rèn轨guǐ道dào维wéi修xiū机jī车chē操cāo作zuò正zhèng常cháng。
3Ten-thirty at night is bedtime for most people, but it's when Zhang Shaohua's overnight shift begins. At eleven, he starts coordinating with colleagues from four other train depots, checking and confirming that the track maintenance vehicles are in working order.
4 在zài获huò得dé指zhǐ挥huī中zhōng心xīn的de指zhǐ示shì后hòu，张zhāng少shǎo华huá和hé团tuán队duì成chéng员yuán驾jià驶shǐ着zhe维wéi修xiū机jī车chē从cóng地dì铁tiě车chē厂chǎng出chū发fā到dào各gè组zǔ负fù责zé的de轨guǐ道dào地dì段duàn。凌líng晨chén1点diǎn15分fēn，各gè地dì铁tiě线xiàn的de末mò班bān车chē都dōu已yǐ收shōu车chē回huí厂chǎng。这zhè时shí，张zhāng少shǎo华huá和hé团tuán队duì成chéng员yuán再zài次cì检jiǎn查chá维wéi修xiū机jī车chē。1点diǎn30分fēn，一yī切qiè准zhǔn备bèi就jiù绪xù后hòu，维wéi修xiū团tuán队duì准zhǔn时shí开kāi工gōng。他tā们men必bì须xū在zài短duǎn短duǎn的de三sān个gè小xiǎo时shí内nèi完wán成chéng多duō项xiàng工gōng作zuò，包bāo括kuò清qīng洗xǐ隧suì道dào，检jiǎn查chá电diàn路lù，扫sǎo描miáo并bìng更gēng换huàn有yǒu裂liè痕hén的de轨guǐ道dào，以yǐ确què保bǎo列liè车chē行xíng驶shǐ安ān全quán、平píng稳wěn、顺shùn畅chàng。
4Once they have instructions from the command centre, Zhang Shaohua and his team drive the maintenance vehicles out of the depot to the sections of track each group is responsible for. By 1.15 a.m., all the last trains on every MRT line have returned to the depot. At that point, Zhang Shaohua and his team run another check on the maintenance vehicles. At 1.30 a.m., with everything ready, the maintenance team begins work right on time. In just three short hours, they have to complete many tasks — cleaning tunnels, checking electrical circuits, scanning for and replacing cracked sections of track — to ensure that trains run safely, smoothly, and without incident.
5 张zhāng少shǎo华huá说shuō：“为wèi了le让ràng隔gé天tiān的de首shǒu班bān列liè车chē能néng准zhǔn时shí投tóu入rù服fú务wù，我wǒ们men每měi天tiān都dōu在zài和hé时shí间jiān赛sài跑pǎo，全quán力lì以yǐ赴fù，不bù断duàn提tí高gāo服fú务wù水shuǐ平píng。希xī望wàng假jiǎ以yǐ时shí日rì，地dì铁tiě的de服fú务wù质zhì量liàng能néng大dà大dà改gǎi善shàn，运yùn行xíng的de可kě靠kào度dù会huì越yuè来lái越yuè高gāo。这zhè也yě是shì这zhè份fèn职zhí业yè给gěi我wǒ们men带dài来lái的de最zuì大dà乐lè趣qù和hé成chéng就jiù感gǎn。”
5Zhang Shaohua says: "To make sure the first train the next morning goes into service on time, we race against the clock every single day — giving our all, constantly raising our standards. I hope that, given time, MRT service quality will improve greatly, and reliability will keep going up. That's what makes this job most satisfying for us, and gives us the greatest sense of achievement."
6 乘chéng客kè李lǐ小xiǎo平píng经jīng常cháng搭dā乘chéng地dì铁tiě上shàng下xià班bān。以yǐ前qián每měi次cì地dì铁tiě发fā生shēng故gù障zhàng，他tā都dū会huì抱bào怨yuàn。在zài他tā看kàn来lái，地dì铁tiě正zhèng常cháng运yùn行xíng是shì理lǐ所suǒ当dāng然rán的de，并bìng不bú是shì件jiàn难nán事shì。后hòu来lái，他tā从cóng新xīn闻wén报bào道dào中zhōng了liǎo解jiě到dào，原yuán来lái为wèi了le确què保bǎo乘chéng客kè每měi日rì出chū行xíng顺shùn畅chàng无wú阻zǔ，有yǒu一yī群qún幕mù后hòu英yīng雄xióng一yì直zhí在zài坚jiān守shǒu岗gǎng位wèi，努nǔ力lì付fù出chū。作zuò为wéi乘chéng客kè，他tā觉jué得de这zhè些xiē默mò默mò无wú闻wén的de小xiǎo人rén物wù应yīng该gāi得dé到dào公gōng众zhòng的de理lǐ解jiě和hé支zhī持chí。
6Commuter Li Xiaoping takes the MRT to and from work every day. In the past, whenever there was a breakdown, he would grumble. As far as he was concerned, keeping the trains running was just the way things ought to be — nothing too difficult. Later, from news reports, he learned that a group of unsung heroes had been quietly standing at their posts and putting in the hard work, all to make sure commuters' daily journeys stayed smooth. As a commuter, he feels these unremarkable, hard-working people deserve the public's understanding and support.
