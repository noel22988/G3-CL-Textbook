《一个蛋两个蛋三个蛋》 One Egg, Two Eggs, Three Eggs

语速 Speed 0.9×
1 开kāi学xué了le，中zhōng一yī甲jiǎ班bān的de级jí任rèn老lǎo师shī是shì赵zhào老lǎo师shī，她tā非fēi常cháng特tè别bié，就jiù连lián给gěi同tóng学xué们men布bù置zhì的de作zuò业yè都dōu与yǔ众zhòng不bù同tóng。
1The school term had started. The form teacher of Secondary 1A was Teacher Zhao. She was very unusual — even the homework she gave her students was out of the ordinary.
2 鸡jī妈mā妈mā孵fū蛋dàn很hěn辛xīn苦kǔ，父fù母mǔ养yǎng育yù孩hái子zi也yě很hěn辛xīn苦kǔ。为wèi了le让ràng同tóng学xué们men体tǐ会huì父fù母mǔ的de辛xīn苦kǔ，培péi养yǎng他tā们men的de责zé任rèn心xīn，赵zhào老lǎo师shī让ràng每měi个gè同tóng学xué随suí身shēn带dài一yí个gè鸡jī蛋dàn， 坚jiān持chí三sān天tiān。在zài这zhè三sān天tiān里lǐ，必bì须xū蛋dàn不bù离lí身shēn，得dé让ràng它tā有yǒu人rén的de体tǐ温wēn，还hái不bù能néng把bǎ它tā弄nòng破pò。
2Mother hens work hard to hatch their eggs, and parents work hard to raise their children. To help the students understand how hard their parents work and to build a sense of responsibility, Teacher Zhao told each student to carry an egg with them for three days. During these three days, the egg had to stay on their person, be kept at body temperature, and must not be broken.
3 同tóng学xué们men很hěn兴xīng奋fèn，马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào兴xīng奋fèn之zhī后hòu却què有yǒu些xiē担dān心xīn:“如rú果guǒ我wǒ孵fū出chū小xiǎo鸡jī怎zěn么me办bàn？”同tóng学xué们men听tīng了le都dōu笑xiào起qǐ来lái，但dàn马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào没méi有yǒu笑xiào，他tā是shì真zhēn的de担dān心xīn。赵zhào老lǎo师shī见jiàn他tā不bú是shì在zài捣dǎo乱luàn，便biàn说shuō三sān天tiān是shì孵fū不bù出chū小xiǎo鸡jī的de，还hái说shuō只zhǐ要yào鸡jī蛋dàn三sān天tiān不bù破pò，就jiù会huì奖jiǎng励lì他tā。马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào很hěn少shǎo得dé到dào奖jiǎng励lì，老lǎo师shī的de话huà瞬shùn间jiān让ràng他tā变biàn得dé兴xìng致zhì勃bó勃bó。
3The students were excited. Ma Xiaotiao, after his initial excitement, became a little worried: "What if I actually hatch a chick?" The other students burst out laughing, but Ma Xiaotiao didn't laugh — he was genuinely worried. Teacher Zhao, seeing that he wasn't fooling around, told him three days wasn't enough for an egg to hatch, and said that if his egg survived three days intact, she would reward him. Ma Xiaotiao rarely received rewards, so the teacher's words instantly made him enthusiastic.
4 第dì一yī天tiān，马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào把bǎ鸡jī蛋dàn放fàng在zài衬chèn衫shān里lǐ面miàn，贴tiē着zhe肚dù皮pí，好hǎo像xiàng母mǔ鸡jī孵fū小xiǎo鸡jī一yī样yàng，还hái时shí不bù时shí用yòng手shǒu摸mō一yī摸mō。没méi想xiǎng到dào，他tā去qù上shàng厕cè所suǒ时shí，在zài走zǒu廊láng上shàng跟gēn别bié人rén撞zhuàng在zài一yì起qǐ，鸡jī蛋dàn破pò了le。他tā的de身shēn上shàng粘zhān着zhe蛋dàn液yè，十shí分fēn狼láng狈bèi。同tóng学xué讥jī讽fěng他tā没méi有yǒu完wán成chéng任rèn务wù，他tā很hěn恼nǎo火huǒ，赌dǔ气qì地dì嚷rǎng道dào：“有yǒu什shén么me了liǎo不bù起qǐ？我wǒ明míng天tiān重chóng新xīn带dài一yí个gè。”
4On the first day, Ma Xiaotiao tucked the egg inside his shirt against his belly, just like a mother hen hatching her chick, and kept touching it every so often. Unexpectedly, when he went to the toilet, he bumped into someone in the corridor — and the egg broke. Egg yolk stuck to his clothes; he was a mess. When his classmates teased him for failing the task, he grew furious and shouted sulkily, "What's the big deal? I'll bring another one tomorrow!"
5 第dì二èr天tiān，他tā小xiǎo心xīn翼yì翼yì地dì用yòng纸zhǐ巾jīn把bǎ蛋dàn层céng层céng包bāo好hǎo，放fàng进jìn裤kù袋dài，这zhè样yàng果guǒ然rán安ān全quán多duō了le。可kě是shì，在zài放fàng学xué离lí开kāi教jiào室shì前qián，他tā忽hū然rán发fā现xiàn，鸡jī蛋dàn竟jìng然rán不bú见jiàn了le。同tóng学xué说shuō他tā没méi有yǒu责zé任rèn心xīn，马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào很hěn不bù服fú气qì，心xīn想xiǎng：谁shuí说shuō我wǒ没méi有yǒu责zé任rèn心xīn？我wǒ一yí定dìng要yào证zhèng明míng给gěi你nǐ们men看kàn！
5On the second day, he carefully wrapped the egg in layer upon layer of tissue and put it in his trouser pocket — this was indeed much safer. But just before leaving the classroom at the end of the day, he suddenly realised the egg was gone. When his classmates said he had no sense of responsibility, Ma Xiaotiao refused to accept it, thinking to himself: Who says I have no sense of responsibility? I'll prove it to you!
6 第dì三sān天tiān，马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào还hái是shì把bǎ鸡jī蛋dàn放fàng在zài裤kù袋dài里lǐ，但dàn是shì，用yòng别bié针zhēn把bǎ裤kù袋dài口kǒu别bié了le起qǐ来lái。他tā想xiǎng：这zhè下xià子zi鸡jī蛋dàn想xiǎng跑pǎo也yě跑pǎo不bù掉diào了le。上shàng课kè时shí，他tā不bù时shí摸mō摸mō裤kù袋dài，确què保bǎo鸡jī蛋dàn还hái在zài。放fàng学xué前qián，赵zhào老lǎo师shī看kàn到dào马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào的de鸡jī蛋dàn居jū然rán完wán好hǎo无wú损sǔn，马mǎ上shàng表biǎo扬yáng了le他tā，还hái让ràng他tā把bǎ这zhè个ge蛋dàn带dài回huí去qù送sòng给gěi爸bà爸bà妈mā妈mā。
6On the third day, Ma Xiaotiao put the egg in his trouser pocket again, but this time he used a safety pin to pin the pocket shut. He thought: Now the egg can't escape even if it tries. During class, he kept touching his pocket to make sure the egg was still there. Before school ended, Teacher Zhao saw that Ma Xiaotiao's egg was completely intact, praised him on the spot, and told him to take the egg home and give it to his parents.
7 马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào开kāi心xīn得dé像xiàng只zhǐ快kuài乐lè的de鸟niǎo儿ér，蹦bèng着zhe跳tiào着zhe，恨hèn不bù得dé马mǎ上shàng就jiù回huí到dào家jiā。回huí家jiā的de路lù上shàng，他tā忽hū然rán想xiǎng到dào：得dé在zài蛋dàn上shàng写xiě几jǐ个gè字zì，再zài送sòng给gěi爸bà爸bà妈mā妈mā。
7Ma Xiaotiao was as happy as a bird, skipping and jumping — he couldn't wait to get home. On the way home, he suddenly had an idea: he should write a few words on the egg before giving it to his parents.
8 马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào走zǒu进jìn一yī家jiā超chāo市shì去qù买mǎi荧yíng光guāng笔bǐ。他tā买mǎi了le笔bǐ正zhèng要yào走zǒu出chū去qù，却què被bèi店diàn员yuán拦lán住zhù了le。因yīn为wèi他tā的de手shǒu一yì直zhí捂wǔ着zhuó装zhuāng鸡jī蛋dàn的de裤kù袋dài，引yǐn起qǐ了le店diàn员yuán的de怀huái疑yí。店diàn员yuán问wèn他tā裤kù袋dài里lǐ装zhuāng的de是shì什shén么me，马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào没méi说shuō。店diàn员yuán上shàng前qián查chá看kàn，马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào拼pīn命mìng抵dǐ挡dǎng，拉lā扯chě之zhī间jiān，鸡jī蛋dàn破pò了le! 马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào“哇wa”的de一yī声shēng哭kū了le起qǐ来lái。店diàn员yuán有yǒu些xiē手shǒu足zú无wú措cuò，连lián忙máng安ān慰wèi他tā说shuō：“别bié哭kū别bié哭kū，不bù就jiù是shì一yí个gè鸡jī蛋dàn吗ma？赔péi你nǐ一yí个gè好hǎo不bù好hǎo？”
8Ma Xiaotiao walked into a supermarket to buy a highlighter. He had bought the pen and was about to leave when a shop assistant stopped him. Because he had been covering the pocket with the egg the whole time, he had made the assistant suspicious. The assistant asked what was in his pocket; Ma Xiaotiao wouldn't say. The assistant stepped forward to check, and Ma Xiaotiao frantically resisted — in the struggle, the egg broke! Ma Xiaotiao burst out crying with a loud "waaah". The assistant didn't know what to do and hurriedly tried to comfort him: "Don't cry, don't cry — it's just one egg. I'll pay you back for one, okay?"
9 “赔péi我wǒ一yì百bǎi个gè我wǒ都dōu不bú要yào，我wǒ就jiù要yào这zhè一yí个gè！”马mǎ小xiǎo跳tiào哭kū得dé更gèng伤shāng心xīn了le，“这zhè是shì我wǒ保bǎo护hù了le一yī天tiān的de鸡jī蛋dàn，呜wū呜wū呜wū......”
9"I wouldn't take a hundred eggs in return — I only want this one!" Ma Xiaotiao cried even more sorrowfully. "This is the egg I protected for a whole day, boo-hoo-hoo…"
