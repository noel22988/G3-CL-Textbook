1 新xīn加jiā坡pō国guó家jiā美měi术shù馆guǎn位wèi于yú市shì政zhèng区qū，占zhàn地dì6万wàn4000平píng方fāng米mǐ，大dà约yuē有yǒu9个gè足zú球qiú场chǎng那nà么me大dà。它tā是shì东dōng南nán亚yà地dì区qū面miàn积jī最zuì大dà的de艺yì术shù博bó物wù馆guǎn之zhī一yī，收shōu藏cáng了le许xǔ多duō举jǔ世shì无wú双shuāng的de东dōng南nán亚yà艺yì术shù珍zhēn品pǐn。
1The National Gallery Singapore is located in the Civic District. Covering an area of 64,000 square metres — roughly the size of nine football fields — it is one of the largest art museums in Southeast Asia and holds many unparalleled Southeast Asian art treasures.
2 你nǐ知zhī道dào吗ma？新xīn加jiā坡pō国guó家jiā美měi术shù馆guǎn藏cáng着zhe许xǔ多duō有yǒu趣qù的de“秘mì密mì”，让ràng我wǒ们men一yì起qǐ去qù探tàn索suǒ一yī下xià吧ba。
2Did you know? The National Gallery Singapore is hiding plenty of interesting "secrets." Let's go and uncover some of them together.
3 要yào探tàn索suǒ国guó家jiā美měi术shù馆guǎn的de“秘mì密mì”，首shǒu先xiān要yào从cóng它tā的de建jiàn筑zhù开kāi始shǐ。实shí际jì上shàng，国guó家jiā美měi术shù馆guǎn是shì由yóu前qián最zuì高gāo法fǎ院yuàn和hé政zhèng府fǔ大dà厦shà合hé二èr为wéi一yī，改gǎi建jiàn而ér成chéng的de。这zhè两liǎng座zuò建jiàn筑zhù有yǒu重zhòng要yào的de历lì史shǐ意yì义yì。前qián最zuì高gāo法fǎ院yuàn在zài60多duō年nián里lǐ一yì直zhí是shì新xīn加jiā坡pō司sī法fǎ制zhì度dù的de最zuì高gāo殿diàn堂táng；政zhèng府fǔ大dà厦shà则zé见jiàn证zhèng了le多duō个gè历lì史shǐ事shì件jiàn，例lì如rú，日rì本běn投tóu降xiáng和hé新xīn加jiā坡pō宣xuān布bù自zì治zhì等děng。
3To explore the Gallery's secrets, we start with its buildings. The National Gallery Singapore was actually created by joining and converting two buildings: the former Supreme Court and the former City Hall. Both are historically significant. The former Supreme Court served as the highest court in Singapore's judicial system for over 60 years. City Hall witnessed many historic events — including the Japanese surrender and Singapore's declaration of self-governance.
4 今jīn天tiān，国guó家jiā美měi术shù馆guǎn还hái保bǎo留liú着zhe这zhè两liǎng座zuò建jiàn筑zhù的de部bù分fèn原yuán貌mào，例lì如rú，法fǎ庭tíng内nèi法fǎ官guān的de座zuò位wèi、被bèi告gào栏lán、棕zōng色sè天tiān花huā板bǎn等děng。这zhè里lǐ有yǒu个gè鲜xiǎn为wéi人rén知zhī的de“秘mì密mì”——地dì下xià通tōng道dào。那nà时shí，犯fàn人rén不bú是shì从cóng大dà门mén进jìn来lái，而ér是shì从cóng这zhè个ge特tè别bié通tōng道dào直zhí接jiē进jìn入rù法fǎ庭tíng被bèi告gào栏lán的de。
4Today, the Gallery has preserved many original features of the two buildings — such as the judge's bench in the old courtroom, the dock, and the brown-coloured ceilings. There's also a little-known "secret" here: an underground tunnel. Back in those days, prisoners didn't come in through the front entrance — they were brought in through this special passage straight into the dock.
5 国guó家jiā美měi术shù馆guǎn里lǐ的de门mén也yě藏cáng着zhe“秘mì密mì”。馆guǎn内nèi悬xuán空kōng桥qiáo两liǎng端duān古gǔ色sè古gǔ香xiāng的de大dà门mén原yuán本běn是shì窗chuāng户hù，经jīng过guò设shè计jì师shī的de巧qiǎo思sī妙miào想xiǎng，变biàn成chéng了le我wǒ们men现xiàn在zài看kàn到dào的de门mén，多duō有yǒu意yì思si！
5The Gallery's doors also hide a secret. The old-fashioned doors at either end of the suspended walkway inside the Gallery were originally windows — cleverly reimagined by the designers to become the doors we see today. How fascinating!
6 来lái到dào五wǔ楼lóu，你nǐ甚shèn至zhì可kě以yǐ近jìn距jù离lí观guān赏shǎng前qián最zuì高gāo法fǎ院yuàn的de圆yuán顶dǐng。这zhè里lǐ也yě有yǒu个gè小xiǎo“秘mì密mì”，这zhè圆yuán顶dǐng原yuán本běn不bú是shì铜tóng绿lǜ色sè，是shì经jīng过guò长cháng年nián的de氧yǎng化huà才cái变biàn成chéng现xiàn在zài这zhè样yàng的de。
6Up on the fifth floor, you can even view the former Supreme Court's dome up close. There's another small "secret" here: the dome wasn't always this greenish colour. It turned that shade over long years of oxidation.
7 我wǒ们men要yào探tàn索suǒ的de“秘mì密mì”还hái藏cáng在zài国guó家jiā美měi术shù馆guǎn的de画huà作zuò中zhōng。国guó家jiā美měi术shù馆guǎn里lǐ馆guǎn藏cáng丰fēng富fù，共gòng有yǒu 9000多duō件jiàn收shōu藏cáng品pǐn，其qí中zhōng一yī幅fú画huà被bèi称chēng作zuò“钱qián包bāo里lǐ的de名míng作zuò”。对duì大dà多duō数shù人rén来lái说shuō，它tā既jì熟shú悉xī又yòu陌mò生shēng。熟shú悉xī，是shì因yīn为wèi它tā就jiù印yìn在zài50新xīn元yuán钞chāo票piào的de背bèi面miàn，每měi个gè人rén都dōu见jiàn过guò；陌mò生shēng，是shì因yīn为wèi大dà多duō数shù人rén对duì这zhè幅fú画huà并bìng不bù了liǎo解jiě。这zhè幅fú名míng为wèi《晒shài咸xián鱼yú》的de画huà，展zhǎn现xiàn了le马mǎ来lái同tóng胞bāo晒shài咸xián鱼yú的de场chǎng景jǐng，画huà面miàn宁níng静jìng而ér平píng和hé。在zài画huà家jiā钟zhōng泗sì滨bīn的de笔bǐ下xià，过guò去qù东dōng南nán亚yà沿yán海hǎi村cūn落luò中zhōng常cháng见jiàn的de生shēng活huó画huà面miàn被bèi生shēng动dòng地dì表biǎo现xiàn了le出chū来lái。
7More secrets are hidden in the Gallery's artworks. With over 9,000 pieces in the collection, one painting has been called "the masterpiece in your wallet." To most people, it's both familiar and strange — familiar because it's printed on the back of the S$50 note, which everyone has seen; strange because most people don't really know much about it. The painting, titled Drying Salted Fish, depicts a Malay community drying salted fish in a calm, peaceful scene. Through the brush of painter Cheong Soo Pieng, the everyday life of Southeast Asia's coastal villages of the past is brought vividly to life.
8 在zài这zhè个ge历lì史shǐ与yǔ艺yì术shù交jiāo融róng的de美měi术shù馆guǎn里lǐ，还hái有yǒu很hěn多duō“秘mì密mì”。亲qīn自zì去qù走zǒu一yī走zǒu，看kàn一yī看kàn吧ba，说shuō不bù定dìng你nǐ也yě会huì有yǒu新xīn发fā现xiàn，找zhǎo到dào属shǔ于yú自zì己jǐ的de秘mì密mì。
8There are many more secrets waiting in this Gallery where history and art come together. Go and explore it for yourself — you might just make a new discovery, and find a secret of your own.
