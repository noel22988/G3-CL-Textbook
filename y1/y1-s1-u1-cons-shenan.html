《张老师“审案”》 Teacher Zhang's "Courtroom"

语速 Speed 0.9× (set before play)
（课kè间jiān休xiū息xī后hòu，同tóng学xué们men回huí到dào教jiào室shì。欧ōu阳yáng甜tián甜tián和hé几jǐ个gè同tóng学xué围wéi在zài陈chén小xiǎo双shuāng身shēn边biān，陈chén小xiǎo双shuāng在zài小xiǎo声shēng地dì哭kū，林lín小xiǎo飞fēi坐zuò在zài一yī旁páng，板bǎn着zhe脸liǎn不bù出chū声shēng。这zhè时shí，张zhāng老lǎo师shī走zǒu了le进jìn来lái。）
(After recess, the students return to the classroom. Ouyang Tiantian and several classmates gather around Chen Xiaoshuang, who is sobbing quietly. Lin Xiaofei sits to one side with a stony face, saying nothing. At this moment, Teacher Zhang walks in.)
张zhāng老lǎo师shī 发fā生shēng了le什shén么me事shì？
Teacher Zhang: What happened?
欧ōu阳yáng甜tián甜tián （瞪dèng了le林lín小xiǎo飞fēi一yī眼yǎn，气qì呼hū呼hū地dì）刚gāng才cái小xiǎo双shuāng回huí来lái时shí发fā现xiàn林lín小xiǎo飞fēi的de笔bǐ盒hé在zài地dì上shàng，就jiù帮bāng忙máng捡jiǎn起qǐ来lái。林lín小xiǎo飞fēi进jìn来lái刚gāng好hǎo看kàn见jiàn，却què说shuō是shì小xiǎo双shuāng弄nòng掉diào了le他tā的de笔bǐ盒hé，还hái把bǎ小xiǎo双shuāng的de笔bǐ盒hé摔shuāi在zài地dì上shàng。
Ouyang Tiantian: (Glaring at Lin Xiaofei, angrily) Just now, when Xiaoshuang came back, she saw Lin Xiaofei's pencil case on the floor and picked it up to help. Lin Xiaofei walked in just at that moment and accused Xiaoshuang of knocking his pencil case off the desk — then he threw her pencil case on the floor!
林lín小xiǎo飞fēi （站zhàn起qǐ来lái）她tā一yí定dìng是shì先xiān弄nòng掉diào了le我wǒ的de笔bǐ盒hé再zài捡jiǎn起qǐ来lái的de！
Lin Xiaofei: (Standing up) She must have knocked it off first and then picked it up!
（同tóng学xué们men听tīng了le，纷fēn纷fēn表biǎo示shì不bù满mǎn。）
(Hearing this, the students murmur in disagreement.)
张zhāng老lǎo师shī （想xiǎng了le想xiǎng）既jì然rán有yǒu争zhēng议yì，我wǒ们men不bù如rú通tōng过guò审shěn案àn的de方fāng式shì来lái查chá出chū真zhēn相xiàng。我wǒ做zuò法fǎ官guān。小xiǎo飞fēi摔shuāi了le小xiǎo双shuāng的de笔bǐ盒hé，是shì被bèi告gào，小xiǎo双shuāng是shì原yuán告gào。谁shuí来lái做zuò小xiǎo双shuāng的de律lǜ师shī？
Teacher Zhang: (After a moment's thought) Since there's a dispute, let's find the truth by holding a mock trial. I'll be the judge. Xiaofei threw Xiaoshuang's pencil case, so he's the defendant; Xiaoshuang is the plaintiff. Who will be Xiaoshuang's lawyer?
欧ōu阳yáng甜tián甜tián （第dì一yí个gè举jǔ起qǐ手shǒu）我wǒ来lái！
Ouyang Tiantian: (Raising her hand first) I will!
同tóng学xué甲jiǎ、乙yǐ （争zhēng先xiān恐kǒng后hòu）让ràng我wǒ来lái！让ràng我wǒ来lái！
Classmates A and B: (Eagerly, all at once) Me! Let me do it!
张zhāng老lǎo师shī 就jiù由yóu甜tián甜tián来lái做zuò小xiǎo双shuāng的de律lǜ师shī吧ba。那nà谁shuí来lái做zuò小xiǎo飞fēi的de律lǜ师shī？
Teacher Zhang: Tiantian will be Xiaoshuang's lawyer then. Now, who will be Xiaofei's lawyer?
林lín小xiǎo飞fēi （向xiàng四sì周zhōu看kàn了le看kàn，见jiàn没méi有yǒu人rén举jǔ手shǒu，赌dǔ气qì地dì）我wǒ自zì己jǐ辩biàn护hù，不bù需xū要yào律lǜ师shī！
Lin Xiaofei: (Looking around and seeing no hands raised, sulkily) I'll defend myself. I don't need a lawyer!
张zhāng老lǎo师shī 好hǎo，现xiàn在zài开kāi庭tíng。请qǐng原yuán告gào说shuō一yī说shuō事shì情qíng的de经jīng过guò。
Teacher Zhang: Alright, court is now in session. Plaintiff, please describe what happened.
（陈chén小xiǎo双shuāng说shuō完wán事shì情qíng的de经jīng过guò，张zhāng老lǎo师shī接jiē着zhe让ràng林lín小xiǎo飞fēi说shuō说shuō他tā为wèi什shén么me摔shuāi陈chén小xiǎo双shuāng的de笔bǐ盒hé。）
(After Chen Xiaoshuang explains what happened, Teacher Zhang asks Lin Xiaofei to explain why he threw her pencil case.)
林lín小xiǎo飞fēi （不bù服fú气qì地dì）我wǒ上shàng厕cè所suǒ前qián，明míng明míng把bǎ笔bǐ盒hé好hǎo好hǎo地dì放fàng在zài桌zhuō上shàng。我wǒ一yī回huí来lái，就jiù看kàn见jiàn她tā从cóng地dì上shàng捡jiǎn起qǐ我wǒ的de笔bǐ盒hé。肯kěn定dìng是shì她tā弄nòng掉diào了le我wǒ的de笔bǐ盒hé！
Lin Xiaofei: (Indignantly) Before I went to the toilet, I clearly put my pencil case on the desk. When I came back, I saw her picking it up from the floor. She must have knocked it off!
欧ōu阳yáng甜tián甜tián 林lín小xiǎo飞fēi同tóng学xué，请qǐng问wèn你nǐ看kàn见jiàn我wǒ当dāng事shì人rén弄nòng掉diào你nǐ的de笔bǐ盒hé了le吗ma？
Ouyang Tiantian: Classmate Lin Xiaofei, did you actually see my client knock your pencil case off the desk?
林lín小xiǎo飞fēi （语yǔ气qì弱ruò了le下xià来lái）没méi看kàn见jiàn，但dàn一yí定dìng是shì她tā！
Lin Xiaofei: (Hesitantly) I didn't see it, but it must have been her!
欧ōu阳yáng甜tián甜tián 法fǎ官guān大dà人rén，被bèi告gào既jì然rán没méi看kàn见jiàn，就jiù不bù能néng认rèn定dìng是shì我wǒ当dāng事shì人rén弄nòng掉diào他tā的de笔bǐ盒hé。请qǐng问wèn在zài场chǎng的de各gè位wèi同tóng学xué，有yǒu谁shuí看kàn见jiàn陈chén小xiǎo双shuāng弄nòng掉diào林lín小xiǎo飞fēi的de笔bǐ盒hé？如rú果guǒ没méi有yǒu，就jiù请qǐng举jǔ手shǒu。
Ouyang Tiantian: Your Honour, since the defendant didn't see it happen, he can't prove my client knocked his pencil case off. I ask all the classmates present: did anyone see Chen Xiaoshuang knock Lin Xiaofei's pencil case off the desk? If not, please raise your hands.
（在zài场chǎng的de同tóng学xué都dōu举jǔ起qǐ了le手shǒu。）
(Every classmate in the room raises their hand.)
张zhāng老lǎo师shī 现xiàn在zài本běn庭tíng宣xuān判pàn：被bèi告gào林lín小xiǎo飞fēi未wèi经jīng证zhèng实shí就jiù指zhǐ控kòng他tā人rén，并bìng且qiě摔shuāi了le原yuán告gào陈chén小xiǎo双shuāng的de笔bǐ盒hé。他tā必bì须xū向xiàng陈chén小xiǎo双shuāng道dào歉qiàn，并bìng赔péi偿cháng损sǔn失shī。
Teacher Zhang: The court now delivers its verdict: the defendant, Lin Xiaofei, accused another person without evidence and threw the plaintiff's pencil case. He must apologise to Chen Xiaoshuang and compensate her for the damage.
林lín小xiǎo飞fēi （低dī下xià头tou）对duì不bù起qǐ，是shì我wǒ错cuò了le。笔bǐ盒hé摔shuāi坏huài了le，我wǒ会huì赔péi给gěi你nǐ。
Lin Xiaofei: (Lowering his head) I'm sorry — I was in the wrong. If your pencil case is damaged, I'll pay for it.
陈chén小xiǎo双shuāng （轻qīng声shēng地dì）笔bǐ盒hé没méi摔shuāi坏huài，没méi关guān系xì。
Chen Xiaoshuang: (Quietly) The pencil case isn't broken, it's alright.
张zhāng老lǎo师shī 小xiǎo飞fēi，你nǐ要yào记jì住zhù：没méi有yǒu证zhèng据jù，就jiù不bù能néng随suí便biàn指zhǐ责zé别bié人rén。小xiǎo双shuāng，我wǒ们men要yào学xué会huì勇yǒng敢gǎn地dì为wèi自zì己jǐ说shuō话huà，不bú要yào受shòu了le委wěi屈qū也yě不bù吭kēng声shēng。同tóng学xué们men之zhī间jiān要yào互hù相xiāng信xìn任rèn、互hù相xiāng包bāo容róng，这zhè样yàng才cái能néng友yǒu好hǎo相xiāng处chǔ。
Teacher Zhang: Xiaofei, remember: without evidence, you shouldn't accuse others. Xiaoshuang, we need to learn to speak up for ourselves and not stay silent when we've been wronged. Classmates should trust and be tolerant of one another — that's how we get along well.
