1 今jīn天tiān是shì我wǒ们men搭dā乘chéng时shí光guāng机jī来lái到dào未wèi来lái市shì的de第dì三sān天tiān。按àn照zhào行xíng程chéng，我wǒ们men要yào搭dā乘chéng五wǔ用yòng车chē去qù游yóu览lǎn海hǎi底dǐ牧mù场chǎng。这zhè种zhǒng五wǔ用yòng车chē除chú了le可kě以yǐ在zài天tiān上shàng飞fēi、路lù上shàng跑pǎo，还hái能néng爬pá山shān、航háng海hǎi和hé潜qián水shuǐ。
1Today is our third day in Future City, where we've arrived by time machine. According to the itinerary, we're taking a Five-in-One Car to visit the Undersea Ranch. These cars can not only fly through the air and drive on roads, but also climb mountains, sail the seas, and dive underwater.
2 五wǔ用yòng车chē腾téng空kōng而ér起qǐ，飞fēi向xiàng蓝lán蓝lán的de海hǎi洋yáng。从cóng空kōng中zhōng往wǎng下xià看kàn，金jīn色sè的de阳yáng光guāng洒sǎ满mǎn海hǎi面miàn。海hǎi面miàn上shàng风fēng平píng浪làng静jìng，像xiàng一yī块kuài无wú边biān无wú际jì的de玻bō璃lí。
2The Five-in-One Car lifted into the air and flew towards the deep blue ocean. From above, golden sunlight spilled across the water. The sea was perfectly calm, like a boundless pane of glass.
3 五wǔ用yòng车chē降jiàng落luò在zài海hǎi面miàn上shàng，激jī起qǐ一yí阵zhèn雪xuě白bái的de浪làng花huā。车chē的de翅chì膀bǎng自zì动dòng收shōu起qǐ来lái，变biàn成chéng一yì艘sōu船chuán，在zài海hǎi面miàn上shàng航háng行xíng。
3The car landed on the water, kicking up a spray of snow-white foam. Its wings folded away automatically, and it turned into a boat cruising along the surface.
4 我wǒ和hé朋péng友yǒu正zhèng在zài说shuō笑xiào时shí，五wǔ用yòng车chē屁pì股gǔ一yī翘qiào，钻zuān进jìn海hǎi里lǐ，成chéng了le潜qián水shuǐ艇tǐng。五wǔ用yòng车chē的de外wài壳ké是shì透tòu明míng的de，海hǎi水shuǐ也yě是shì透tòu明míng的de。鱼yú儿ér从cóng容róng地dì从cóng我wǒ跟gēn前qián游yóu过guò，虾xiā儿ér悄qiāo悄qiāo地dì从cóng我wǒ身shēn边biān溜liū走zǒu，我wǒ仿fǎng佛fú来lái到dào了le水shuǐ晶jīng宫gōng。
4Just as my friend and I were laughing and chatting, the car's rear tilted up and plunged into the sea — now a submarine. The shell of the car was transparent, and the water was clear; fish drifted calmly past in front of me, and shrimps slipped quietly away at my side. It felt as though I'd arrived at the Crystal Palace under the sea.
5 五wǔ用yòng车chē慢màn慢màn下xià降jiàng，停tíng在zài海hǎi底dǐ草cǎo原yuán。这zhè儿ér是shì浅qiǎn海hǎi，阳yáng光guāng透tòu过guò海hǎi水shuǐ，淡dàn淡dàn地dì照zhào耀yào着zhe海hǎi底dǐ。这zhè里lǐ长zhǎng满mǎn了le海hǎi草cǎo、海hǎi带dài，我wǒ好hǎo像xiàng来lái到dào大dà草cǎo原yuán。草cǎo原yuán上shàng怎zěn么me没méi有yǒu牛niú羊yáng？我wǒ正zhèng疑yí惑huò着zhe，忽hū然rán看kàn到dào远yuǎn处chù有yǒu些xiē黑hēi影yǐng。靠kào近jìn一yì些xiē，我wǒ才cái发fā现xiàn原yuán来lái是shì一yí个gè机jī器qì人rén正zhèng在zài放fàng牧mù牛niú羊yáng。那nà些xiē牛niú羊yáng看kàn到dào五wǔ用yòng车chē不bù但dàn不bù怕pà，反fǎn而ér都dōu跑pǎo了le过guò来lái，确què切qiè地dì讲jiǎng，是shì游yóu了le过guò来lái。它tā们men挤jǐ在zài车chē子zi周zhōu围wéi，好hào奇qí地dì打dǎ量liàng着zhe我wǒ们men。有yǒu的de甚shèn至zhì跑pǎo到dào五wǔ用yòng车chē上shàng面miàn，“站zhàn”在zài我wǒ们men的de头tóu顶dǐng上shàng。
5The car descended slowly and came to rest on an underwater meadow. This was shallow sea, and sunlight filtered through the water, casting a soft glow on the seabed. Sea grass and kelp grew thickly all around — it felt just like standing on a great prairie. But why weren't there any cattle or sheep on this prairie? As I was wondering, I suddenly noticed some dark shapes in the distance. Getting closer, I realised it was a robot herding cows and sheep. When those animals saw the car, instead of being frightened, they all ran over — or, to be exact, swam over. They crowded around the car, examining us curiously. Some even climbed on top and "stood" right above our heads.
6 我wǒ仔zǐ细xì一yī看kàn，好hǎo一yī副fù古gǔ怪guài的de长zhǎng相xiàng：那nà羊yáng，虽suī然rán浑hún身shēn长cháng着zhe白bái色sè的de羊yáng毛máo，却què没méi有yǒu脚jiǎo，还hái长zhǎng着zhe鱼yú鳍qí和hé鱼yú尾wěi巴bā。那nà牛niú就jiù更gèng怪guài了le，长zhǎng着zhe牛niú头tóu，却què浑hún身shēn披pī着zhe银yín闪shǎn闪shǎn的de鱼yú鳞lín。它tā们men也yě没méi有yǒu脚jiǎo，同tóng样yàng长zhǎng着zhe鱼yú尾wěi巴bā。朋péng友yǒu告gào诉sù我wǒ，我wǒ才cái知zhī道dào，这zhè些xiē动dòng物wù就jiù是shì“羊yáng鱼yú”和hé“牛niú鱼yú”。科kē学xué家jiā在zài鱼yú的de细xì胞bāo中zhōng加jiā进jìn羊yáng或huò牛niú的de基jī因yīn，这zhè么me一yī来lái，它tā们men能néng够gòu像xiàng鱼yú那nà样yàng用yòng鳃sāi呼hū吸xī，在zài海hǎi里lǐ生shēng活huó，还hái能néng为wèi我wǒ们men提tí供gōng羊yáng肉ròu、牛niú肉ròu、羊yáng毛máo、牛niú奶nǎi呢ne！
6Looking more closely, I saw how strange they were: the sheep, though covered in white wool, had no legs, and instead had fins and a fish tail. The cattle were even odder — cow heads, but bodies clad in shiny silver scales. They had no legs either, just fish tails like the sheep. My friend explained that these were "Sheep-Fish" and "Cow-Fish." Scientists had inserted sheep and cow genes into fish cells, so the animals could breathe through gills and live in the sea — yet still provide mutton, beef, wool, and milk!
7 我wǒ想xiǎng看kàn得dé更gèng清qīng楚chǔ些xiē，就jiù把bǎ所suǒ有yǒu的de灯dēng都dōu打dǎ开kāi。没méi想xiǎng到dào，明míng亮liàng的de灯dēng光guāng把bǎ它tā们men吓xià跑pǎo了le。它tā们men一yī哄hòng而ér散sàn，都dōu不bú见jiàn了le！机jī器qì人rén快kuài速sù地dì来lái到dào车chē前qián。它tā跟gēn我wǒ们men比bǐ着zhuó手shǒu势shì，车chē中zhōng传chuán来lái它tā的de声shēng音yīn：“请qǐng立lì即jí关guān灯dēng。未wèi来lái市shì市shì政zhèng府fǔ颁bān布bù了le法fǎ令lìng，不bù得dé扰rǎo乱luàn海hǎi底dǐ牧mù场chǎng。谢xiè谢xiè！”我wǒ连lián忙máng把bǎ灯dēng关guān上shàng。
7Wanting a better look, I turned on every light in the car. To my surprise, the bright lights startled the animals away — they scattered in every direction and vanished. The robot quickly moved in front of the car. It signalled with its hands, and a voice came through the speaker: "Please turn off the lights immediately. The Future City government has issued a regulation against disturbing the Undersea Ranch. Thank you!" I hurried to switch them off.
8 我wǒ们men在zài海hǎi底dǐ牧mù场chǎng又yòu逛guàng了le几jǐ个gè小xiǎo时shí。海hǎi底dǐ牧mù场chǎng景jǐng色sè优yōu美měi，让ràng我wǒ们men流liú连lián忘wàng返fǎn。
8We spent several more hours exploring the Undersea Ranch. The scenery was beautiful — we were reluctant to leave.
9 当dāng五wǔ用yòng车chē钻zuān出chū海hǎi面miàn时shí，海hǎi上shàng正zhèng好hǎo起qǐ风fēng，大dà风fēng搅jiǎo起qǐ巨jù浪làng，蓝lán色sè的de海hǎi面miàn上shàng泛fàn起qǐ了le白bái花huā花huā的de泡pào沫mò。真zhēn难nán以yǐ想xiǎng象xiàng海hǎi底dǐ却què是shì那nà样yàng一yí个gè宁níng静jìng的de世shì界jiè！
9When the car broke through the surface again, a wind had picked up, whipping the water into huge waves, with white foam frothing across the blue sea. Hard to believe that down below was such a peaceful world.
